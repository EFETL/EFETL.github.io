一、單字填空（15 題，每題 2 分，共 30 分）

新北高中 113上期中1#1
Even hours after the coffee was made, the smell of the freshly brewed coffee ________ throughout the office.
答案：D persist 持續存在　與 L1〈If—〉核心字 persevere（堅持）同字根概念。咖啡香久久不散。
小港高中 113上期中2#2
Her ________ reaction was to refuse, but then she reconsidered.
答案：C initial 最初的　L2 課文 the initial study（最初那篇研究）。but then 提示前後對比。
基隆高中 112上期中2#3
When the scientist dropped the gas cylinder, gas ________ed out and began to fill the room.
答案：C stream 湧出、流洩　L3 地理／流動語意場。❌ penetrate 穿透／exceed 超過。
基隆高中 113上期中1#4
Artwork created by truly great artists such as Picasso and Monet will no doubt ________ the test of time.
答案：B stand the test of time 禁得起時間考驗　固定片語，呼應 L1〈If—〉「歷久彌新」的主題。
新店高中 113上期中1#5
The protesters ________ their leaflets in tourist areas to reach a large audience.
答案：B distribute 散發、傳播　L2 資訊傳播主題字。
基隆高中 112上期中2#6
The man lives in a ________ room with only a bed and a closet.
答案：B stark 空蕩簡陋的　L3 對比景觀字彙。❌ lush 蒼翠的／majestic 雄偉的。
明倫高中 113上期中1#7
The prospect of increased prices has already ________ an outcry.
答案：C provoke 引發　L2 衛生紙漲價恐慌的情境字；provoke an outcry 引發強烈抗議。
基隆高中 113上期中2#8
As we looked out over the vast landscape, we noticed that thousands of sheep were peacefully grazing on the ________ and beautiful green meadow that covered the entire hillside.
答案：D lush 蒼翠茂盛的　L3 landscape 主題；與 green meadow 呼應。
小港高中 113上期中2#9
He traveled to many countries in ________ of knowledge and experience.
答案：C in pursuit of 追求　L1〈If—〉第二節 pursuing one's dreams 的名詞用法。
明倫高中 113上期中1#10
Crowds of people ________ to see the Picasso exhibition.
答案：B flock 蜂擁而至　L2 課文 people flocked to stores（民眾湧向商店搶購）。
基隆高中 113上期中2#11
The outbreak of a contagious disease has caused a dramatic reduction in the number of ________ on my grandfather’s farm, as many animals fell ill and died.
答案：D cattle 牛隻　L3 課文 the number of cattle exceeds 1.5 million（阿根廷 Formosa 以養牛為主）。集合名詞，恆為複數。
明倫高中 113上期中1#12
The facts behind the scandal are sure to ________ eventually.
答案：C emerge 浮現、顯露　L2 主題：真相終將水落石出。
基隆高中 113上期中2#13
The people living in this isolated village have been leading a simple and ________ life, without many of the modern conveniences that we take for granted in larger cities.
答案：B primitive 原始的　與 L3 advanced（先進的）形成對比。
明倫高中 上學期期中2#14
Churchill was considered one of the most ________ rulers in British history, for he persevered in his efforts to lead his country to victory during World War II.
答案：C obstinate 固執的、不屈的　由 persevered 推知語意。❌ sloppy 草率的（L2 sloppy journalism）／obedient 順從的。
基隆高中 113上期中2#15
The model of the famous historical ship is a perfect miniature, with all the parts that match those of the real vessel, but it is built on a much smaller ________ to showcase the design in detail.
答案：C scale 比例、規模　L3 面積／規模比較的關鍵字（two times the size / scale）。

三、克漏字測驗（10 題，每題 2 分，共 20 分）

(A) L1〈If—〉第三節　題源：基隆高中 113 上期中1 綜合測驗 16–20

The third stanza explores mental toughness and the power of will  26  one must possess. Life is a game of chance, the speaker warns, so sometimes you must bet  27  you have on one game of “pitch-and-toss” to be successful. However,  28  you lose everything, don’t indulge in self-pity. And if exhaustion  29  you, rely on your willpower to carry you through.  30 , do everything you can to accomplish your goals and never give up.

基隆高中 113上期中1#26
第 26 格
答案：D that　關係代名詞引導形容詞子句修飾先行詞 the power of will，且在子句中當 possess 的受詞。❌ what 不可有先行詞。
基隆高中 113上期中1#27
第 27 格
答案：C whatever　複合關代＝anything that，引導名詞子句當 bet 的受詞：「把你擁有的一切」。
基隆高中 113上期中1#28
第 28 格
答案：A even if 即使　讓步語氣，與後句 don’t indulge in self-pity 相配。❌ no matter 須接 what/how 等疑問詞。
基隆高中 113上期中1#29
第 29 格
答案：C overtake 侵襲、壓倒　exhaustion overtakes you 疲憊襲來（L1 課文原句）。
基隆高中 113上期中1#30
第 30 格
答案：B In short 總之　本句總結前述內容。❌ In contrast 表對比，語意不合。
(B) L2 Battling Fake News　題源：新北高中 113 上期中1 綜合測驗 18–22

Fake news can be defined as news containing false or misleading information that appears truthful. It has long been in existence, but the Internet has recently made creating and sharing it  31  than ever. As a result, online users are often fooled by untrue headlines and reports.

Although fake news  32  many forms, false reporting is usually the most common. This occurs when reporters write stories based on unreliable sources, or when the news deliberately contains false information in order to influence people’s behavior or opinions, or to make them support a certain politician or  33 . The unreliable reports that mislead the public can have very serious consequences.

An example of this took place in 2018, when a hypermarket in Taiwan emailed various news outlets,  34  an impending rise in toilet paper prices.  35  was later revealed that the company’s real intention was to promote their own toilet paper sale.

新北高中 113上期中1#31
第 31 格
答案：C much easier　make + O + 形容詞補語；much 修飾比較級 easier。❌ more easier 雙重比較級；easily 為副詞不可當受詞補語。
新北高中 113上期中1#32
第 32 格
答案：D come in many forms 有多種形式　L2 課文原句 Fake news comes in various forms。
新北高中 113上期中1#33
第 33 格
答案：B cause 理念、主張　support a certain politician or cause（支持某位政治人物或某個訴求）。
新北高中 113上期中1#34
第 34 格
答案：D overstating　現在分詞構句表附帶狀況：emailed…, overstating…（寄信，同時誇大）。主詞 hypermarket 與 overstate 為主動關係。
新北高中 113上期中1#35
第 35 格
答案：A It　虛主詞：It was later revealed that…，that 子句為真主詞。

四、閱讀測驗（8 題，每題 2 分，共 16 分）

(A) L3 Formosa: One Name, Two Stories　短文取自台南二中 113 上期中2 綜合測驗原文

Formosa is an unofficial name for Taiwan. However, there is another Formosa on the banks of the Paraguay River in Argentina. Let’s take a look at these two namesakes and see the aspects in which they are similar and those in which they are different. Besides their shared names, they were found by European explorers almost at the same time. These explorers considered the area they had found to possess such marvelous beauty that they decided to reside there. What’s more, each of them sits on the exact opposite side of the world from the other. If someone in northern Taiwan drilled a hole straight down through the Earth, he or she would end up standing in Formosa, Argentina!

Despite these special links, the two Formosas are, in many ways, still worlds apart. In Taiwan, mountains and dense forests constitute most of the island’s 36,193 square kilometers. On the other hand, over three fourths of Argentina’s Formosa is fairly flat and covered with trees, grassland, and marshland. As for economy, Taiwan is famous for its lively business and advanced information technology industry. By contrast, Formosa in Argentina, which is two times the size of Taiwan, relies primarily on cattle raising and other agricultural activities. In conclusion, whatever differences and similarities exist between the pair, people of these two lands on opposite sides of the world can come face to face and get to know each other better. That could make for a beautiful friendship!

台南二中 113上期中2#36
What is the main purpose of the passage?
答案：B　全文結構＝相似之處（命名、發現時間、對蹠點）＋相異之處（地形、經濟），是典型的比較文。
台南二中 113上期中2#37
According to the passage, what do the two Formosas have in common?
答案：B　they were found by European explorers almost at the same time。❌ (A) 只有台灣是葡萄牙水手命名，阿根廷那個是西班牙探險家。
台南二中 113上期中2#38
Which of the following is TRUE about Argentina’s Formosa?
答案：C　relies primarily on cattle raising and other agricultural activities。❌ (A) 它是台灣的兩倍大；(B)(D) 是台灣的特徵。
台南二中 113上期中2#39
What does the writer suggest in the last sentence of the passage?
答案：B　That could make for a beautiful friendship!（make for 促成、有助於，L3 片語）。
(B) L1 延伸：吉卜林與他的兒子　短文取自新北高中 112 上期中1 綜合測驗 27–31 原文

Rudyard Kipling was a poet, a writer, a husband, and a father. John Kipling, his only son, came into the world in August 1897. A bright and well-educated youth, John mostly performed well at anything that he put his young mind to. Being severely shortsighted, however, meant that his attempt to join the navy at the start of WWI was not successful, nor was his application to serve his country as a member of the air force.

Kipling senior stepped in to deal with the matter. Using his fame and influence, the famous author secured a spot for his son in the army. It was thus as a foot soldier that John Kipling finally arrived in France.

Tragically, just weeks later, the brave 18-year-old was declared “wounded and missing.” Most believed he’d been killed in battle, but this didn’t stop his father from beginning a hopeless search for his remains. This search lasted a full four years before Rudyard Kipling finally put an end to his quest. He admitted that “neither foes nor loving friends [could] hurt” John now. His only comfort was that his son had died “a man.”

新北高中 112上期中1#40
Why did John Kipling fail to join the navy and the air force?
答案：B　Being severely shortsighted（嚴重近視）導致海軍與空軍都無法錄取。
新北高中 112上期中1#41
How did John Kipling finally get into the army?
答案：C　Using his fame and influence, the famous author secured a spot for his son in the army。
新北高中 112上期中1#42
What happened to John Kipling in France?
答案：C　just weeks later, the brave 18-year-old was declared “wounded and missing.”
新北高中 112上期中1#43
What does “put an end to his quest” in the last paragraph mean?
答案：B　put an end to 終結（L1 片語）；quest 為尋找遺骸的追尋，故指「停止搜尋」。前文說那是 a hopeless search。

五、英翻中（3 題，每題 4 分，共 12 分）

基隆高中 113上期中1#44
However, even if you lose everything, don’t indulge in self-pity; and if exhaustion overtakes you, rely on your willpower to carry you through.
參考答案：然而，即使你失去一切，也不要沉溺於自憐；而如果疲憊襲來，就依靠你的意志力支撐你度過難關。
關鍵：even if 即使；indulge in 沉溺於；overtake 侵襲；carry sb. through 支撐某人度過。
新北高中 113上期中1#45
The unreliable reports that mislead the public can have very serious consequences.
參考答案：那些誤導大眾的不可靠報導可能造成非常嚴重的後果。
關鍵：unreliable 不可靠的；that 關係子句修飾 reports；mislead 誤導（L2 核心字）；consequences 後果。
台南二中 113上期中2#46
In Taiwan, mountains and dense forests constitute most of the island’s 36,193 square kilometers.
參考答案：在台灣，山脈與茂密的森林構成了這座島嶼 36,193 平方公里中的大部分土地。
關鍵：dense 茂密的（L3 核心字）；constitute 構成（＝make up）；square kilometers 平方公里。

六、中翻英（4 題，每題 3 分，共 12 分）

基隆高中 113上期中1#47
人們經常把蛾錯當成蝴蝶，但是 Sheila 已經廣泛地研究過這兩種昆蟲，而且能夠輕易地分辨差異。
（限用 mistake…for…）
參考答案：People often mistake moths for butterflies, but Sheila has studied both kinds of insects extensively and can easily tell the difference.
關鍵：mistake A for B 把 A 誤認為 B（L2 片語）；extensively 廣泛地；tell the difference 分辨差異。
基隆高中 113上期中1#48
當一個人在度假時，重要的是把工作或課業拋諸腦後並充分地享樂。
（限用 to the full）
參考答案：When one is on holiday, it’s important to forget all about work or school and enjoy oneself to the full.
關鍵：虛主詞 it + to V；to the full 充分地（L1〈If—〉片語 cherish life to the full）。
新北高中 112上期中1#49
雖然這些粉絲來自於各行各業，但是這些差異並未阻止他們一起支持那支隊伍。
（限用 all walks of life）
參考答案：While the fans came from all walks of life, these differences didn’t stop them from supporting their team together.
關鍵：(from) all walks of life 各行各業（L1 課文片語）；stop sb. from V-ing 阻止某人。
新北高中 112上期中1#50
在接受我們點餐之前，這名服務生拿出菜單並且清楚說明晚餐的各種不同選擇。
（限用 lay out）
參考答案：Before he took our order, the waiter held up a menu and laid out the various options we had for dinner.
關鍵：lay out 鋪陳、清楚陳述（L1 片語，三態 lay-laid-laid）；various options 各種選擇。

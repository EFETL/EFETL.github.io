📖 A. 疫情後的通貨膨脹與物價上漲第 21–24 題　點任一句展開解析
全篇大意　本篇說明疫情後物價上漲（通膨）的成因、對不同族群的衝擊、央行升息的因應，以及消費者如何精打細算。文體為說明文，時態以過去式敘述成因、現在式陳述通則。
本篇答案21up22affect23more24before they buy anything at all
▶A-1第 21 題After the pandemic, many families noticed that the prices of daily goods kept going 21up.
疫情之後，許多家庭注意到日常用品的價格持續上漲。
🔍 句構分析①After the pandemic修飾語介系詞片語，當時間副詞
②many familiesS 主詞主詞（複數）
③noticedV 動詞及物動詞，過去式
④that the prices of daily goods kept going upO 受詞that 引導名詞子句當受詞
⑤S: the prices / V: kept / C: going up└ 子句keep + V-ing「持續…」
文法重點　① that 名詞子句作受詞，that 可省略。② keep + V-ing 表「持續做某事」，故空格 21 填 up（go up 物價上漲）。
📚 單字pandemic n. (全球) 大流行病；疫情notice vt. 注意到daily adj. 每日的；日常的goods n. 商品（恆用複數）go up phr. 上漲；上升
▶A-2Around the globe, the cost of living climbed at a pace not witnessed in decades.
全球各地的生活成本，以數十年未見的速度攀升。
🔍 句構分析①Around the globe修飾語介系詞片語，當地方副詞
②the cost of livingS 主詞主詞；of living 修飾 cost
③climbedV 動詞不及物動詞，過去式
④at a pace not witnessed in decades修飾語介系詞片語；not witnessed in decades 為過去分詞片語，後位修飾 pace（＝ that had not been witnessed）
文法重點　過去分詞片語後位修飾名詞，等於省略「關代＋be 動詞」的關係子句。
📚 單字globe n. 地球；全球cost of living phr. 生活成本climb vi. 攀升pace n. 速度；步調witness vt. 目睹；見證decade n. 十年
▶A-3Several forces combined to drive this surge: tangled supply chains, soaring energy bills, and a sudden rebound in demand.

🔍 句構分析①Several forcesS 主詞主詞
②combinedV 動詞不及物動詞，過去式
③to drive this surge修飾語不定詞片語，表目的／結果
④tangled supply chains, soaring energy bills, and a sudden rebound in demand同位語冒號後三個名詞片語並列，說明 Several forces 的內容
文法重點　冒號（:）用來「列舉、說明」前面的抽象名詞，是說明文常見寫法。
📚 單字force n. 力量；因素combine vi. 結合；聯合drive vt. 促成；推動surge n. 激增；急漲tangled adj. 糾結的；混亂的supply chain phr. 供應鏈soaring adj. 飆漲的rebound n. 反彈；回升demand n. 需求
▶A-4A simple lunch box, a cup of coffee, and even a bus ticket suddenly cost more than before.
一個簡單的便當、一杯咖啡，甚至一張公車票，突然都比以前貴了。
🔍 句構分析①A simple lunch box, a cup of coffee, and even a bus ticketS 主詞三個名詞片語並列作主詞
②costV 動詞及物動詞，過去式（cost-cost-cost 三態同形）
③moreO 受詞受詞；more 此處為代名詞
④than before修飾語比較的對象
文法重點　cost 三態同形，須靠句中 suddenly、than before 判斷是過去式。
📚 單字lunch box n. 便當even adv. 甚至cost vt. 花費（cost-cost-cost）
▶A-5When factories finally reopened, they struggled to produce goods quickly enough to satisfy eager customers.
當工廠終於重新開工時，他們卻難以把商品生產得夠快，來滿足急切的顧客。
🔍 句構分析①When factories finally reopened副詞子句S: factories / V: reopened
②theyS 主詞指 factories
③struggledV 動詞不及物動詞，「費力、難以」
④to produce goods修飾語不定詞片語，接在 struggle 之後
⑤quickly enough to satisfy eager customers修飾語adv. + enough + to V「夠…而足以…」
文法重點　enough 修飾形容詞／副詞時要「後位」：quickly enough（不可說 enough quickly）。
📚 單字factory n. 工廠reopen vi. 重新開張struggle vi. 掙扎；費力produce vt. 生產satisfy vt. 滿足eager adj. 渴望的；急切的
▶A-6第 22 題Economists call this steady rise in prices "inflation," and it can 22affect how much our money is really worth.
經濟學家把這種價格的持續上升稱為「通貨膨脹」，它會影響我們的錢究竟值多少。
🔍 句構分析①Economists子句一 S主詞
②callV 動詞不完全及物動詞
③this steady rise in pricesO 受詞受詞
④"inflation"OC 受詞補語受詞補語（名詞）
⑤對等連接詞and連接兩個對等子句
⑥it子句二 S指 inflation
⑦can affectV 動詞情態助動詞 + 原形動詞
⑧how much our money is really worthO 受詞how 引導名詞子句作受詞，子句內用「肯定句語序」
文法重點　① call + O + OC（把 O 稱為 OC）。② 名詞子句用直述句語序：how much our money is（不可寫 is our money）。空格 22 選 affect（vt. 影響），勿與名詞 effect 混淆。
📚 單字economist n. 經濟學家steady adj. 穩定的；持續的rise n. 上升inflation n. 通貨膨脹affect vt. 影響worth adj. 值…的
▶A-7Whereas a mild rise in prices can signal a healthy economy, runaway inflation quietly erodes people's savings.
溫和的物價上漲雖可能代表經濟健康，失控的通膨卻悄悄侵蝕人們的存款。
🔍 句構分析①Whereas a mild rise in prices can signal a healthy economy副詞子句S: a mild rise / V: can signal / O: a healthy economy
②runaway inflationS 主詞主詞
③quietly修飾語情狀副詞
④erodesV 動詞及物動詞，現在式（陳述通則）
⑤people's savingsO 受詞受詞
文法重點　whereas＝「然而、相對地」，為從屬連接詞，用來作前後對比（同 while 的對比用法）。
📚 單字whereas conj. 然而；相對地mild adj. 溫和的signal vt. 顯示；預示runaway adj. 失控的erode vt. 侵蝕savings n. 存款（恆用複數）
▶A-8Retirees on fixed incomes suffered most, for their pensions bought noticeably less with each passing month.
靠固定收入的退休族受害最深，因為他們的退休金每過一個月能買到的東西就明顯變少。
🔍 句構分析①RetireesS 主詞主詞；on fixed incomes 介片後位修飾
②sufferedV 動詞不及物動詞
③most修飾語副詞最高級「最…」
④對等連接詞「因為」for語氣較 because 弱，只能置於後句
⑤their pensions bought noticeably less with each passing month原因子句S: their pensions / V: bought / O: noticeably less
文法重點　for 當「因為」時是對等連接詞，不可放句首；because 則可。
📚 單字retiree n. 退休人士fixed income phr. 固定收入suffer vi. 受苦；受害pension n. 退休金noticeably adv. 明顯地
▶A-9第 23 題When wages do not grow as fast as prices, people must spend 23more carefully than they used to.
當薪資的成長趕不上物價時，人們花錢必須比以前更謹慎。
🔍 句構分析①When wages do not grow as fast as prices副詞子句S: wages / V: do not grow / M: as fast as prices（同級比較）
②peopleS 主詞主詞
③must spendV 動詞情態助動詞 + 原形
④more carefully than they used to修飾語比較級副詞；used to 後省略 spend
文法重點　① 同級比較 as + 原級 + as。② 兩音節以上的副詞比較級用 more + adv. + than，故空格 23 選 more。③ used to + V 表「過去習慣」。
📚 單字wage n. 薪資；工資grow vi. 成長as…as phr. 和…一樣（同級比較）used to phr. 過去習慣於
▶A-10Given the pressure, central banks raised interest rates in an effort to cool the overheating market.

🔍 句構分析①Given the pressure修飾語given 當介系詞「考量到、由於」
②central banksS 主詞主詞
③raisedV 動詞及物動詞（raise-raised-raised）
④interest ratesO 受詞受詞
⑤in an effort to cool the overheating market修飾語表目的：「為了…而努力」
文法重點　raise（vt. 提高，需受詞）vs. rise（vi. 上升，不接受詞）是統測常考對比。
📚 單字given prep. 考量到；由於central bank phr. 中央銀行raise vt. 提高；調升interest rate phr. 利率in an effort to phr. 為了…而努力overheating adj. 過熱的

▶A-12Moreover, once shoppers expect prices to keep climbing, they rush to buy, which pushes prices even higher.
此外，一旦消費者預期物價會繼續上漲，就會搶著購買，這又把價格推得更高。

④rushV 動詞不及物動詞

📖 B. 遠距與混合辦公的新常態第 25–28 題　點任一句展開解析
全篇大意　本篇談疫情如何把「在家工作」從特權變成常態，並比較遠距辦公的好處（省通勤、生產力持平）與代價（公私界線模糊、孤獨與倦怠），最後導向「混合辦公」與「彈性」成為求職者的新要求。
本篇答案25whether26called27traveling28wherever they choose to work
▶B-1During the pandemic, millions of workers had to stay home, and many of them worked online for the very first time.
疫情期間，數百萬名工作者必須待在家裡，其中許多人是生平第一次在線上工作。
🔍 句構分析①During the pandemic修飾語介系詞片語表期間（during + 名詞）
②millions of workers / had to stay子句一 S/Vhave to 的過去式，表「必須」
③homeC 補語此處 home 為副詞，「在家」
④many of them / worked子句二 S/VM: online, for the very first time
文法重點　during（介系詞）＋名詞；while（連接詞）＋子句，勿混用。
📚 單字millions of phr. 數百萬的have to phr. 必須for the first time phr. 第一次
▶B-2Before the crisis, working from home was regarded as a rare privilege granted to only a fortunate few.
在這場危機之前，在家工作被視為只有少數幸運兒才享有的稀有特權。
🔍 句構分析①Before the crisis修飾語時間介片
②working from homeS 主詞動名詞片語作主詞
③was regarded asV 動詞被動語態（regard A as B → A be regarded as B）
④a rare privilegeC 補語主詞補語
⑤granted to only a fortunate few修飾語過去分詞片語後位修飾 privilege（被授予）
文法重點　regard A as B 變被動後 as 保留；the few＝「少數人」，few 前加形容詞可作名詞用。
📚 單字crisis n. 危機regard A as B phr. 把 A 視為 Brare adj. 稀有的privilege n. 特權grant vt. 授予fortunate adj. 幸運的

▶B-4第 25 題Now that offices have reopened, a lot of companies still let their staff choose 25whether to work from home or come in.

🔍 句構分析①Now that offices have reopened副詞子句now that＝「既然、如今…」；子句用現在完成式
②a lot of companiesS 主詞主詞
③letV 動詞使役動詞

⑤choose whether to work from home or come inOC 受詞補語原形動詞作受詞補語；whether…or… 為 choose 的受詞

📚 單字now that conj. 既然；如今staff n. 員工（全體）whether…or… phr. 是…還是…come in phr. 進辦公室；進來
▶B-5To everyone's surprise, productivity in many firms held steady, and in some cases even rose.
令所有人驚訝的是，許多公司的生產力維持穩定，有些情況下甚至還提升了。
🔍 句構分析①To everyone's surprise修飾語「令人驚訝的是」，to one's + 情緒名詞
②productivityS 主詞主詞；in many firms 介片修飾
③held / steadyV/Chold + 形容詞＝維持…狀態
④對等連接and後句主詞相同而省略：(productivity) even rose
文法重點　hold steady 中的 steady 是形容詞（作補語），不是副詞。rise 為不及物動詞（rise-rose-risen）。
📚 單字productivity n. 生產力firm n. 公司hold steady phr. 保持穩定case n. 情況rise vi. 上升
▶B-6第 26 題This mix of home and office is often 26called "hybrid work."
這種在家與辦公室並行的方式，常被稱為「混合辦公」。
🔍 句構分析①This mix of home and officeS 主詞主詞；of 片語修飾 mix
②often修飾語頻率副詞，置於 be 動詞之後
③is calledV 動詞被動語態（be + 過去分詞）
④"hybrid work"C 補語主詞補語；由主動 call O OC 轉來
文法重點　主動 They call this mix “hybrid work.” → 被動 This mix is called “hybrid work.” 空格 26 須填過去分詞 called。
📚 單字mix n. 混合hybrid adj. 混合的；複合的
▶B-7Freed from long commutes, employees reported sleeping better and spending more meaningful hours with their families.

🔍 句構分析①Freed from long commutes分詞構句＝ Because they were freed…；被動關係用過去分詞開頭
②employeesS 主詞主詞
③reportedV 動詞及物動詞
④sleeping better and spending more meaningful hours with their familiesO 受詞兩個動名詞片語並列作受詞
文法重點　分詞構句：主動用 V-ing，被動用 p.p. 開頭；report 後接動名詞不接不定詞。
📚 單字free vt. 使解脫commute n. 通勤report vt. 表示；陳述meaningful adj. 有意義的
▶B-8Yet remote work brought fresh challenges, for the line between office and home grew dangerously blurred.

🔍 句構分析①Yet修飾語轉折連接副詞「然而」
②remote work / brought / fresh challengesS/V/OS+V+O
③對等連接詞「因為」for引導原因子句
④the line between office and home grew dangerously blurred原因子句S: the line / V: grew / C: blurred（grow + adj.＝變得）
文法重點　grow 當連綴動詞時後接形容詞；dangerously 是副詞，修飾形容詞 blurred。
📚 單字remote adj. 遠距的；遙遠的fresh adj. 新的line n. 界線grow vi. 變得blurred adj. 模糊的
▶B-9第 27 題Employees enjoy it because it saves the long time they once spent 27traveling to work every morning.
員工喜歡這種方式，因為它省下了他們過去每天早上通勤所花的漫長時間。
🔍 句構分析①Employees / enjoy / itS/V/O主要子句
②because it saves the long time …副詞子句S: it / V: saves / O: the long time
③(that) they once spent traveling to work every morning關係子句受格關代 that 省略，修飾 the long time
④spend + 時間 + V-ing句型空格 27 須填 V-ing：traveling
文法重點　spend + 時間／金錢 + (in) V-ing「花時間／錢做…」；受格關代可省略。
📚 單字save vt. 節省spend vt. 花費（時間、金錢）travel to work phr. 通勤上班
▶B-10Some workers, isolated from their colleagues, quietly struggled with loneliness and a creeping sense of burnout.

②isolated from their colleagues補述過去分詞片語，前後用逗號補充說明主詞

文法重點　分詞片語插在主詞與動詞之間作補述時，前後都要有逗號。

▶B-12Recognizing these drawbacks, managers began redesigning offices as places for collaboration rather than routine tasks.

②managersS 主詞主詞

▶B-13第 28 題As long as the job gets done well, more bosses today believe that their teams can be trusted 28wherever they choose to work.

②more bosses todayS 主詞主詞
③believeV 動詞及物動詞

文法重點　① as long as＝「只要」（條件）。② get + p.p. 是口語化的被動。③ wherever 引導讓步副詞子句。

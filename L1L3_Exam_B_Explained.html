(A) L1〈If—〉導讀　題源：金門高中 112 上期中1 Cloze (B) 17–21

The poem “If—” has not only moved millions of its readers but also has inspired them for more than a century.  26  in 1910, this epic poem contains nothing more than a father’s advice to his son on  27  to live a good life. The words in the poem are full of wisdom and are still  28  to this day. In the first stanza, the son is advised to  29  when people are not friendly to him. At the same time, he needs to have faith  30  himself when others blame him and to refrain from returning the hatred.

第 26 格
答案：C Published　過去分詞構句：詩「被」發表 ＝ (Having been) Published in 1910。主詞 this epic poem 與 publish 為被動關係。
金門高中 112上期中1#27
第 27 格
答案：C how　advice on how to live a good life（如何過好人生）。疑問詞 + to V。
金門高中 112上期中1#28
第 28 格
答案：A relevant 切題的、仍適用的　呼應 to this day（至今仍受用）。
金門高中 112上期中1#29
第 29 格
答案：D remain calm　be advised to + 原形動詞；remain 為連綴動詞，後接形容詞 calm 當補語，不可用副詞 calmly。

(B) L3 Formosa　題源：金門高中 112 上期中2 克漏字 (B) 11–15

Many people know that Formosa is an alternative name for Taiwan. However, there is also another place called Formosa  31  the banks of the Paraguay River in Argentina. While  32  some similarities, there are also several ways  33  they are different.  34 , both were unintentionally discovered by Europeans in the sixteenth century. Second, they are both at a latitude of 23.5°, with the exception that one is in the northern hemisphere and the other is in the southern hemisphere.  35 , this geographical fact also means an interesting coincidence—the two Formosas are on the exact opposite sides of the world.

金門高中 112上期中2#31
第 31 格
答案：A located on　＝(which is) located on the banks of…，過去分詞片語後位修飾 another place。
金門高中 112上期中2#32
第 32 格
答案：B sharing　While (they are) sharing some similarities——副詞子句省略主詞與 be 動詞的分詞構句。
金門高中 112上期中2#33
第 33 格
答案：B in which　several ways in which they are different ＝ they are different in several ways。
金門高中 112上期中2#34
第 34 格
答案：C For a start 首先　與下一句 Second, they are both at a latitude… 形成列舉順序。
金門高中 112上期中2#35
第 35 格
答案：B What’s more 此外　再補充一項有趣巧合，屬累加。❌ In short/In sum 是總結；Worse still 表更糟。

四、閱讀測驗（8 題，每題 2 分，共 16 分）

(A) L2 Battling Fake News　短文取自金門高中 112 上期中1 Cloze (A) 原文

Fake news is usually defined as news containing false information that appears true. Its existence has long been an issue. In recent years, the Internet and social media have made creating and sharing news easier and faster. With so much fake news being spread every day, it is now more important to think critically about the news we read online. Fake news basically comes in two forms. One is from sloppy journalism and the other is due to a deliberate attempt to achieve some social results.

In 1998, a British researcher published a small-scale study, which linked the MMR vaccine to autism. Soon after, there was much coverage of this flawed study. Though later studies with larger samples countered the British researcher’s claim and proved the original study misleading, the damage had been done. Parents who were exposed to the coverage of these false reports refused to vaccinate their children for fear that it might do more harm than good. For another example, a major hypermarket sent an email to the media outlets in 2018, exaggerating an upcoming hike in the price of toilet paper. The reporting prompted a wave of panic buying among Taiwanese people, many of whom feared that they would run out of it soon. Nevertheless, people later found out that the news was not real and that the true purpose of the hypermarket was to advertise its own toilet paper promotion.

After these events, every one of us should learn to tell fake news from real news. The true news usually includes a writer’s name, multiple sources, and objective language that doesn’t appeal to readers’ emotion. These features can help us avoid falling into the trap of fake news so that we can see the world in a balanced perspective.

答案：B　the Internet and social media have made creating and sharing news easier and faster。
金門高中 112上期中1#37

答案：C　Parents… refused to vaccinate their children for fear that it might do more harm than good。❌ (A) 後續研究是「推翻」；(D) 文中說 much coverage。
金門高中 112上期中1#38
What was the hypermarket’s real intention in 2018?
答案：C　the true purpose of the hypermarket was to advertise its own toilet paper promotion。
金門高中 112上期中1#39

答案：D　文中列出署名、多個來源、客觀用語三項，未提「先發表在社群媒體」。
(B) L1 延伸：吉卜林生平　短文取自基隆高中 113 上期中1 文意選填 26–35 原文

Rudyard Kipling was a famous British writer, born in India on December 30, 1865. His childhood in India influenced much of his writing. When he was five years old, Kipling was sent to England for his education, which was common for British children living in India. However, he did not have a happy time in school, and this experience affected him for the rest of his life.

Kipling returned to India as a young man and began working as a journalist. During this time, he started writing stories and poems. His first book of poems, Departmental Ditties, was published in 1886. However, Kipling is best known for his books The Jungle Book (1894) and Kim (1901). The Jungle Book is a collection of stories about a boy named Mowgli, who is raised by animals in the jungle. These stories are still very popular today and have been made into movies and TV shows.

Kipling was also known for his poetry, including famous works like If— and Gunga Din. His writing often focused on themes of bravery, loyalty, and the British Empire.

In 1907, Kipling became the first English-language writer to receive the Nobel Prize in Literature, which is one of the highest honors for a writer. His ability to create memorable stories and poems has made him one of the most well-known authors of his time. Despite some criticism of his views on the British Empire, his contributions to literature are still appreciated today. Kipling passed away on January 18, 1936, but his works continue to inspire readers around the world.

What is the passage mainly about?
答案：B　依時間順序介紹出生、求學、記者生涯、代表作、諾貝爾獎與身後評價——典型的生平介紹。
基隆高中 113上期中1#41
Why was Kipling sent to England when he was five?
答案：C　which was common for British children living in India。
基隆高中 113上期中1#42
Which of the following is TRUE about Kipling’s career?
答案：B　先當記者（返回印度後），之後才以 The Jungle Book(1894) 聞名。❌ (C) 諾貝爾獎 1907 晚於 1894；(D) 文中明說 some criticism。
基隆高中 113上期中1#43
What does the passage suggest about Kipling’s reputation today?
答案：C　Despite some criticism…, his contributions to literature are still appreciated today。

金門高中 112上期中1#44
Kipling reminds his son that life is a game of chance, so he sometimes must bet whatever he has on one game of “pitch-and-toss” to be successful.

關鍵：a game of chance 賭運氣的遊戲；bet A on B 把 A 押在 B 上；whatever 複合關代＝任何…的東西。
金門高中 112上期中1#45
The true news usually includes a writer’s name, multiple sources, and objective language that doesn’t appeal to readers’ emotion.
參考答案：真實的新聞通常會包含作者的姓名、多個消息來源，以及不訴諸讀者情緒的客觀用語。
關鍵：multiple sources 多個來源；objective 客觀的（L2 核心字）；appeal to 訴諸、打動。
金門高中 112上期中2#46
Most of Argentina’s Formosa, which is twice the size of Taiwan, is fairly flat and covered with trees, grassland, or marshland.

關鍵：twice the size of 倍數表達（L3 句型）；be covered with 覆蓋著；marshland 沼澤地。

▶B-9第 27 題When goods are few but buyers are many, prices usually 27rise.

🔍 句構分析①When goods are few but buyers are many副詞子句兩個對比子句由 but 連接

文法重點　空格 27 選 rise：rise 為**不及物**動詞（自己上升，不接受詞）；raise 為及物動詞（提高某物），是常考對比。

▶B-10This basic principle of supply and demand, familiar to any economist, explains much of the recent inflation.

🔍 句構分析①This basic principle of supply and demandS 主詞主詞；中心語為 principle
②familiar to any economist補述形容詞片語補述主詞；be familiar to + 人
③explainsV 動詞及物動詞，第三人稱單數

文法重點　be familiar to + 人「為某人所熟知」；be familiar with + 事物「熟悉某事物」，主客易位須分清。

▶B-11Moreover, global factors such as fuel prices and shipping delays made the situation even more complicated.

Passage 1
Riding to School

I go to school by (11) every day. It takes me about twenty minutes. On the (12), I pass a small bridge and a big park. Last Monday my bike had a (13), so I had to push it. A kind man in the (14) near the school helped me, and he did not take any money. Now I check my bike every night, and I never ride on the (15) because the cars there go too fast.

全文中譯我每天騎腳踏車上學，大約要花二十分鐘。路上我會經過一座小橋和一座大公園。上星期一我的腳踏車爆胎，所以只好用推的。學校附近停車場的一位好心先生幫了我，而且沒有收錢。現在我每天晚上都會檢查我的車，也絕不騎上公路，因為那裡的車開得太快了。

解析後文提到 my bike，可知是騎「腳踏車 bicycle」上學。planet 行星、crayon 蠟筆、flower 花都不是交通工具。

Passage 2
The City Bus

Taking the (16) is cheap and easy in our city. Most buses come every ten minutes. In the morning many (17) must stand because all the seats are full. When you want to get off, please press the button before your (18). The (19) will open the door for you. Buses are slow when the (20) is heavy, but they are still the best way for students.

全文中譯在我們的城市裡，搭公車既便宜又方便。大部分的公車每十分鐘一班。早上很多乘客必須站著，因為位子都坐滿了。當你要下車時，請在你要下的那一站之前按鈴。司機會為你開門。交通擁擠的時候公車很慢，但對學生來說仍然是最好的方式。

Passage 1
The Day the System Went Down

At our school everything runs on one computer system: the bell, the doors, the lunch list. One Monday in May the system failed at 7:50. (11) No bell rang, so the first class began when a teacher looked at her watch and said so. The lunch list was gone, so the cooks made the same meal for everybody. Nobody could enter the office downstairs, (12) the door needed a card that no longer worked. The strange part is that the day went well, in a soft and quiet way. (13), several teachers said it was the calmest Monday of the year. A system is there to hold the small choices for us, (14) that day we found out we could still make them.

全文中譯我們學校所有東西都靠一套電腦系統運作：鐘聲、門禁、午餐名單。五月某個星期一，系統在七點五十分故障了。接下來發生的事出乎所有人意料。鐘沒有響，所以第一堂課是在一位老師看了手錶、開口說了之後才開始。午餐名單不見了，於是廚師替所有人做了同一道菜。沒有人能進到樓下的辦公室，因為那扇門需要一張已經失效的卡。奇怪的是，那一天以一種柔和安靜的方式過得很順利。事實上，好幾位老師說那是一年中最平靜的星期一。系統本來是替我們保管那些小決定的，但那天我們發現，我們其實還是做得出來。

Passage 2
Why a Phone Is Hard to Put Down

A phone is not one thing. It is a door to a hundred places, and each one was built by people whose job is to keep you there. (15) A page that never ends has no natural stopping point, so your hand keeps moving. A red dot on the screen is small, (16) it asks a question your brain cannot leave alone: what did I miss? None of this means you are weak or that the people who built them are horrible. (17), it means you are playing against a system built by many people with a great deal of data. The answer is seldom to try harder. It is to change the room: leave the phone downstairs, not upstairs, (18) turn off the dot that asks the question.

全文中譯手機不是一樣東西。它是通往一百個地方的門，而每一個地方都是由那些以「留住你」為工作的人打造的。這些地方被設計得很難離開。一個永遠滑不到底的頁面沒有自然的停止點，所以你的手就一直動。螢幕上的紅點很小，但它提出了一個你的大腦無法放著不管的問題：我錯過了什麼？這一切都不代表你意志薄弱，也不代表做這些東西的人很可怕。相反地，它代表你面對的是一套由許多人用大量資料打造出來的系統。答案很少是「更努力一點」。而是改變環境：把手機留在樓下，不要放樓上，並關掉那個提出問題的紅點。

NOTICE
Computer Room Rules

Open 12:30 to 13:10 and after school until 17:00

  • Sit at the machine with your own number; do not move to another
  • No food at the desks, not even a hot dog; stationery only
  • Save your work on your own page, not on the machine
  • Tell the teacher at once if a machine will not start
  • No sweet drinks; one naughty moment can end a machine's life

Work saved on a machine is cleaned off every Friday night. Students lose work this way every term, and nothing can be done about it later. Save twice: once here and once on an independent machine of your own. Lost work is a small bomb in a busy week.

Games are allowed after school but not at lunch time. If every machine is in use at lunch, students who are doing school work have the first right to a seat, and a student playing a game must give it up.

ARTICLE
The Machines That Wait for Us

Fifty years ago, in one European country as in ours, a computer sat in a room of its own and people went to it. Today we carry one, and it goes to every place we go.

This changed something nobody planned. When a machine waits in a room, there are hours when you are simply not near it. Those hours are gone now. A question that used to wait until Monday is answered at eleven on Saturday night, and the person who answers it is not paid for that hour.

The machines did not ask for this. We have not yet made the new rules that a machine in every pocket needs. Every tool before this one got such rules in the end; this one is simply too young, and we are writing them now, badly, one family at a time.

全文中譯

五十年前，不論在某個歐洲國家或在我們這裡，電腦都待在自己的房間裡，人們走去找它。今天我們隨身帶著一台，它跟著我們到任何地方。

這改變了一件沒有人計畫過的事。當機器待在一個房間裡，就會有一些時段你根本不在它旁邊。那些時段現在消失了。以前要等到星期一才處理的問題，現在在星期六晚上十一點就被回覆，而回覆的人並沒有因為那個小時拿到薪水。

機器並沒有要求這樣。我們還沒有訂出「每個口袋裡都有一台機器」所需要的新規則。每一種更早的工具最後都有了這樣的規則；這一種只是太年輕，而我們正在訂，訂得很糟，一個家庭一個家庭地訂。

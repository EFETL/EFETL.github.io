AD
Big Weekend Sale at Green Market

Saturday and Sunday only!

  • Fresh fruit — buy two boxes, get one free
  • Milk — only 25 dollars a bottle
  • Beef and pork — 30% off all day
  • Cookies and candy — five bags for 100 dollars

We open at seven in the morning and close at ten at night. Come early, because the fruit goes fast. Members save another five dollars on everything.

全文中譯

只有週六和週日！

  • 新鮮水果——買兩盒送一盒
  • 牛奶——一瓶只要 25 元
  • 牛肉和豬肉——全日七折
  • 餅乾和糖果——五包 100 元

我們早上七點開門，晚上十點關門。請早點來，因為水果賣得很快。會員每樣商品再省五元。

Questions 21–24
21When is the sale?
AEvery day this week
BOn Saturday and Sunday
COnly on Saturday
DOnly on Sunday
✔ 正確答案：(B)　On Saturday and Sunday
中譯這場特賣是什麼時候？
解析廣告開頭寫 Saturday and Sunday only，表示只有週六和週日兩天。
22How much is one bottle of milk?
A25 dollars
B5 dollars
C30 dollars
D100 dollars
✔ 正確答案：(A)　25 dollars
中譯一瓶牛奶多少錢？
解析清單中寫 Milk — only 25 dollars a bottle。100 元是五包餅乾糖果的價錢，5 元是會員折扣。
23Why should people come early?
AThe beef is more expensive later
BMembers must come first
CThe fruit sells out fast
DThe store closes at seven
✔ 正確答案：(C)　The fruit sells out fast
中譯人們為什麼應該早點來？
解析文中說 Come early, because the fruit goes fast，表示水果很快就賣完。七點是開門時間，不是關門時間。
24What do members get?
AFree milk
BA free box of fruit
CFive dollars off
DNone of them
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Five dollars off
中譯會員可以得到什麼？
解析最後一句 Members save another five dollars on everything，表示會員每樣東西再折五元。買兩盒送一盒是全部客人都有的優惠。
EMAIL
An Invitation to a Food Festival

Dear Ken,

There will be a food festival in my hometown next Saturday. It starts at ten in the morning and ends at five.

There will be more than forty small shops. You can try beef soup, steamed pork buns, and ice cream made with fresh milk. Everything is cheap, and some shops give free food to children.

My mother will cook her famous chicken. It is really good, and everyone likes it. Take the train to East Station, and I will meet you there.

Yours,
Peter

全文中譯

親愛的肯：

下星期六我的家鄉會有一場美食節。活動早上十點開始，五點結束。

現場會有四十多家小店。你可以嚐嚐牛肉湯、蒸豬肉包，還有用新鮮牛奶做的冰淇淋。每樣東西都很便宜，有些店還會免費請小朋友吃。

我媽媽會煮她拿手的雞肉。那真的很好吃，大家都喜歡。你搭火車到東站，我會去那裡接你。

你的朋友
彼得

ARTICLE
Less Sugar, Better Health

Many students in Taiwan drink sweet tea and eat candy every day. Doctors say this is not good for us. Sweet food can stop your hunger for a short time, but you get hungry again soon.

Too much sugar can make people fat, and it is also bad for the heart. It hurts our teeth, too.

Doctors do not ask us to give up all sweet food. They only ask us to eat less of it. Fruit is a much better choice than candy, because it has water and other good things in it.

Small steps can improve our health in a big way.

全文中譯

台灣有很多學生每天喝甜茶、吃糖果。醫生說這樣對我們不好。甜食可以暫時止住飢餓，但你很快又會餓了。

糖分太多會讓人變胖，也對心臟不好。它同樣會傷害我們的牙齒。

醫生並不是要我們完全戒掉甜食。他們只是要我們少吃一點。水果是比糖果好得多的選擇，因為水果含有水分和其他有益的成分。

小小的一步，就能大大改善我們的健康。

Questions 29–32
29What do many students in Taiwan do every day?

BCook their own food

DDrink only water

30What can too much sugar do?
AClean the teeth
BMake people thin
CHelp the heart
DMake people fat

ATo eat less sweet food
BTo eat more candy

中譯醫生要求我們怎麼做？
解析文中說 Doctors do not ask us to give up all sweet food. They only ask us to eat less of it，是少吃而不是完全不吃。
32Why is fruit a better choice than candy?

CIt is sweeter

中譯為什麼水果比糖果好？
解析文中說 because it has water and other good things in it。文章沒有提到價錢或方便攜帶。

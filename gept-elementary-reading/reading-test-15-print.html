ARTICLE
Night Markets in Taiwan

Night markets are very popular in Taiwan. They open when the sun goes down, and many of them are still busy at midnight.

You can find almost anything there. Some shops sell hot food, and others sell clothes, shoes or toys. Prices are usually low, and you can ask the seller for a better deal.

Many foreign visitors think the night market is the best place to learn about our way of life. It is loud and crowded, but nobody seems to mind.

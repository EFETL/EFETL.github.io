EMAIL
An E-mail from a Cousin

Hi Kevin,

How are you? My family is going to have a big party for my grandmother next Saturday. She will be seventy-five years old. All our relatives will come, and my aunt will cook spaghetti and fried chicken.

Can you come with your parents? Our house is on Peace Street, right behind the junior high school. The party starts at six in the evening, and we will cut the cake at eight.

Please call me before Thursday. But if you are busy that day, we can meet next month.

Your cousin,
Amy

全文中譯

凱文你好：

你好嗎？下星期六我家要為我奶奶辦一場盛大的宴會。她要滿七十五歲了。我們所有的親戚都會來，我阿姨會煮義大利麵和炸雞。

你可以和你爸媽一起來嗎？我家在和平街上，就在國中後面。宴會晚上六點開始，我們八點會切蛋糕。

請在星期四之前打電話給我。不過，如果你那天很忙，我們也可以下個月再見面。

你的表妹
艾咪

Questions 21–24
21Why does Amy write this e-mail?
ATo borrow a car from Kevin
BTo ask Kevin for money
CTo say sorry to Kevin
DTo invite Kevin to a family party
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To invite Kevin to a family party
中譯艾咪為什麼寫這封電子郵件？
解析信中先說 My family is going to have a big party for my grandmother，接著問 Can you come with your parents?，是邀請凱文參加家庭聚會。
22Where is Amy's house?
ANext to a park
BIn front of a hospital
CBehind the junior high school
DOn a farm in the country
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Behind the junior high school
中譯艾咪家在哪裡？
解析信中說 Our house is on Peace Street, right behind the junior high school。
23What will happen at eight?
AThe guests will leave
BThey will cut the cake
CThe party will start
DAmy will call Kevin
✔ 正確答案：(B)　They will cut the cake
中譯八點會發生什麼事？
解析The party starts at six in the evening, and we will cut the cake at eight，可知八點是切蛋糕，六點才是宴會開始。
24What does Amy ask Kevin to do before Thursday?
ACall her
BCook spaghetti
CVisit her grandmother
DBuy a gift
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Call her
中譯艾咪要凱文在星期四前做什麼？
解析Please call me before Thursday，所以是打電話給她。煮義大利麵的是艾咪的阿姨。

ARTICLE
My Grandmother Goes to School

My grandmother is seventy years old, but she is still a student. Last year she began to learn English at a school on Park Street. On her first day, she did not even know the alphabet.

Her teacher is a young woman from a college near her home. Grandmother puts on her black glasses and studies for two hours every night. Sometimes the homework is difficult, but she never gives up.

Now she can write short letters to her granddaughter in Canada. She always says that it is never too late to learn.

全文中譯

我奶奶七十歲了，但她還是個學生。去年她開始在公園街上的一所學校學英文。第一天上課時，她連字母表都不會。

她的老師是一位年輕女生，來自她家附近的一所大學。奶奶會戴上她的黑色眼鏡，每天晚上讀兩個小時。有時候作業很難，但她從不放棄。

現在她可以寫短信給在加拿大的孫女了。她總是說，學習永遠不嫌晚。

Questions 29–32
29What did Grandmother not know on her first day?
AThe teacher's name
BThe alphabet
CThe way to school
DThe price of the book
✔ 正確答案：(B)　The alphabet
中譯奶奶第一天不知道什麼？
解析On her first day, she did not even know the alphabet，指她連字母表都不會。
30Who teaches Grandmother English?
AHer granddaughter
BA young woman from a college
CHer son
DA teacher from Canada
✔ 正確答案：(B)　A young woman from a college
中譯誰教奶奶英文？
解析Her teacher is a young woman from a college near her home。孫女住在加拿大，不是老師。
31How long does Grandmother study every night?
AHalf an hour
BTwo hours
CThree hours
DSeven hours
✔ 正確答案：(B)　Two hours
中譯奶奶每天晚上讀多久？
解析studies for two hours every night，每晚讀兩個小時。seventy 是她的年齡，不是時間。
32What can Grandmother do now?
AWrite short letters in English
BTeach English at school
CLive in Canada
DDrive a car to school
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Write short letters in English
中譯奶奶現在會做什麼？
解析Now she can write short letters to her granddaughter in Canada，她現在能用英文寫短信。

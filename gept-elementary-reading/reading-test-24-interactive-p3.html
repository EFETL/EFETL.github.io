AD
Star Music Shop — Winter Sale

Come to Star Music Shop on North Road!

  • All guitars and drums: 20% off
  • Imported CDs: buy one, get one free

Our shop is never boring. Every Wednesday evening a young band plays here for free. Come early and get a good seat.

Each person can buy only two guitars at this price. The sale ends on December 24. We also sell cheap music books for students. Ask our salesperson about them.

全文中譯

快來北路上的星星音樂行！

  • 所有吉他與鼓：八折
  • 進口 CD：買一送一

我們的店一點也不無聊。每個星期三晚上都有年輕樂團在這裡免費演出。早點來才有好位子。

每人以此價格最多可買兩把吉他。特賣到 12 月 24 日結束。我們也賣便宜的學生音樂書。詳情請詢問我們的銷售員。

Questions 21–24
21What happens at the shop every Wednesday evening?
AThe shop closes early
BA guitar is given away
CAll books are half price
DA band plays for free
✔ 正確答案：(D)　A band plays for free
中譯每個星期三晚上店裡有什麼活動？
解析文中說 Every Wednesday evening a young band plays here for free。其他三項廣告都沒提到。
22Whom should you ask about the music books?
AAnother customer
BThe person at the door
CA salesperson in the shop
DThe band on Wednesday
✔ 正確答案：(C)　A salesperson in the shop
中譯關於音樂書，你應該問誰？
解析文末寫 We also sell cheap music books for students. Ask our salesperson about them.，所以要問店裡的銷售員。樂團只是週三來表演、其他顧客與門口的人文中都沒提到會回答問題。
23What do you get when you buy one imported CD?
AA better seat
BA free guitar
CA music book
DAnother CD for free
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Another CD for free
中譯買一張進口 CD 可以得到什麼？
解析Imported CDs: buy one, get one free，買一張就多送一張。吉他和音樂書都要另外付錢。
24When does the sale end?
AOn December 24
BIn the winter of next year
CEvery Wednesday
DOn Christmas Day
✔ 正確答案：(A)　On December 24
中譯特賣什麼時候結束？
解析The sale ends on December 24。聖誕節是 12 月 25 日，比特賣結束日晚一天，星期三則是樂團表演的日子。
EMAIL
A Thank-You Letter

Dear Mr. Lin,

Thank you for the chance to work in your store this summer. I learned a lot from your workers. They showed me the difference between a good thing and a cheap one.

You also taught me how to talk to new customers. That was not easy at first, but it inspired me.

I hope I can have the same opportunity next year.

Best wishes,
Kevin

全文中譯

林先生您好：

謝謝您讓我今年夏天有機會在您的店裡工作。我從您的員工身上學到很多。他們讓我看見好東西和便宜貨之間的差別。

您也教我怎麼和新客人交談。一開始並不容易，但這件事給了我很大的啟發。

希望明年我還能有同樣的機會。

祝好
凱文

Questions 25–28
25Why does Kevin write this email?
ATo ask for a higher pay
BTo say he is sorry
CTo thank Mr. Lin
DTo buy something in the store
✔ 正確答案：(C)　To thank Mr. Lin
中譯凱文為什麼寫這封電子郵件？
解析信一開頭就說 Thank you for the chance to work in your store，主要是道謝。信中沒有談薪水、購買或道歉。
26What did the workers show Kevin?
AThe best price in town
BThe difference between good and cheap things
CThe way to a big store
DThe way to pack a box
✔ 正確答案：(B)　The difference between good and cheap things
中譯店裡的員工讓凱文看見了什麼？
解析They showed me the difference between a good thing and a cheap one。其他選項信中都沒提到。
27How did Kevin feel about talking to new customers at first?
AIt was not easy
BIt was fun
CIt was too noisy
DIt was boring
✔ 正確答案：(A)　It was not easy
中譯一開始和新客人說話，凱文覺得如何？
解析That was not easy at first，所以一開始覺得不容易，但後來受到啟發。
28What does Kevin want next year?
AA trip to the north
BThe same chance to work there
CA lower price
DA new sweater
✔ 正確答案：(B)　The same chance to work there
中譯凱文明年想要什麼？
解析I hope I can have the same opportunity next year，opportunity 就是 chance（機會）。
ARTICLE
Two Ways to Go Shopping

Many people buy things on the Internet today. It is easy. You just look at the pictures, click, and wait for your order.

But some people still like to go to a store. They can touch the clothes and see the real colors. In general, they also feel safer.

Smart shoppers plan ahead. They make a list before they leave home. They also compare the price of the same thing at two or three shops. Some famous shops send a message when a sale begins.

全文中譯

現在許多人在網路上買東西。這很方便。你只要看看照片、點一下，然後等訂單送來。

但有些人還是喜歡去商店。他們可以摸摸衣服、看到真正的顏色。一般來說，他們也覺得比較安心。

聰明的購物者會提早計畫。他們出門前會先列一張清單。他們也會比較同一樣東西在兩三家店的價格。有些有名的店會在特賣開始時發訊息通知。

Questions 29–32
29What is one good point of buying on the Internet?
AThe quality is always better
BIt is easy
CYou can touch the clothes
DThe color is always right
✔ 正確答案：(B)　It is easy
中譯在網路上買東西有什麼好處？
解析文中說 It is easy，只要看照片、點一下就好。摸衣服和看到真正顏色是去商店的好處。
30Why do some people still go to a store?
ATo meet famous people
BTo pay a lower price
CTo get a free gift
DTo touch things and see real colors
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To touch things and see real colors
中譯為什麼有些人還是會去商店？
解析They can touch the clothes and see the real colors。文中沒提到名人或免費禮物。
31What do smart shoppers do before they leave home?
AThey eat first
BThey call the shop
CThey make a list
DThey pack a bag
✔ 正確答案：(C)　They make a list
中譯聰明的購物者出門前會做什麼？
解析They make a list before they leave home。打包、吃東西、打電話都不是文中說的。
32What do some famous shops do?
AThey give away free water
BThey send a message when a sale begins
CThey open only on Wednesday
DThey sell imported CDs
✔ 正確答案：(B)　They send a message when a sale begins
中譯有些有名的店會做什麼？
解析Some famous shops send a message when a sale begins。其他三項文章都沒有提到。

Questions 33–35
33Which thing sold the most?
ABuns
BToy puppies
CNoodles
DBottled water
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Bottled water
中譯哪一樣東西賣得最多？
解析表格中 Bottled water 賣出 85 個，比 buns 的 60、noodles 的 48、toy puppies 的 12 都多。
34How much is one bun?
ANT$25
BNT$100
CNT$40
DNT$20
✔ 正確答案：(A)　NT$25
中譯一個小圓麵包多少錢？
解析表格 Buns 那一列的 Price 是 NT$25。NT$20 是瓶裝水、NT$40 是麵、NT$100 是小狗玩偶。
35How much money did the noodles make?
ANT$1,200
BNT$480
CNT$4,800
DNT$1,920
✔ 正確答案：(D)　NT$1,920
中譯麵一共賣了多少錢？
解析麵一份 NT$40，賣出 48 份，40×48＝1,920，所以是 NT$1,920。

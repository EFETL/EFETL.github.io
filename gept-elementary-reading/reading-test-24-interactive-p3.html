NOTICE
Messages and a Price List

Ken: I am at the store. Here is what they have for the class party.

SUN MART · Wednesday Sale
Noodles, box of 30 540
Buns, pack of 10 180
Water, box of 24 240
Free bag if you spend over 1,000
Christmas cards and contact lens water also on sale

May: We have 1,000 in total. Thirty students, so we need enough for everyone.

Ken: One box of noodles and one box of water is 780. That leaves 220 for buns.

May: One pack of buns is only ten. Get two packs and we go over 1,000 by 140.

Ken: Then one pack. Not everyone eats buns anyway.

全文中譯

肯：我在店裡。這是他們有的班級派對用品。

陽光超市 · 星期三特賣
麵　30 入一箱　540
包子　10 入一包　180
水　24 入一箱　240
消費滿 1,000 送購物袋
聖誕卡與隱形眼鏡藥水同時特價

梅：我們總共有一千元。三十個學生，所以要夠每個人。

肯：一箱麵加一箱水是 780。剩 220 買包子。

梅：一包包子只有十個。買兩包就超過一千元 140。

肯：那就一包。反正不是每個人都吃包子。

NOTICE
School Fair on Saturday

Saturday · 9:00 to 15:00 · On the playground

Each class will run one shop. You may sell food you made at home, things you no longer need, or a service such as washing a bicycle. In general, think ahead about what people will want. All money goes to the library.

  • Set up your table between 8:00 and 8:45
  • No price may be over 200 dollars
  • Nothing may be sold after 14:30; use the last half hour to clean up

Please do not shout to bring people to your table. A sign is enough. If it rains, the fair moves to the hall, and each class gets a smaller space, so pack only what you need.

  • 請於 8:00 至 8:45 之間布置攤位
  • 單價不得超過 200 元
  • 14:30 之後不得販售，最後半小時用來收拾

EMAIL
About an Order That Never Came

Dear Sir,

On March 2 I made an order on your page for two sweaters, order number 44812. The money left my bank the same day, but nothing has come. That was three weeks ago.

I wrote to your shop twice and got the same short answer both times: the pack has been sent. If it has been sent, could you tell me the day and the name of the company carrying it? With that, I can look for it myself.

If the order was never packed, I would like my money back this week. I have bought from you before without any trouble, so I hope we can fix this quickly.

Sincerely, Hu Ling

全文中譯

敬啟者：

三月二日我在貴網頁下單購買兩件毛衣，訂單編號 44812。款項當天就從我銀行扣除，但東西一直沒到。那已經是三個星期前的事了。

我寫信給貴店兩次，兩次都得到同樣簡短的回覆：包裹已寄出。如果已經寄出，能否告訴我寄出日期與承運公司的名稱？有了這些，我可以自己去追查。

如果訂單根本沒有打包，我希望本週退款。我以前向你們買過東西都沒有問題，所以希望這次能盡快解決。

敬上，胡玲

ARTICLE
What a Low Price Does Not Show

A shirt for 199 dollars looks like a win. Somebody, however, has already paid the difference.

To sell at that price, a shop must buy at a lower one. The maker then pays less for the material, gives the worker less time for each piece, and sends the shirt across the sea in a way that costs little. None of this appears on the sign in the window.

This does not actually mean a cheap thing is wrong. It means the price you see is only the part of the story that reaches you. When we ask how a shop can sell so low, we are not being difficult. We are asking the one question the sign was made to stop.

全文中譯

一件一百九十九元的襯衫看起來像是賺到了。然而已經有人替那個差額付了帳。

要用那個價格賣出，商店就必須用更低的價格買進。製造者於是在材料上少花錢、給工人處理每一件的時間更少，並用成本極低的方式把襯衫運過海洋。這些都不會出現在櫥窗的標示上。

這其實不表示便宜的東西是錯的。它表示你看到的價格，只是這個故事中傳到你面前的那一部分。當我們問一家店怎麼能賣這麼低時，我們並不是在找麻煩。我們問的，正是那張標示被設計來阻止我們問的問題。

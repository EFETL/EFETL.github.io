段落填空 Cloze

共 8 題／2 個段落，每段 4 格
共八題，包括兩個段落，每個段落各含四個空格。每格均有四個選項，請依照文意選出最適合的答案。
Passage 1
The School Music Club

Our school has a music club which meets every Thursday after class, and forty students joined it this year. Some of them play an instrument, while others only sing. Our teacher, Mr. Lin, is a real (11) who played in a big band for ten years before he came to our school. (12), I was so afraid of singing in front of other people that my face turned red every time I opened my mouth. (13) He asked me to sing with two friends first, so that I could get used to the stage. Now I stand in the front row. Next month we will give a show for our parents, (14) we practice twice a week.

全文中譯我們學校有一個音樂社，每週四放學後聚會，今年有四十位學生加入。有些人演奏樂器，有些人只唱歌。我們的林老師是一位真正的音樂家，他來我們學校之前，在一個大樂團演奏了十年。一開始，我非常害怕在別人面前唱歌，每次一開口臉就漲紅。但林老師說，每個人在學習時都會犯錯。他要我先和兩個朋友一起唱，好讓我能習慣站上舞台。現在我站在第一排。下個月我們要為家長表演，所以我們每週練習兩次。
Questions 11–14
11
Afox
Bcomic
Cmusician
Dpurse
✔ 正確答案：(C)　musician
解析「林老師是真正的＿＿」，後面的關係子句 who played in a big band for ten years 說明他的經歷，可見是「音樂家 musician」。comic 漫畫、fox 狐狸、purse 錢包都不是人，也不能用 who 修飾。
12
AFor example
BFinally
CTherefore
DAt first
✔ 正確答案：(D)　At first
解析空格後描述「我」過去很怕唱歌、一開口就臉紅（so…that 句型），後文說 Now I stand in the front row，形成「起初…現在…」的對比，所以用 At first。Therefore 因此——前面講的是老師的經歷，推不出「我很怕唱歌」；Finally 最後、For example 例如都和時間順序不合。

BSo I stopped going to the club after that.
CMr. Lin asked me to leave the music club.
DBut Mr. Lin said everyone makes mistakes when they learn.
✔ 正確答案：(D)　But Mr. Lin said everyone makes mistakes when they learn.
解析前面說「我」怕唱歌、臉會紅，後面說 He asked me to sing with two friends first, so that I could get used to the stage，中間要填林老師鼓勵的話：「但林老師說每個人學習時都會犯錯」。「之後就不去社團了」「林老師要我離開社團」都和後文「現在站第一排」矛盾；「媽媽買鋼琴給我」與上下文無關。

Abut
Bbecause
Cso
Dif
✔ 正確答案：(C)　so
解析「下個月要為家長表演」是原因，「每週練習兩次」是結果，用 so 連接。because 要放在原因前面，填了會變成「因為每週練習兩次，所以要表演」，因果顛倒；but 表轉折、if 表條件都不合。
Passage 2
Learning to Draw

My cousin Kevin is a young artist. He started to draw when he was five, and now he takes an art class every Saturday morning. His teacher, (15) has painted for thirty years, always tells him to look carefully before he picks up a brush. Last month Kevin decided to paint the beach near his house, so he went there every day and watched the sea for hours. (16) Because of this, he made ten small pictures, but none of them felt right to him. (17), after three weeks of hard work, he finished a big (18) in which the water looks so real. His teacher put it near the school entrance so that everyone can view it.

全文中譯我表哥凱文是一位年輕的藝術家。他五歲開始畫畫，現在每週六早上都去上美術課。他的老師已經畫了三十年，總是告訴他拿起畫筆前要先仔細觀察。上個月凱文決定畫他家附近的海灘，所以他每天都去那裡，一看海就是好幾個小時。海水的顏色每個小時都不一樣。因為這樣，他畫了十張小圖，但沒有一張讓他滿意。最後，經過三週的努力，他完成了一幅大畫作，畫裡的海水看起來非常真實。老師把它掛在學校入口附近，讓大家都能欣賞。
Questions 15–18
15
Awho
Bwhat
Cwhen
Dwhich
✔ 正確答案：(A)　who
解析空格引導關係子句補充說明 His teacher（人），且在子句中當主詞（後面直接接 has painted），用 who。which 修飾事物、when 修飾時間、what 不能當關係代名詞修飾先行詞。
16
AThe color of the water was different every hour.
BHe forgot to bring his brushes that day.
CHe did not like the beach at all.
DHis teacher drew the picture for him.
✔ 正確答案：(A)　The color of the water was different every hour.
解析前句說他每天去看海、一看好幾小時，後句 Because of this, he made ten small pictures, but none of them felt right，表示空格必須是「讓他畫了很多張仍不滿意」的原因——「海水顏色每小時都不同」最合理。「他那天忘了帶畫筆」只是一天的事，接不上 Because of this 畫了十張；「他一點也不喜歡海灘」與他決定畫海灘矛盾；「老師替他畫了這幅畫」與後文他自己完成不合。
17
AAt first
BFor example
CIn addition
DFinally
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Finally
解析前面畫了十張都不滿意，after three weeks of hard work 才完成大作，用 Finally 表示「經過一番努力後終於」。At first 起初與時間順序相反；In addition 此外是補充同類的資訊，不合「從失敗到成功」的轉變；For example 例如也不合。

Bpainting

✔ 正確答案：(B)　painting
解析他完成的「大＿＿」，裡面的海水看起來非常真實，還被老師掛在入口附近讓大家欣賞，是一幅「畫作 painting」。lamp 燈、powder 粉末、doll 玩偶都不合。

Messages and a Ticket

Amy: Guess what? My uncle, who works at City Music Hall, gave me two tickets to Nina Chen's show. Do you want to come with me?

Ben: Yeah! She has been my favorite pop music singer for years, so I'd love to go.

Amy: Here is the ticket that my uncle gave me.

NINA CHEN - FINAL SHOW OF THE YEAR
City Music Hall, East Entrance
Saturday, December 20
Doors open 6:30 p.m. / Show starts 7:30 p.m.
Row 9, Seat 14
$1,200

Ben: How do we get there if my dad can't drive us?

Amy: The bus takes forty minutes. I'd like to arrive right when the doors open, because I want to buy her new album before the line gets too long.

19What is the purpose of Amy's first message?
ATo ask Ben to buy a ticket for her
BTo find out Ben's favorite singer
CTo tell Ben about her uncle's new job
DTo invite Ben to go to a show with her
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To invite Ben to go to a show with her
中譯艾咪第一則訊息的目的是什麼？
解析艾咪說叔叔給了她兩張票，接著問 Do you want to come with me?，目的是邀班一起去看表演。票是叔叔送的，不必請班幫她買票；她雖然提到叔叔在音樂廳工作，但沒說是「新工作」，也不是傳訊目的；最喜歡的歌手是班自己說的，不是艾咪要打聽的。

BAt 5:30 p.m.
CAt 6:30 p.m.
DAt 5:50 p.m.
✔ 正確答案：(D)　At 5:50 p.m.

ARTICLE
Music and Art at School

Long ago, Confucius told his students that music was very important, because he believed songs could make people kind and help them get along. Today many schools in Taiwan still see the value of music and art.

At Green Hill School, every class has an art and music program on Friday afternoon, when students may learn an instrument, sing in a group, or draw pictures. In addition, the school plans to record the best songs of the students this year and send them to every family.

Some parents, who are concerned about tests, think students should spend more time on math and English. However, the teachers do not agree, because they believe that music and art play a big role in a happy school life and that happy students often learn better in every class.

全文中譯

很久以前，孔子告訴他的學生音樂非常重要，因為他相信歌曲能讓人變得善良，並幫助人們和睦相處。今天台灣仍有許多學校看重音樂與美術的價值。

在綠丘學校，每班星期五下午都有美術與音樂課程，學生可以學一種樂器、參加合唱或畫畫。此外，學校今年計畫把學生最好的歌曲錄下來，寄給每個家庭。

有些擔心考試的家長認為，學生應該多花時間在數學和英文上。然而，老師們並不同意，因為他們相信音樂和美術在快樂的校園生活中扮演重要角色，而快樂的學生往往在每一科都學得更好。

Passage 1
A Small Cafe on Green Street

There is a small (11) on Green Street. It opens at seven in the morning. Many students go there before school. The (12) there is not expensive, and the bread is fresh every day. Mr. Wang, the owner, makes the bread himself. He gets up at (13) and turns on the big (14) at four o'clock. His wife is the (15) who brings the food to the tables. People like the shop because it feels warm.

全文中譯綠街上有一家小咖啡館。它早上七點開門。很多學生上學前會去那裡。那裡的咖啡不貴，麵包每天都很新鮮。老闆王先生自己做麵包。他黎明就起床，四點就打開那台大烤箱。他太太是把食物送到桌上的女服務生。大家喜歡這家店，因為它讓人感覺溫暖。
Questions 11–15
11
Acafe
Bdrawer
Cdinosaur
Dpillow
✔ 正確答案：(A)　cafe
解析賣咖啡和麵包的店是「咖啡館 cafe」。pillow 枕頭、drawer 抽屜、dinosaur 恐龍都不是店。
12
Afire
Bpen
Ccoffee
Dsign
✔ 正確答案：(C)　coffee
解析和麵包並列、在咖啡館賣的是「咖啡 coffee」。pen 筆、fire 火、sign 標示都不合。
13
Achoice
Bdawn
Cinterview
Dfavor
✔ 正確答案：(B)　dawn
解析四點就開始工作，表示他「黎明 dawn」就起床。choice 選擇、favor 恩惠、interview 面談都不是時間。
14
Aoven
Bteapot
Cmenu
Dsaucer
✔ 正確答案：(A)　oven
解析做麵包要用大「烤箱 oven」。teapot 茶壺、saucer 茶碟、menu 菜單都不能烤麵包。
15
Acarrot
Bwaiter
Cwaitress
Dcook
✔ 正確答案：(C)　waitress
解析送食物到桌上的女性是「女服務生 waitress」。waiter 是男服務生，與 His wife 不合；cook 是廚師、carrot 是胡蘿蔔。
Passage 2
Cooking with Grandmother

Every Saturday I cook with my grandmother in her (16). Last week we made fried rice. First she washed the rice. Then I cut a (17) and a tomato into small pieces. She let me (18) the eggs in hot oil. The kitchen smelled wonderful. After dinner we had ice cream for (19). Grandmother said my cooking was getting better. I felt happy, and I hope she will (20) me again next week.

全文中譯每個星期六我都和奶奶在她的廚房裡做菜。上星期我們做了炒飯。她先把米洗好。接著我把一根胡蘿蔔和一顆番茄切成小塊。她讓我把蛋放進熱油裡炒。廚房聞起來好香。晚餐後我們吃冰淇淋當甜點。奶奶說我的廚藝越來越好了。我很開心，希望下星期她會再教我。
Questions 16–20
16
Adining room
Bkitchen
Cbar
Dcafeteria
✔ 正確答案：(B)　kitchen
解析做菜的地方是「廚房 kitchen」。dining room 是吃飯的飯廳、cafeteria 自助餐廳、bar 吧檯都不是家裡做菜的地方。
17
Apen
Bpillow
Ccarrot
Dsaucer
✔ 正確答案：(C)　carrot
解析和番茄並列、可以切塊的是「胡蘿蔔 carrot」。saucer 茶碟、pen 筆、pillow 枕頭都不能吃。
18
Atoast
Bbake
Cfry
Ddrink
✔ 正確答案：(C)　fry
解析in hot oil（在熱油裡）要用「炒、炸 fry」。bake 是用烤箱烤、toast 是烤吐司、drink 是喝。
19
Adessert
Bbeer
Ctea
Dwine
✔ 正確答案：(A)　dessert
解析飯後吃冰淇淋，冰淇淋是「甜點 dessert」。beer 啤酒、wine 酒、tea 茶都是飲料。
20
Aoperate
Bdeny
Csearch
Dteach
✔ 正確答案：(D)　teach
解析希望奶奶下週再「教 teach」自己。deny 否認、search 搜尋、operate 操作都不合。

ARTICLE
Big Animals in the Sea

Whales are the biggest animals in the world. A blue whale can be thirty meters long. It is not a fish, so it must come up for air.

Sharks are fish. They have strong teeth and can smell blood from far away. Most sharks do not eat people, but people kill many sharks every year for their meat.

Scientists remind us that the sea is their home. If we throw trash into the water, these animals will be in trouble.

全文中譯

鯨魚是世界上最大的動物。一隻藍鯨可以長達三十公尺。牠不是魚，所以必須浮上來呼吸空氣。

鯊魚是魚類。牠們有強壯的牙齒，可以聞到很遠的血腥味。大多數鯊魚不吃人，但人類每年為了魚肉殺死許多鯊魚。

科學家提醒我們，大海是牠們的家。如果我們把垃圾丟進水裡，這些動物就會有麻煩。

Questions 21–24
21Why must a whale come up above the water?
ATo find sharks
BTo look for people

23What do people do to sharks?

中譯人類對鯊魚做了什麼？

24What do scientists want people to do?
AEat more fish
BStop throwing trash into the sea

DSwim with whales

EMAIL
A Letter About a Sick Rabbit

Dear Ms. Wang,

My family flew to England two weeks ago, and my grandmother took care of my rabbit for me. When we came back, we found that his ears were red and he was not eating anything.

We took him to the animal hospital below your office. The doctor said he just felt cold, so we bought a small heater for his box. Now he is eating and running again.

Thank you for telling us about that hospital.

Kelly

全文中譯

王小姐您好：

我們家兩星期前飛去英格蘭，我的兔子由奶奶幫我照顧。回來以後，我們發現牠的耳朵紅紅的，而且什麼都不吃。

我們帶牠去您辦公室樓下的動物醫院。醫生說牠只是覺得冷，所以我們買了一台小暖氣機放在牠的箱子旁邊。現在牠又會吃、又會跑了。

謝謝您告訴我們那家醫院。

凱莉

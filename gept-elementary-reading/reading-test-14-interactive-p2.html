段落填空 Cloze

共 8 題／2 個段落，每段 4 格
請閱讀段落，並由四個選項中選出最適合每個空格的答案。
Passage 1
The Kitchen Drawer

Every home has one drawer that nobody can explain. In ours there is a rope, half a candle, three keys, and a needle that has been there since I was born. My mother says she will clean it out every spring. (11) Last year I tried to help her. I held up an old tool and asked what it was for, (12) she took it from my hand and put it back at once. She said we might need it one day. (13) that drawer is the only place in the house where nothing is ever thrown away. (14), I have started my own drawer in my room.

全文中譯每個家裡都有一個沒人說得清楚的抽屜。我們家那個裡面有一條繩子、半根蠟燭、三把鑰匙，還有一根我出生以來就在那裡的針。媽媽說她每年春天都要清理它。但她從來沒有真的清過。去年我想幫她。我拿起一個舊工具問它是做什麼用的，但她立刻把它從我手上拿走放回去。她說我們哪天可能會用到。也許那個抽屜是家裡唯一什麼都不會被丟掉的地方。說真的，我自己房間裡也開始有一個這樣的抽屜了。
Questions 11–14
11
ABut she has never really done it.
BSo the drawer is empty now.
CShe throws away one item each day.
DNobody opens that drawer.
✔ 正確答案：(A)　But she has never really done it.
解析空格前說「每年春天都要清理」，空格後描述抽屜仍然滿滿的、東西被放回去，所以要填「但她從來沒真的清過」。B、C 都表示已清理，D 與下文「我拿起工具」矛盾。

Aso
Bbecause
Cif
Dbut
✔ 正確答案：(D)　but
解析「我問它做什麼用」與「她立刻拿走放回去」形成轉折，用 but。
13
APerhaps
BUsually

DHardly
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Perhaps
解析這是作者的推測，用 Perhaps。Hardly 幾乎不、Usually 通常、Already 已經都不能引出推測。
14
AAt first
BHowever
CTo be honest
DFor example
✔ 正確答案：(C)　To be honest
解析最後一句坦承自己也開始這麼做，用 To be honest。At first 指最初、However 表轉折、For example 是舉例。
Passage 2
A Public Kitchen in Our Town

Last year a group of neighbors opened a public kitchen beside the bookstore on Middle Road. Anyone may use the stove, the wok, and the big pot there, but each person must wash every plate and glass before leaving. (15) On Sunday morning it is full of people who do not usually cook at home. An old man from Singapore, who cooks everything himself, teaches others how to make soup, (16) he never writes down the amounts. (17), three neighbors have opened small food shops of their own. The kitchen has no prize and no sign, (18) everyone in town knows where it is.

全文中譯去年一群鄰居在中路書店旁開了一間公共廚房。任何人都可以使用那裡的爐子、炒鍋和大鍋，但每個人離開前都必須把每個盤子和杯子洗乾淨。這個想法比任何人預期的更受歡迎。星期天早上，那裡擠滿了平常不在家下廚的人。一位凡事都自己下廚的新加坡老先生教別人煮湯，但他從不寫下份量。結果，已經有三位鄰居自己開了小吃店。這間廚房沒有獎項也沒有招牌，但鎮上每個人都知道它在哪裡。
Questions 15–18
15
ANobody has used the stove yet.
BThe idea grew more popular than anyone expected.
CThe kitchen closed after two weeks.
DOnly children are allowed to enter.
✔ 正確答案：(B)　The idea grew more popular than anyone expected.
解析空格後說星期天擠滿了人、還有人因此開店，所以要填「這個想法比預期更受歡迎」。B、C、D 都與後文的熱鬧景象矛盾。

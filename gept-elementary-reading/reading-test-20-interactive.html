Passage 1
The House with Two Gates

My grandmother's house has two gates. The front one opens to the street and is used by guests; the back one opens to a path behind the kitchen. (11) For years I thought this was strange. Then one cold afternoon a fisherman came to the back gate with a basket of fish and some bananas. He did not knock; he called her name, and she answered from inside. I realized that the two gates were not about doors at all, (12) about who needs to be welcomed and who is already inside. (13) my grandmother has moved to an apartment with one door and a bell. She says she is happy there, (14) she still leaves the kitchen window open every morning.

全文中譯我奶奶的房子有兩道門。前門通向街道，是客人走的；後門通向廚房後面一條小路。家人從來不用前門。有好幾年我覺得這很奇怪。後來在一個寒冷的下午，一位漁夫提著一籃魚和一些香蕉來到後門。他沒有敲門，只是喊了她的名字，而她在裡面應了聲。我明白了那兩道門根本不是關於門，而是關於誰需要被迎接、誰已經在家裡面。如今我奶奶搬到了一間只有一扇門和門鈴的公寓。她說她在那裡很開心，但她每天早上仍然把廚房的窗戶打開。

Passage 2
Why Rooms Have Corners

A room is really just air with walls around it, yet the shape of those walls changes how we feel and even what we can achieve in a day. (15) People in a room with a high roof think of larger ideas, while a low room helps them work on small careful tasks. Light matters even more. A window on the east side wakes a person in a soft way, (16) the same room facing west can feel hot and heavy by evening. (17), people who build houses in this town have long put bedrooms on the east and kitchens on the north. Few families today can choose the direction of their block, (18) even a curtain moved by one meter can change a whole afternoon.

全文中譯房間其實只是被牆圍起來的空氣，然而那些牆的形狀會改變我們的感受，甚至影響我們一天能完成什麼。研究人員多年前就發現了這件事。待在挑高房間裡的人會想到比較大的想法，而低矮的房間則有助於處理細小而需要專注的工作。光線更重要。東邊的窗戶會溫和地把人喚醒，但同一個房間若朝西，到了傍晚就會顯得又熱又悶。因此，這個鎮上蓋房子的人長久以來都把臥室設在東邊、廚房設在北邊。今天很少有家庭能選擇自己那棟樓的座向，但即使只是把窗簾移動一公尺，也可能改變整個下午。

ARTICLE
The Room That Makes the Family

Ask people to draw their childhood home and most will draw one room, not the whole house. It is usually the room where food was made, and interest in this idea has grown in recent years.

For a long time, houses in this part of the world were built around the kitchen, and that was where family life took place. Everyone passed through it, so nobody could be alone for long. Later, flats were built with small kitchens at the back and a large room for guests at the front. The family ended up at the edge of its own home.

Now some people who build houses are turning back. They open the kitchen to the living room and put the table in the middle, so a family can share a pizza without anyone leaving the room. It is not because cooking has changed. It is because people discovered that a family needs one room that nobody can walk past.

全文中譯

請人畫出童年的家，多數人畫的是一個房間，而不是整棟房子。那通常是做飯的房間，而近年來人們對這個想法的興趣也不斷增加。

長久以來，我們這個地方的房子是圍著廚房蓋的，家庭生活也在那裡發生。每個人都會經過那裡，所以沒有人能獨處太久。後來的公寓把小廚房放在後面，把待客的大房間放在前面。家人反而被推到自己家的邊緣。

現在有些蓋房子的人開始走回頭路。他們把廚房與客廳打通，把餐桌放在中間，讓一家人可以一起吃披薩而不必有人離開。這不是因為烹飪改變了，而是因為人們發現，一個家需要一個沒有人能夠直接走過去的房間。

Different Places on the Earth

There are many different kinds of places on Earth. A 16 gets very little rain, and only a few plants can live there. A 17 is full of trees, and it is often dark and wet inside. A 18 is flat land, and farmers like to grow rice or corn on it. In the middle of the 19, some small 20 are only a few kilometers wide. People there live on fish because they cannot grow much food.

全文中譯地球上有許多不同的地方。沙漠幾乎不下雨，只有少數植物能活在那裡。森林裡滿是樹木，裡面常常又暗又濕。平原是平坦的土地，農夫喜歡在上面種稻或玉米。在海洋中央，有些小島只有幾公里寬。那裡的人靠魚維生，因為他們種不出多少食物。

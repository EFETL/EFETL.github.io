A Great Woman Scientist

Marie Curie was one of the greatest scientists in the world. She was born in Poland, but she studied physics and chemistry in France.

At that time, few women could study science. She often worked late in a cold room without enough food. In the end she found two new chemical elements.

Curie won two big prizes. She always said that hard work was more important than luck.

註chemistry 化學
全文中譯

瑪麗．居禮是世界上最偉大的科學家之一。她在波蘭出生，卻在法國研讀物理和化學。

在那個年代，很少有女性能讀科學。她常常在寒冷的房間裡工作到很晚，也吃不飽。最後她發現了兩種新的化學元素。

居禮贏得了兩座大獎。她總是說，努力比運氣更重要。

NOTICE
Rules of the Science Room

Dear students, please read these rules before class.

  • Do not eat or drink here. Some chemicals are not safe.
  • Wear your safety glasses when you use fire.
  • Do not put anything hot into the sink.
  • Tell the teacher at once if you cut your hand.

Put all the tools back on the main table before you leave. The last student must turn off the air conditioner and close the door. The room is also closed on New Year's Eve.

全文中譯

親愛的同學，上課前請先閱讀以下規定。

  • 不可以在這裡吃東西或喝東西，有些化學藥品並不安全。
  • 使用火的時候要戴上護目鏡。
  • 不要把任何燙的東西放進水槽。
  • 如果割傷手，請馬上告訴老師。

離開前請把所有工具放回主桌上。最後一位學生必須關掉冷氣並把門關好。除夕當天教室也不開放。

Passage 1
The Small Theater on River Road

The theater on River Road has ninety seats and one screen. It shows one movie twice a day, at four and at seven, and a little bird lives above the door. The man who sells the tickets also starts the machine and sweeps the floor. (11) A large new theater opened three streets away, with twelve screens and warm seats. Everybody said the small one would close within a year. It did not. (12) people who go there say they go for something the big one cannot sell: a room dark enough that ninety strangers laugh at the same second. (13), the owner now shows old films on Sunday mornings. The seats are still hard, (14) the room is always full.

全文中譯河路上那家戲院有九十個座位、一個銀幕。每天放兩場，四點和七點各一場，門上方還住著一隻小鳥。賣票的人同時也負責開機器和掃地。兩年前情況變了。三條街外開了一家大型新戲院，有十二個廳和溫暖的座椅。大家都說那家小戲院一年內就會關門。它沒有。相反地，去那裡的人說他們是為了大戲院賣不出的東西而去：一個夠暗的房間，讓九十個陌生人在同一秒笑出來。因此，老闆現在星期天早上放老片。椅子還是很硬，但那個房間總是滿的。

Passage 2
Why a Story Needs a Room

A film can be watched on a phone on a bus, and millions are. (15) A story works on us partly because we cannot leave it. In a dark room with no clock, two hours pass as the film decides, not as we decide. At home the same film is cut into pieces by a bell at the door and a message, (16) the pieces never add up to the same thing. This is not about big screens. (17), it is about giving one story all of our attention for once. Nobody needs a theater to do that, (18) a theater makes it hard to do anything else.

全文中譯一部電影可以在公車上用手機看，而且數以百萬的人正是這樣看的。但有些東西在途中消失了。一個故事之所以能打動我們，部分原因是我們無法離開它。在一個沒有時鐘的暗房裡，兩個小時是由電影決定怎麼過，而不是由我們決定。在家裡，同一部電影被門鈴和訊息切成碎片，而那些碎片永遠拼不回同一個東西。這跟大銀幕無關。而是關於：難得一次把全部的注意力給一個故事。沒有人非得靠戲院才能做到，但戲院讓你很難去做別的事。

ARTICLE
Why We Watch People Act

Every country in the world has some form of drama, and no country had to be taught it. Children begin to act out other people before they can read.

One reason may be that watching a person act out a life is a safe way to feel something real. In two hours we can lose a home, forgive a brother, and stand in a court, and then walk out into the same evening we left.

A film also lets us look at a face longer than we ever may in life. We watch somebody be afraid for a full minute without turning away. Perhaps that is the true work of drama: it gives us practice at paying attention to another person, which is the one skill no machine has learned to do for us.

全文中譯

世界上每個國家都有某種形式的戲劇，而且沒有一個國家是被教會的。孩子在會讀字之前，就已經開始扮演別人。

一個原因也許是：看著一個人演出另一段人生，是感受真實情緒的安全方式。在兩個小時裡，我們可以失去一個家、原諒一個兄弟、站上法庭，然後走出去，回到自己離開時的同一個傍晚。

電影也讓我們能比現實中更久地注視一張臉。我們可以整整一分鐘看著某個人害怕，而不必移開視線。也許那才是戲劇真正的功用：它讓我們練習專注在另一個人身上，而這正是機器至今仍無法替我們做的一件事。

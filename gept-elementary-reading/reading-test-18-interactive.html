Passage 1
The Cat That Chose Us

We did not decide to keep a cat; the cat decided for us. One rainy night a thin gray animal walked into our garden and sat under the table as if it had always lived there. My father said we could not feed it. (11) In the morning there was an empty dish by the door, and nobody said a word about who filled it. We called her Bean, because she was small and round. She never learned to come when we called, (12) she always knew when someone in the house was sad. (13) we understand that we are not really her owners. We have never had to say goodbye to her; she is simply an animal who has agreed to stay, (14) that feels like enough.

全文中譯我們並沒有決定要養貓，是貓替我們決定的。一個下雨的夜晚，一隻瘦瘦的灰色動物走進我們的花園，坐在桌子底下，好像牠一直都住在那裡。我爸爸說我們不能餵牠。那天晚上他自己卻起床了兩次。早上門邊有一個空盤子，而沒有人提起是誰裝的。我們叫牠豆子，因為牠又小又圓。牠從來沒學會叫牠就過來，但牠總是知道家裡誰心情不好。過了這麼多年，我們明白我們並不是牠真正的主人。我們從來不必和牠說再見；牠只是一隻同意留下來的動物，而那樣就夠了。

Passage 2
Small Animals, Big Work

When people speak about saving animals, they usually think of the lion or the bear. Few think about the bee. (15) About one third of the food on your table needs an insect to help the flower make fruit. No bees, no apples; no bees, no beans, and no tofu. The latest count shows that bees have dropped in many countries, (16) farmers use strong chemicals and cut down the wild flowers along field lines. Some cities now leave the grass in one section of every park tall. (17), bees have somewhere to rest in the middle of the town. It takes little effort, (18) it may matter more than a new zoo.

全文中譯當人們談到拯救動物時，通常想到的是獅子或熊。很少有人想到蜜蜂。然而蜜蜂可能是我們最需要的。你餐桌上大約三分之一的食物，需要昆蟲幫助花朵結成果實。沒有蜜蜂就沒有蘋果，沒有蜜蜂就沒有豆子，也沒有豆腐。最新的統計顯示許多國家的蜜蜂數量下降，因為農民使用強效藥劑，並砍掉田邊的野花。現在有些城市讓公園裡一區的草長高。這樣一來，蜜蜂在市中心也有地方可以歇息。這只需要很少的力氣，但它的意義可能比一座新動物園更大。

ARTICLE
The Animals We Do Not Count

Ask a child to name an animal in danger, and you will hear lion, whale, or bear. You will not hear the name of an ant, and yet more than half of all animals on this planet are insects.

This matters because insects do work that nothing else can do. They help flowers make fruit, they clean away dead leaves, and they feed the birds and frogs above them. Take them away, and the line of life above them falls too.

Insects are also simple to help. A garden with a few wild corners, a light turned off at night, a section of grass left tall: none of this costs money, and you do not have to be a genius to do such things. The hardest part is not the effort. It is learning to care about an animal we have been taught to brush away.

全文中譯

請一個孩子說出一種瀕危動物，你會聽到獅子、鯨魚或熊。你不會聽到螞蟻的名字，然而這個星球上超過一半的動物是昆蟲。

這件事很重要，因為昆蟲做的是別的生物做不到的工作。牠們幫助花朵結果、清除落葉，也餵養牠們上方的鳥類與青蛙。把牠們拿走，上面整條生命線也會崩塌。

昆蟲也很容易幫。一座留有幾處野生角落的花園、夜裡關掉的一盞燈、一區任其長高的草地：這些都不花錢，而且做這些事也不必是天才。最難的不是力氣，而是學會去在乎一種我們從小被教著揮開的動物。

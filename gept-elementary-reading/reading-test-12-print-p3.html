ARTICLE
Study Less, Learn More

Many students study all night before an exam. They think more hours mean better grades. However, this is not always true.

Doctors say sleep is important for the brain. A tired student cannot remember new lessons well. Many students struggle with math, but the answer is not staying up late. It is better to learn a little every day.

Fresh air helps, too. Open the window, or take a short walk outside. The aim of study is a strong mind, not a long night.

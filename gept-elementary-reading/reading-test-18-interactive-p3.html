NOTICE
Messages and a Zoo Ticket

Sam: Hey, our class is going downtown to the zoo on Friday. Here is my ticket.

CITY ZOO · Student Group
Friday, April 12
Enter before 10:00 · East Gate
Bear House closed for repair
Student 60 · Teacher free

Joy: Only sixty dollars? That is cheap. Which animal do you want to see?

Sam: The monkeys, and I hope the insect room is open. I want a photo of a butterfly.

Joy: Bring water. Last time we walked for four hours and I had no rest at all.

全文中譯

山姆：嘿，我們班星期五要到市區的動物園。這是我的票。

市立動物園 · 學生團體
四月十二日星期五
10:00 前入園 · 東門
熊館整修中休館
學生 60 · 教師免費

喬伊：只要六十元？好便宜。你想看哪種動物？

山姆：猴子，希望昆蟲館有開。我想拍一張蝴蝶的照片。

喬伊：記得帶水。上次我們走了四小時，我完全沒有休息到。

BThe monkey area
CThe Bear House
DThe East Gate

解析票上寫 Bear House closed for repair，熊館休館中。
20How much will a teacher pay?
ANothing
BSixty dollars
CHalf of sixty
DTen dollars
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Nothing
中譯老師要付多少錢？
解析票上寫 Teacher free，老師免費。六十元是學生票價。
21Why does Joy tell Sam to bring water?
AThe zoo sells no drinks
BThe weather will be hot
CThe walk last time was long and hard
DThe insect room is far away
✔ 正確答案：(C)　The walk last time was long and hard
中譯喬伊為什麼要山姆帶水？
解析Last time we walked for four hours and I had no rest at all，是根據上次的經驗提醒。其他三項文中都沒提到。
NOTICE
Rules for the Animal Corner

Welcome to the Animal Corner behind Room 12.

  • Wash your hands before and after you touch any animal.
  • Feed the rabbits only the food in the green box.
  • Do not open the door of the mouse house by yourself.

Two students look after the corner each week. Their names are on the line beside the door. If an animal looks sick, tell a teacher at once; do not try to help it yourself.

The corner is open at noon and after school, but not in the morning, because the animals rest then. Students who come in the morning will be asked to leave. On Friday we clean everything, so the corner closes at three.

全文中譯

歡迎來到 12 教室後面的動物角。

  • 觸摸任何動物前後都要洗手。
  • 只能用綠色箱子裡的食物餵兔子。
  • 不可以自己打開鼠屋的門。

每週由兩位學生負責照顧動物角，名字寫在門邊那一行上。如果動物看起來生病，請立刻告訴老師，不要自己想辦法處理。

動物角在中午和放學後開放，早上不開放，因為動物那時在休息。早上來的學生會被請離開。星期五要大掃除，所以三點就關閉。

EMAIL
A Question about a Lost Cat

Dear Mrs. Lee,

I live in the house at the corner of Bean Road, two doors from yours. Yesterday evening I saw a gray cat with a short tail under my car. It would not come to me, and it left before I could look closely.

I remember that your cat is gray. Is yours still at home? If it is missing, I can leave some food in my garden tonight and send you a photo if it comes back. Please do not worry too much; cats certainly stay away for a day or two sometimes and then walk in as if nothing has happened.

My phone is on all evening. Just knock if you would rather talk.

Chen Ping

全文中譯

親愛的李太太：

我住在豆路轉角那棟房子，和您家隔兩戶。昨天傍晚我看到一隻短尾巴的灰貓在我車底下。牠不肯靠近我，我還來不及看清楚牠就走了。

我記得您的貓是灰色的。您的貓還在家嗎？如果牠不見了，我今晚可以在花園裡放一些食物，如果牠回來我會傳照片給您。請不要太擔心；貓確實有時會在外面待個一兩天，然後就像沒事一樣走回來。

我的電話整晚都開著。您想當面談的話，直接來敲門就好。

陳萍

ARTICLE
The Animals We Do Not Count

Ask a child to name an animal in danger, and you will hear lion, whale, or bear. You will not hear the name of an ant, and yet more than half of all animals on this planet are insects.

This matters because insects do work that nothing else can do. They help flowers make fruit, they clean away dead leaves, and they feed the birds and frogs above them. Take them away, and the line of life above them falls too.

Insects are also simple to help. A garden with a few wild corners, a light turned off at night, a section of grass left tall: none of this costs money, and you do not have to be a genius to do such things. The hardest part is not the effort. It is learning to care about an animal we have been taught to brush away.

全文中譯

請一個孩子說出一種瀕危動物，你會聽到獅子、鯨魚或熊。你不會聽到螞蟻的名字，然而這個星球上超過一半的動物是昆蟲。

這件事很重要，因為昆蟲做的是別的生物做不到的工作。牠們幫助花朵結果、清除落葉，也餵養牠們上方的鳥類與青蛙。把牠們拿走，上面整條生命線也會崩塌。

昆蟲也很容易幫。一座留有幾處野生角落的花園、夜裡關掉的一盞燈、一區任其長高的草地：這些都不花錢，而且做這些事也不必是天才。最難的不是力氣，而是學會去在乎一種我們從小被教著揮開的動物。

Questions 28–30
28What is the main idea of this article?
AGardens should have no wild corners
BLions and whales need more help than insects
CChildren should learn the names of all animals
DInsects are simple to help but simple to forget
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Insects are simple to help but simple to forget
中譯這篇文章的主旨是什麼？
解析文章指出昆蟲佔動物一半以上、做著無可取代的工作，而且容易幫助，難的是我們願不願意在乎。

CGardens will be simple to keep
DBirds will eat more leaves
✔ 正確答案：(A)　The animals above them will also be in danger
中譯根據作者，如果昆蟲消失會發生什麼事？
解析they feed the birds and frogs above them. Take them away, and the line of life above them falls too。
30Which of these does the writer suggest?
ACutting all the grass short
BKeeping insects in a box
CMoving insects to a zoo
DTurning off a light at night
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Turning off a light at night

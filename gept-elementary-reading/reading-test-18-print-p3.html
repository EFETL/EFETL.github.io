Rules of the City Butterfly Garden

Welcome to the City Butterfly Garden. Please read these rules before you go in.

  • Do not touch the insects with your hands.
  • Please keep quiet. Loud voices make the butterflies leave.
  • Food and drinks are not allowed inside.
  • Children under six must come with a parent.

The garden is open from 9:00 to 17:00 every day except Monday. The latest time to go in is 16:30.

ARTICLE
Small Animals, Big Work

Ants are small, but they never work alone. Thousands of ants live together deep under the ground. They walk in long lines and carry food home.

Bees work as a team, too. They fly from flower to flower all day. Without bees, many plants cannot make seeds or fruit.

Some people think insects are dirty and want to kill all of them. Scientists do not agree. They say we should understand these small animals and learn from their hard work.

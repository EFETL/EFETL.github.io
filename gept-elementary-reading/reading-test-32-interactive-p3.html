EMAIL
Waiting at the Airport

Hi Peter,

I am at the airport now. My plane to Hong Kong leaves in two hours, so I have time to write.

I am a little nervous. This is my first long flight, and I am going alone. My family talked about it for a long time, but in the end they let me go. My mother put some chocolate and a book in my bag.

My uncle will meet me outside the airport tomorrow morning. He is very helpful, and he speaks good English.

I will send you photos later. Please water my plant!

David

全文中譯

嗨，彼得：

我現在在機場。我飛往香港的班機兩小時後起飛，所以有時間寫信。

我有點緊張。這是我第一次長途飛行，而且是自己一個人去。我家人討論了很久，最後還是讓我去了。我媽媽在我的包包裡放了一些巧克力和一本書。

我叔叔明天早上會在機場外面接我。他很熱心，英文也說得很好。

我之後會傳照片給你。請幫我澆花！

ARTICLE
Ships and Planes

Ships are slow, but they are still very important. Nine out of ten things we buy travel by ship. Big ships carry oil, cars, corn and even doughnuts across the ocean.

A plane is much faster. It can fly from Taipei to Hong Kong in less than two hours. Large planes can also carry things, but the cost is high.

Therefore, people send letters and small things by plane, and heavy things by ship. Both of them help us sell things to other countries and buy things from far away.

全文中譯

船很慢，但仍然非常重要。我們買的東西中，每十樣就有九樣是靠船運送的。大船載著油、汽車、玉米，甚至甜甜圈橫越海洋。

飛機快得多。它可以在兩小時內從台北飛到香港。大型飛機也能載貨，但費用很高。

因此，人們用飛機寄信和小東西，用船運送重的東西。兩者都幫助我們把東西賣到其他國家，也買到遠方的商品。

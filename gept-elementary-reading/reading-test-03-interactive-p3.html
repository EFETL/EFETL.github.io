A Letter to a Pen Pal

Dear Mia,

How are you? Our new term started two weeks ago. This year I have five classes every weekday and one test every month.

My older sister just went to college, so our room is quiet now. I miss her, but she comes home on weekends.

Yours,
Ruby

ARTICLE
How Long Is a Month?

Long ago, people had no clocks. They looked at the moon to measure time. The moon changes its shape in about thirty days, and this period became our month.

People also watched the sun. The Earth goes around the sun in about 365 days, and that is the length of a year.

In the past, farmers used these ideas to plan their work. Today we still use them every day.

全文中譯

很久以前，人們沒有時鐘。他們看月亮來測量時間。月亮大約三十天改變一次形狀，這段期間就成了我們的一個月。

人們也觀察太陽。地球大約 365 天繞太陽一圈，這就是一年的長度。

在過去，農夫用這些概念來安排工作。今天我們每天仍然在使用它們。

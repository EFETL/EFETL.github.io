Passage 1
My Weekend Diary

I started to keep a (11) last year, after my teacher told us that writing a little every day would help our English. On weekdays I am so busy with school and homework that I only write a few lines. On weekends, when I have more time, I can fill a whole page. Last Saturday my father and I went to the zoo, where we stayed for three hours and watched a man feed the birds. (12) Sunday, however, was quiet. I read a book beside the window (13) it rained all day. Sometimes I also draw small pictures in my diary, which help me remember little happy things. (14), I will keep writing it for many years.

全文中譯我去年開始寫日記，因為老師告訴我們，每天寫一點會對英文有幫助。平日我忙著上學和寫功課，忙到只能寫幾行。週末比較有空閒時間，我可以寫滿一整頁。上星期六我和爸爸去動物園，我們在那裡待了三個小時，看一位先生餵鳥。那是忙碌又令人興奮的一天。然而，星期天很安靜。因為下了一整天的雨，我就坐在窗邊看書。有時候我也會在日記裡畫些小圖，這些小圖幫助我記住許多快樂的小事。因此，我會繼續寫很多年。

Passage 2
A Train Trip with My Class

Last month our class took a train to Hualien for two days. We waited on (15) two for twenty minutes because the train was late, and some of us began to complain. (16), once it left, it ran at such a high speed that we still arrived before noon. During our brief stay, we visited a small museum and walked on the beach, where the sound of the sea was louder than our voices. Our teacher, (17) grandfather was born in Hualien, told us many stories about the past of this old town. (18) On the way home, everyone was sleepy but happy, and I knew I would remember this trip for many years.

全文中譯上個月我們班搭火車去花蓮兩天。因為火車誤點，我們在第二月台等了二十分鐘，有些同學開始抱怨。不過，火車一開動就跑得非常快，我們還是在中午前就到了。在那裡短暫停留的期間，我們參觀了一間小博物館，也在海灘上散步，那裡海浪的聲音比我們說話的聲音還大。我們的老師——他的祖父在花蓮出生——跟我們說了許多這座老城過去的故事。有些同學甚至買了小禮物給家人。回家的路上，大家都很睏但很開心，我知道我會把這趟旅行記很多年。

ARTICLE
How Long Is a Month?

Long ago, before people had clocks or calendars, they looked up at the sky to measure time. The moon, which seems to change its shape every night, goes from a thin line to a full circle and back again in about thirty days. We call this period a month.

People also watched the sun, whose path across the sky changes with the seasons. The earth goes around the sun in about 365 days, and that is the length of one year. In the past, farmers used the moon and the sun to decide when to plant rice and when to protect their fields from the cold.

Today most people check the time on their phones without thinking about the sky. However, the months and years that we use every day still come from it.

全文中譯

很久以前，在人們還沒有時鐘或月曆的時候，他們抬頭看天空來計算時間。月亮的形狀似乎每晚都在變，它從細細的一條線變成滿滿的圓、再變回來，大約要三十天。我們把這段時間稱為一個「月」。

人們也觀察太陽，太陽在天空中走的路線會隨著季節改變。地球繞太陽一圈大約要 365 天，那就是一年的長度。過去，農夫利用月亮和太陽來決定什麼時候種稻、什麼時候要保護田地不受寒害。

現在大部分的人用手機看時間，不會想到天空。然而，我們每天使用的月和年，仍然來自天空。

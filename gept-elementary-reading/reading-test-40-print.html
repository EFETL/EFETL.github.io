Rules of the Science Room

Dear students, please read these rules before class.

  • Do not eat or drink here. Some chemicals are not safe.
  • Wear your safety glasses when you use fire.
  • Do not put anything hot into the sink.
  • Tell the teacher at once if you cut your hand.

Put all the tools back on the main table before you leave. The last student must turn off the air conditioner and close the door. The room is also closed on New Year's Eve.

ARTICLE
Colors and Feelings

Colors can change the way people feel, even when they do not notice it. Many people say that blue is a quiet color which helps them feel calm, but too much blue may also make them feel down.

Red is a strong color that can make people excited, and sometimes it can even make them angry. That is why many fast food restaurants use red walls: people who sit there eat fast and leave soon. In many parts of Asia, red means good luck, so people wear it at weddings and on holidays.

Bright yellow, on the other hand, is like the sun. Doctors say that it can make a dark room feel warm and happy. So the next time you paint your room, think about your emotions before you choose a color.

I Am Sorry

Dear Kevin,

I am writing to say sorry. Last Friday I was mad at you in the classroom, and I said something bad about the picture that you had worked on for a whole week. I was not really angry with you. That morning I got a low grade on my math test, which I had studied hard for, and I felt down all day. I let my anger come out at the wrong person.

When I saw your sad face, I was shocked at myself, because you are my best friend and I do not want to lose you.

Can we eat hamburgers at our usual place after school tomorrow? I will pay this time. If you are still mad at me, I will understand, but please write back any time.

Ted

Stress Less Week

Stress Less Week

Do you often feel nervous before tests, or do you stay up until midnight and then feel sleepy in class? You are not alone. Last term, twelve out of every twenty students in our school said that they had too much stress.

Next week, our school will hold Stress Less Week. Every day at lunch time, students can come to the library and try a new hobby, such as drawing, playing chess, or making paper flowers. On Friday, a teacher from an international school, who has helped many students with this problem, will talk about ways to sleep better.

Here are some tips: Go to bed before eleven, and take a walk after dinner. When you feel afraid or sad, talk to people you trust. Do not keep your feelings inside!

Colors and Feelings

Colors can change how we feel. Many people say blue is a quiet color. It makes them calm, but too much blue can also make them sad.

Red is a strong color. It can make people excited, and sometimes angry. That is why some restaurants use red walls. In some parts of Asia, red also means good luck.

Bright yellow is like the sun. Doctors say it can make a dark room feel warm and happy.

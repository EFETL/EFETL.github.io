NOTICE
Messages and a Ticket

Mom: The bus leaves at 12:40. Please be at the bus stop ten minutes early.

Ken: All right. I have to return a book to the library first.

Mom: Aunt Lisa sent me this.

BALL GAME WEEK — FINAL
Sun Field · Gate 3
Sunday 2:00 p.m.
ROW 12　SEAT 8
STUDENT　$150

Mom: She got two seats behind third base. A famous player from Japan will play this autumn, so the seats sold out fast.

Ken: Great! I will bring my cap and a bottle of water.

全文中譯

媽媽：公車十二點四十分開。請提早十分鐘到公車站。

肯：好。我要先去圖書館還一本書。

媽媽：麗莎阿姨傳了這個給我。

球類週——冠軍賽
太陽球場 · 三號門
星期日 下午 2:00
第 12 排　8 號座
學生票　150 元

媽媽：她拿到兩個三壘後方的位子。有一位日本來的知名選手今年秋天會上場，所以票很快就賣完了。

肯：太好了！我會帶帽子和一瓶水。

Questions 19–21
19Why does Ken's mother send him the picture?
ATo ask him to buy a ticket
BTo remind him to return a book
CTo invite Aunt Lisa to the game
DTo show him the game information
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To show him the game information
中譯肯的媽媽為什麼傳那張圖給他？
解析媽媽傳圖後接著說明座位與選手，可見目的是讓肯知道這場比賽的資訊。票不是要他買（是阿姨給的），還書是肯自己提的，阿姨也不是被邀請的對象。
20What time should Ken be at the bus stop?
AAt 12:30
BAt 2:00
CAt 12:50
DAt 12:40
✔ 正確答案：(A)　At 12:30
中譯肯應該幾點到公車站？
解析公車 12:40 開，媽媽要他提早十分鐘，12:40 減十分鐘是 12:30。12:40 是發車時間，2:00 是比賽時間。
21What does the ticket NOT tell us?
AThe gate number
BThe seat number
CThe day of the game
DThe price for adults
✔ 正確答案：(D)　The price for adults
中譯票上沒有告訴我們什麼？
解析票上只寫 STUDENT $150，沒有大人的票價。星期日、第 12 排 8 號座、三號門票上都有。
NOTICE
Ball Game Week

Ball Game Week is coming, and this semester it will be bigger than ever.

  • Basketball — Monday, on the court behind the library
  • Badminton — Wednesday, in the gym
  • Dodge ball — Friday, on the field

Ball Game Week is always held in the week before the Dragon Boat Festival. All games start at 3:30 p.m. and end before 5:00 p.m. The form outside the PE office indicates how many players each class must send. Please write your names on it before Monday. Students who sign up late cannot join.

Parents are welcome to watch. They may sit under the big trees beside the court, but not behind the white line. We appreciate the help of every parent. Remember to bring a bottle of water, because the weather will be hot.

全文中譯

球類週要來了，這學期規模比以往更大。

  • 籃球——星期一，在圖書館後面的球場
  • 羽球——星期三，在體育館
  • 躲避球——星期五，在操場

球類週固定在端午節前一週舉行。所有比賽下午三點半開始、五點前結束。體育室外面的表格會標明每班必須派出幾名球員。請在星期一前把姓名寫上去。太晚報名的學生不能參加。

歡迎家長觀賽。家長可以坐在球場旁的大樹下，但不可以坐在白線後面。我們感謝每一位家長的協助。記得帶一瓶水，因為天氣會很熱。

EMAIL
An Invitation from Ken

Hi Judy,

I am glad you are back from Taipei. Are you free this Saturday afternoon? My aunt works at the sports center near the mall, and she gave me two free tickets for the badminton court. I have not played since the school game last month, so I am a little afraid that I will lose to you again.

The court opens at two, but it is always full after four, so let us meet at one thirty. If it rains, we can go bowling on the third floor of the mall, where students pay only half price before five.

Please bring a bottle of water. I will bring some rice balls. Our Chinese teacher says I should eat before I play, so I will eat a big lunch. Call me before Friday night.

Ken

全文中譯

嗨，茱蒂：

很高興你從台北回來了。你這星期六下午有空嗎？我阿姨在購物中心附近的運動中心上班，她給了我兩張羽球場的免費入場券。我從上個月校內比賽之後就沒打過了，所以有點怕又會輸給你。

球場兩點開門，但四點以後總是客滿，所以我們一點半碰面吧。如果下雨，我們可以去購物中心三樓打保齡球，學生在五點前只要付半價。

請帶一瓶水。我會帶一些飯糰。我們的國文老師說打球前應該先吃東西，所以我會好好吃頓午餐。星期五晚上前打電話給我。

肯

ARTICLE
Your Body at Play

When you run, jump, or kick a ball, your whole body is working hard. Your heart beats faster so that it can send blood to every part of your body, and your bones slowly grow stronger.

Doctors say that students who exercise for thirty minutes a day sleep better at night and remember more in class the next morning. Exercise also helps people who feel sad, because the brain makes something that brings a happy feeling.

However, more is not always better. If the floor is wet, you may slip and hurt your leg. If you feel pain, you should stop and rest. We must learn to take care of ourselves; a good player knows when to run, and also knows when to sit down.

全文中譯

當你跑步、跳躍或踢球時，全身都在努力運作。你的心臟跳得更快，好把血液送到身體的每個部位，而你的骨頭也會慢慢變得更強壯。

醫生說，每天運動三十分鐘的學生晚上睡得更好，隔天早上在課堂上也記得更多。運動也能幫助心情低落的人，因為大腦會製造出一種帶來快樂感覺的東西。

然而，並不是愈多愈好。如果地板是濕的，你可能會滑倒、弄傷腿。如果感到疼痛，就應該停下來休息。我們必須學會照顧自己；好的選手知道什麼時候該跑，也知道什麼時候該坐下來。

Questions 28–30
28What is the main idea of this article?
ARunning is the best exercise for every person
BExercise helps the body and the mind, but it needs care
COnly students who play ball games stay healthy
DDoctors should decide how long children exercise
✔ 正確答案：(B)　Exercise helps the body and the mind, but it needs care
中譯這篇文章的主旨是什麼？
解析前兩段講運動對身體與心情的好處，第三段提醒不要過量，合起來就是「運動有益身心，但要小心」。其他三項都超出文章範圍。

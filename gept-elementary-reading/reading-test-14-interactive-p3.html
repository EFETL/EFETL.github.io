ARTICLE
Kangaroos

Kangaroos live in Australia. They are famous for their strong legs. A kangaroo does not walk; it jumps. Some of them can jump very far and very fast.

A mother kangaroo has a pocket in front of her body. Her baby stays there and grows for about six months. It is warm and safe inside the pocket.

Zoos in Singapore and Taiwan also keep kangaroos. In Australia, drivers must pay attention at night, because a kangaroo can suddenly run in front of a car.

全文中譯

袋鼠住在澳洲。牠們以強壯的後腿聞名。袋鼠不走路，牠用跳的。有些袋鼠可以跳得又遠又快。

母袋鼠的身體前面有一個袋子。小袋鼠待在裡面長大，大約六個月。袋子裡又溫暖又安全。

新加坡和臺灣的動物園也養袋鼠。在澳洲，駕駛人晚上必須小心注意，因為袋鼠可能突然衝到車子前面。

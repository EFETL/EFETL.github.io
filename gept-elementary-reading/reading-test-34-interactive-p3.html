NOTICE
Trash Rules for Green Park Building

To all people living here:

  • Put paper, cans and bottles in the yellow box on the first floor.
  • Put food trash in the white bag only.
  • The truck comes at six o'clock every day except Sunday.

Please do not leave bags on the ground at night. Dogs open them, and the garbage goes everywhere. If you are not sure which box to use, ask the officer at the front door.

全文中譯

致本大樓所有住戶：

  • 紙類、鐵鋁罐和瓶子請放在一樓的黃色箱子。
  • 廚餘只能放在白色袋子裡。
  • 垃圾車除了星期日之外，每天六點來。

晚上請勿把袋子留在地上。狗會把袋子咬開，垃圾就會散得到處都是。如果你不確定該用哪個箱子，請問大門口的管理員。

A Letter from Grandpa

Dear Ben,

Thank you for your letter. Life on the farm is busy. Two of our sheep had babies last week, and the little pigs are growing fast.

The river behind our house is clean again. Ten years ago a factory put poison in the water, and all the fish died. Now the water flows clear, and the turtles are back.

Come and stay with us in July. You can help me feed the animals.

Love,
Grandpa

全文中譯

我們家後面的河又乾淨了。十年前有一間工廠把毒排進水裡，魚全都死光了。現在河水流得清澈，烏龜也回來了。

七月來和我們住一陣子吧。你可以幫我餵動物。

愛你的

ARTICLE
Small Animals, Big Trouble

A snail is a tiny animal with a soft body. Many snails live in gardens and near ponds. They eat green leaves at night.

Farmers do not like them, because snails can eat a whole field of young plants. Some farmers use poison to kill them, but the poison also hurts birds and fish.

Today more farmers keep ducks. The ducks eat the snails, and the farm stays clean. This way is slow, but it is safe for the environment.

全文中譯

蝸牛是一種身體柔軟的小小動物。許多蝸牛住在花園裡和池塘邊。牠們在夜裡吃綠色的葉子。

農夫不喜歡牠們，因為蝸牛可以吃掉一整片田的幼苗。有些農夫用毒藥殺牠們，但那些毒也會傷到鳥和魚。

現在有更多農夫改養鴨子。鴨子會吃蝸牛，農場也保持乾淨。這個方法比較慢，但對環境是安全的。

Class 302 has sixty students. For one week, they put paper, bottles, cans and glass into four boxes.

BoxKilograms
Paper18
Plastic bottles9

No one brought glass this week, so that box was still empty on Friday.

箱子公斤
紙類18

這週沒有人帶玻璃來，所以到星期五那個箱子仍然是零。

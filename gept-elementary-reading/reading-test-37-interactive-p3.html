ARTICLE
Walls of the Past

Long ago, many cities in the world had high walls. The walls kept the enemy out. Soldiers stood on top of them day and night. When they saw an army far away, they made a big fire, and everyone in the city knew.

Some of these walls are more than twenty centuries old. Today they are quiet and beautiful in the sun. People no longer need them for war, but many families walk there on Sundays and take pictures.

全文中譯

很久以前，世界上許多城市都有高高的城牆。城牆把敵人擋在外面。士兵日夜站在城牆上。當他們看見遠方有軍隊時，就會升起一堆大火，全城的人就都知道了。

其中有些城牆已經超過二十個世紀。今天它們在陽光下靜靜地、美麗地矗立著。人們不再需要用它們來打仗，但許多家庭星期天會去那裡散步、拍照。

Questions 21–24
21Why did cities build high walls?
ATo stop the rain
BTo hold military games
CTo make the city beautiful
DTo keep the enemy out
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To keep the enemy out
中譯城市為什麼要建高牆？
解析The walls kept the enemy out，其他選項文中都沒有提到。
22What did the soldiers do when they saw an army?
AThey ran into the city
BThey called the king
CThey made a big fire
DThey closed the gate
✔ 正確答案：(C)　They made a big fire
中譯士兵看到軍隊時做了什麼？
解析they made a big fire, and everyone in the city knew，用火當作信號。
23How old are some of these walls?
AAbout one full year
BMore than twenty centuries
CFifty years
DTwo miles
✔ 正確答案：(B)　More than twenty centuries
中譯有些城牆有多久的歷史？
解析Some of these walls are more than twenty centuries old。two miles 是長度不是時間。
24What do people do at the walls today?
AThey walk there and take pictures
BThey live inside them
CThey fight there
DThey build new ones
✔ 正確答案：(A)　They walk there and take pictures
中譯現在人們在城牆做什麼？
解析many families walk there on Sundays and take pictures。

EMAIL
A Letter to a School Friend

Hi Peter,

How are you? I am writing a report about ancient China for history class. I need some help.

You said your father has a big collection of old coins. Can I see them next Saturday? I want to take a few pictures for my report.

I will ride my scooter to your house at two o'clock. Please reply before Friday. If that day is not OK, we can change the time.

Your friend,
Amy

全文中譯

嗨，彼得：

你好嗎？我正在為歷史課寫一份關於古代中國的報告。我需要一些幫忙。

你說過你爸爸收藏了很多古錢幣。下星期六我可以去看看嗎？我想拍幾張照片放進報告裡。

我會在兩點騎機車到你家。請在星期五之前回覆我。如果那天不方便，我們可以改時間。

你的朋友
艾咪

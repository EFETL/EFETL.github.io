An E-mail from a Cousin

Hi Kevin,

How are you? My family is going to have a big party for my grandmother next Saturday. She will be seventy-five years old. All our relatives will come, and my aunt will cook spaghetti and fried chicken.

Can you come with your parents? Our house is on Peace Street, right behind the junior high school. The party starts at six in the evening, and we will cut the cake at eight.

Please call me before Thursday. But if you are busy that day, we can meet next month.

Your cousin,
Amy

全文中譯

凱文你好：

你好嗎？下星期六我家要為我奶奶辦一場盛大的宴會。她要滿七十五歲了。我們所有的親戚都會來，我阿姨會煮義大利麵和炸雞。

你可以和你爸媽一起來嗎？我家在和平街上，就在國中後面。宴會晚上六點開始，我們八點會切蛋糕。

請在星期四之前打電話給我。不過，如果你那天很忙，我們也可以下個月再見面。

你的表妹
艾咪

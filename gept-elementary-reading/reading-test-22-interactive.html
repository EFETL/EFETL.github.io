ARTICLE
New Year Around the World

People around the world have their own New Year traditions. In Taiwan, families clean the house and eat a big traditional dinner together. Children get red envelopes because red is the color of good luck.

In Spain, people eat twelve grapes at midnight. In Japan, many families visit a temple and pray for a healthy year.

These customs look different, but the belief behind them is the same. People simply hope that the new year will be better than the last one.

全文中譯

世界各地的人都有自己的新年傳統。在台灣，家人會打掃房子，一起吃一頓豐盛的傳統晚餐。小孩會拿到紅包，因為紅色是幸運的顏色。

在西班牙，人們會在午夜吃十二顆葡萄。在日本，許多家庭會去參拜寺廟，祈求健康的一年。

這些習俗看起來不同，但背後的信念是一樣的。人們只是希望新的一年會比去年更好。

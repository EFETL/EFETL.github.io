NOTICE
New Phone Rules at Green Junior High

From September 1:

  • Students may bring a cellphone to school, but it must be turned off in class.
  • Put your phone in the box by the door before the first class.
  • You can take it back at four o'clock.

If a teacher finds a phone in your hand during a test, you will fail that test. Please make sure your alarm is off. Do not eat hot dogs or ice cream in the classroom. Ask Mr. Chen in the office if you have any questions.

考試中若老師發現你手上有手機，該次考試將以不及格計。請確認鬧鐘已關閉。教室內請勿吃熱狗或冰淇淋。有任何問題請到辦公室找陳老師。

A Problem with the New Game

Dear Sir,

I bought your game last Friday and downloaded it to my computer. It looked great at first.

But after ten minutes the screen goes black, and the game stops. I tried three times. My brother has the same problem, so it is probably a bug in the game.

My computer is new, and other games run well on it. Can you send me a new copy or give me my money back?

Thank you.
Ken Wu

我的電腦是新的，其他遊戲在上面都跑得很順。你們可以寄一份新的給我，或是退錢給我嗎？

謝謝。
吳肯

ARTICLE
Where Does the Word Bug Come From?

Sixty years ago computers were as big as a room. One day a machine in an office stopped, and no one knew why. A worker opened it and found a small insect inside. She took the insect out, and the machine ran again.

After that, people started to call a computer problem a bug. Today no real insect lives in your phone, but we still use the word. When something is wrong with a program, we say there is a bug in it.

全文中譯

六十年前的電腦大得像一個房間。有一天辦公室裡的一台機器停了，沒有人知道原因。一位工作人員打開機器，發現裡面有一隻小蟲。她把小蟲拿出來，機器就又開始運轉了。

從那之後，人們開始把電腦的問題叫做 bug。今天你的手機裡當然沒有真的蟲，但我們還是在用這個字。當程式出問題時，我們就說裡面有一個 bug。

Passage 1
The Pond Behind the School

The pond behind our school was green and empty for years. The number of fish was zero, and nobody could say when they had gone. (11) Our class chose the pond as a project for the whole year. We took out eleven bags of trash in the first week, including a bicycle, a swimsuit and an old bath toy. Then we did the hard part: we stopped putting anything into it and waited. Nothing happened for four months, (12) in May a classmate saw a small snail on a stone. By September there were tiny fish again. (13), nobody put a single animal in that water. We did not build the pond back, (14) we stopped breaking it.

全文中譯我們學校後面的池塘多年來又綠又空。魚的數量是零，沒有人說得出牠們是什麼時候消失的。去年情況改變了。我們班把這座池塘選為為期一年的專題。第一個星期我們清出十一袋垃圾，包括一台腳踏車、一件泳衣和一個舊的洗澡玩具。接著是最難的部分：我們停止往裡面倒任何東西，然後等待。四個月什麼都沒發生，但五月時，一位同學在石頭上看到一隻小蝸牛。到了九月，又有小魚了。值得注意的是，沒有人放過任何一隻動物進那片水裡。我們不是把池塘重建起來，而是停止破壞它。

Passage 2
Where Our Garbage Really Goes

Most of us think about garbage for about three seconds: the time it takes to drop something in a box. (15) A bottle that leaves your hand on Monday may be on a truck on Tuesday, on a ship by the end of the month, and in a field in another country a year later. Recycling is real, (16) it is not a machine that makes things disappear. It costs energy, water and human work, and some materials can only be used again once or twice. (17), the first question is never how to recycle a thing. It is whether the thing needed to exist, (18) that question has to be asked before we buy, not after.

全文中譯我們多數人思考垃圾的時間大約三秒：把東西丟進箱子所需的時間。之後它的長途旅程才開始。星期一離開你手中的一個瓶子，星期二可能在卡車上，月底之前上了船，一年後出現在另一個國家的田裡。回收是真實存在的，但它不是一台能讓東西消失的機器。它需要耗費能源、水與人力，而有些材料只能再利用一兩次。因此，第一個問題從來都不是「怎麼回收這樣東西」，而是「這樣東西有沒有必要存在」，而這個問題必須在購買之前問，不是之後。

ARTICLE
The Things We Throw Away Twice

About a third of the food grown in the world is never eaten. That happens everywhere, and it is not only a waste of food; it is a waste of everything that went into it.

A tomato that reaches a box and is thrown away has already used water, land, work and the oil that moved it. When we throw it out, we throw all of that away a second time. The tomato is the small part of the loss.

This is why keeping food is worth more than it looks. A family that plans one week of meals may save only a little money, but the water and land behind that food are saved too. The quietest kind of care for the environment happens inside a refrigerator.

全文中譯

全世界生產的食物約有三分之一從未被吃掉。這種情形到處都有，而那不只是食物的浪費，也是投入其中的一切的浪費。

一顆送到箱子裡卻被丟掉的番茄，已經用掉了水、土地、人力，以及運送它的油。當我們把它丟掉，我們是把這一切再丟掉一次。番茄本身只是這份損失的一小部分。

這就是為什麼把食物留住，價值比看起來更高。一個規劃一週餐食的家庭，也許只省下一點錢，但那些食物背後的水與土地也一併被省下了。對環境最安靜的一種照顧，發生在冰箱裡面。

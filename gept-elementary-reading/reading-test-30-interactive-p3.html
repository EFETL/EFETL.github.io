Sun Star Bookstore — Part-time Clerk Wanted

Sun Star Bookstore is looking for a part-time clerk!

  • Work: Saturday and Sunday, 1:00-6:00 p.m.
  • Pay: NT$200 an hour
  • Age: over eighteen

You will help people find books and keep the shelves clean. You do not need to bring any documents on the first day.

Our shop is next to the MRT station on Central Road. Come and talk to Mr. Lin before Friday. We will hire another clerk in June, too. Call 04-2211-5566 for more information.

全文中譯

太陽星書店徵求兼職店員！

  • 工作時間：週六與週日下午 1:00-6:00
  • 時薪：新台幣 200 元
  • 年齡：十八歲以上

你要幫客人找書，並保持書架整潔。第一天上班不必帶任何文件。

本店就在中央路捷運站旁。請在星期五前來找林先生談。我們六月還會再雇用一位店員。想知道更多，請打 04-2211-5566 洽詢。

EMAIL
Good News from Mei

Dear Ken,

I have good news. I got the job! The company will hire me as a secretary in the sales department. I start next Monday.

My new office is small, but it has a big window. The building is not ours; my company rents two floors.

My boss seems friendly. She promised to teach me everything, so I am not afraid. The pay is not high, but I think this job is a good start for my career.

Can we have lunch on Sunday? I want to tell you more.

Mei

全文中譯

我的新辦公室很小，但有一扇大窗戶。這棟大樓不是我們的；我們公司租了兩層樓。

我們星期天可以一起吃午餐嗎？我想再多告訴你一些。

小梅

ARTICLE
Part-time Jobs for Students

Many students in Taiwan take part-time jobs in the summer. Some work as clerks in shops, and others help in restaurants.

A part-time job is not only about money. Students learn to be on time, to obey rules, and to talk with strangers. These things are useful in any career.

Of course, a job can become terrible when the hours are too long. Teachers say students should work no more than twenty hours a week. Rest and study are still the most important things.

全文中譯

台灣有許多學生會在暑假打工。有些人在店裡當店員，有些人在餐廳幫忙。

打工不只是為了錢。學生學會準時、學會遵守規定，也學會和陌生人說話。這些事在任何職業裡都用得到。

當然，工時太長時，工作也可能變得很糟糕。老師說學生一週工作不應超過二十小時。休息和讀書仍然是最重要的事。

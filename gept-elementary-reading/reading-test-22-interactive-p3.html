NOTICE
School Moon Festival Night

School Moon Festival Night

  • Date: Friday, September 20
  • Time: 6:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.
  • Place: The playground behind the library

Come and celebrate this old holiday with us! Every class will cook food outside, and there will be moon cakes for everyone. Students may bring one family member. Please tell Mrs. Lin before Wednesday, or sign up online. If it rains, we will move the party to the gym.

全文中譯

學校中秋之夜

  • 日期：9 月 20 日星期五
  • 時間：晚上 6:00 到 9:00
  • 地點：圖書館後面的操場

來和我們一起慶祝這個古老的節日吧！每個班級都會在戶外烤東西吃，也會準備月餅給大家。學生可以帶一位家人參加。請在星期三前告訴林太太，或是上網報名。如果下雨，我們會把活動移到體育館。

A Thanksgiving Dinner in Boston

Dear Wei,

Last week I stayed with an American family in Boston. On Thursday we had a Thanksgiving dinner. Ten people sat around a long table, and the turkey was very big. Before we ate, everyone said one thing they were happy about. That custom was new to me, but I liked it a lot.

I am sending you a small gift from the airport shop. My address here has changed, so please write to my email address.

Your friend,
Kevin

全文中譯

親愛的偉：

上星期我住在波士頓的一個美國家庭。星期四我們吃了感恩節大餐。十個人圍著一張長桌坐，火雞非常大。開動前，每個人都說一件自己覺得開心的事。這個習俗對我來說很新鮮，但我很喜歡。

我要從機場商店寄一份小禮物給你。我這裡的地址換了，所以請改寄到我的電子信箱。

你的朋友
凱文

ARTICLE
New Year Around the World

People around the world have their own New Year traditions. In Taiwan, families clean the house and eat a big traditional dinner together. Children get red envelopes because red is the color of good luck.

In Spain, people eat twelve grapes at midnight. In Japan, many families visit a temple and pray for a healthy year.

These customs look different, but the belief behind them is the same. People simply hope that the new year will be better than the last one.

全文中譯

世界各地的人都有自己的新年傳統。在台灣，家人會打掃房子，一起吃一頓豐盛的傳統晚餐。小孩會拿到紅包，因為紅色是幸運的顏色。

在西班牙，人們會在午夜吃十二顆葡萄。在日本，許多家庭會去參拜寺廟，祈求健康的一年。

這些習俗看起來不同，但背後的信念是一樣的。人們只是希望新的一年會比去年更好。

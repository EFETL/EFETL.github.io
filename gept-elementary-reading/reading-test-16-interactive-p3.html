An Invitation from Lisa

Dear Ben,

How are you? My family will have a small party next Tuesday evening. My mother will cook noodles and make apple juice. My neighbor, Mr. Wu, will bring his guitar.

We start at six. Please come to my house at 5:30 if you can. You can bring a friend, but please tell me before Sunday.

After dinner my sister will teach us to roller skate in the park. If it rains, we will go somewhere else. It will be terrific!

See you soon,
Lisa

再見

Do you want to make new friends? Join the Sunshine Club!

ARTICLE
Talking to a Stranger

Many students feel shy when they meet a stranger. Their hands get cold, and they do not know what to say. Almost every student feels this way, so your fellow classmates are the same.

Here is an easy way. Just say hello and ask a simple question about the place or the weather. Most people are happy to answer. Listen carefully, and then talk about something you both like.

Friendship often starts from one short talk. The first minute is hard, but it becomes easy after a few times.

全文中譯

許多學生遇到陌生人時會害羞。他們的手會發冷，也不知道要說什麼。幾乎每個學生都是這樣，你的同學們也一樣。

這裡有個簡單的方法。就說聲哈囉，再問一個關於地點或天氣的簡單問題。大部分的人都很樂意回答。仔細聽，然後聊聊你們都喜歡的事。

友誼常常就從一次短短的談話開始。第一分鐘很難，但試過幾次後就會變得容易。

Passage 1
The Old Dresser

When my grandmother moved from Hualian to our house, she brought only one big (11) with her. It was old, and one of its legs was short, so it never stood straight on the carpet. My father wanted to throw it away. (12) She said her own mother had kept her letters and her wallet in it for forty years. (13), my father put a small piece of wood under the short leg and painted the whole thing white. Now it stands in the living room, (14) every visitor says it is the nicest item in our house.

全文中譯當我奶奶從花蓮搬到我們家時，她只帶了一個大梳妝台。它很舊，其中一隻腳比較短，所以放在地毯上總是站不直。我爸爸想把它丟掉。但我奶奶請他再想一想。她說她自己的母親把信件和皮夾放在裡面四十年。最後，我爸爸在短腳下面墊了一小塊木頭，把整個梳妝台漆成白色。現在它就放在客廳，而每位訪客都說那是我們家最好看的物品。

Passage 2
A Small Room for Two

My brother and I share a room that is only three (15) wide. There is one bed, one desk, and a bookcase that we made ourselves from an old box. Every item must have its own position, or we cannot walk to the door. (16) We agreed that he keeps the top shelf and I keep the bottom one. He is careful about his things, (17) I often leave my cup on the mat. Last week he did not blame me; he just put a small dish beside my bed for the cup. (18), sharing a small room has taught me more about people than any book.

全文中譯我和弟弟共用一個只有三個單位寬的房間。裡面有一張床、一張書桌，還有一個我們用舊箱子自己做的書櫃。每樣東西都必須有自己的位置，不然我們連走到門口都有困難。所以我們去年訂了一條規則。我們說好上層架子歸他，下層歸我。他對自己的東西很小心，但我常常把杯子留在墊子上。上星期他沒有責備我，只是在我床邊放了一個小盤子放杯子。說真的，共用一個小房間教我認識人的事，比任何書都多。

NOTICE
Messages and a Price List

Mom: The store on Second Road is selling old furniture this weekend. We need a chair for your desk.

Wei: How much is it? I saved two thousand dollars from my summer job.

Mom: Look at this paper.

SECOND ROAD FURNITURE
Item: Wooden Chair
Old price: 1,200
This weekend: 800
Free if you buy a desk

Wei: We already have a desk, so I will just take the chair.

Mom: Then bring your wallet. The store closes at six, and it is on the way back from the temple.

全文中譯

媽媽：二路上那家店這週末在賣二手家具。你的書桌需要一張椅子。

偉：多少錢？我打工存了兩千元。

媽媽：看看這張紙。

二路家具行
品項：木椅
原價：1,200
本週末：800
買書桌即免費

偉：我們已經有書桌了，所以我就只買椅子。

媽媽：那就帶著你的皮夾。店六點關門，而且就在從寺廟回來的路上。

C1,200 dollars

✔ 正確答案：(A)　800 dollars
中譯偉買這張椅子要付多少錢？
解析本週末價 800 元。免費的條件是同時買書桌，但他們已經有書桌，所以不適用；1,200 是原價，2,000 是他的存款。

解析關門時間是媽媽在訊息裡說的，不在價目表上。品名、本週價、原價表上都有。

Welcome to the Reading Room. Walk up to the second floor and turn left.

  • Take off your shoes before you walk on the mat.

ARTICLE
Where Our Furniture Comes From

Look around your living room. The chair you sit on may have started as a tree in Australia, and the carpet under your feet may have come from a small town far away.

A hundred years ago, most families in Taiwan used furniture made in their own village. A bed or a bookcase was built by a person whose name everyone knew, and it was made to last for three or four families.

Today a chair costs less, but it often lasts only a few years. Some people now buy old furniture and fix it. They say that an old desk with a mark on it holds something a new one cannot: the story of the people who used it before.

全文中譯

看看你的客廳。你坐的椅子可能一開始是澳洲的一棵樹，腳下的地毯可能來自遙遠的小鎮。

一百年前，台灣大多數家庭使用的是自己村裡做的家具。一張床或一個書櫃是由大家都認識的人打造的，而且做得能用上三、四代。

今天椅子便宜多了，卻常常只能用幾年。現在有些人改買二手家具來修。他們說，一張有刮痕的舊書桌擁有新家具沒有的東西：那就是曾經使用它的人的故事。

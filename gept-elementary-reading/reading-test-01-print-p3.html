ARTICLE
Breakfast Around the World

People eat very different things in the morning. In Taiwan, many people have hot soybean milk and a piece of bread on the way to work. In America, some people eat eggs and toast at home.

In France, a cup of coffee and a small cake are enough for many people. In Japan, rice, fish and soup are still very common.

One thing is the same everywhere. Doctors say people who eat breakfast can think better at school and at work.

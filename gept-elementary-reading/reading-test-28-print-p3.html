ARTICLE
How a Movie Is Made

Watching a movie is easy, but making one is hard work. First, a writer puts the story on paper. Then the actors read it again and again.

On the first day, the workers fill the stage with lights, cameras and other objects. The lights stay on all day. Some parts are shot in the dark, and some are shot in the rain.

One short part may take a whole day. When the film is finally ready, the audience sees only two hours of it.

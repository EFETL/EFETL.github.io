Houses Around the World

Houses around the world are not all the same. In cold places, people build thick walls and make small windows to keep the heat inside. Some roofs are very high, so the snow can slide off easily.

Near the sea, fishermen sometimes build their houses above the water. In hot places, many people sleep on the roof in summer.

People build houses in different ways, but whether they are rich or poor, they all want the same thing: a safe and warm room to come back to.

Big Animals in the Sea

Whales are the biggest animals in the world. A blue whale can be thirty meters long. It is not a fish, so it must come up for air.

Sharks are fish. They have strong teeth and can smell blood from far away. Most sharks do not eat people, but people kill many sharks every year for their meat.

Scientists remind us that the sea is their home. If we drop trash into the water, these animals will be in trouble.

A Letter About a Sick Rabbit

Dear Ms. Wang,

My family flew to England two weeks ago, and my grandmother took care of my rabbit for me. When we came back, we found that his ears were red and he did not eat anything.

We took him to the animal hospital below your office. The doctor said he only felt cold, so we bought a small heater for his box. Now he eats and runs again.

Thank you for telling us about that hospital.

Kelly

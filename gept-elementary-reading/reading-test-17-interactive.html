ARTICLE
Bicycles in the City

More and more people in Taiwan ride bicycles to work. A bicycle is cheap, and it does not make the air dirty. It is also good for your health.

Our city is proud of its new bike paths along the river. In the northern part of the city, the paths are even wider. Riders do not need to share the road with cars, so the ride is safe and quiet.

Of course, there are problems. After a heavy rain, riding is not fun. And a flat tire is a big problem on a busy day. Still, most riders say they will keep riding.

全文中譯

臺灣越來越多人騎腳踏車上班。腳踏車很便宜，也不會汙染空氣，而且對健康有益。

我們的城市以沿著河邊的新自行車道為傲。在城市的北邊，車道甚至更寬。騎士不必和汽車共用道路，所以騎起來又安全又安靜。

當然，也有問題。下大雨過後騎車就不好玩了。忙碌的日子裡爆胎也是個大麻煩。儘管如此，大多數騎士還是說他們會繼續騎。

Two days only · Saturday and Sunday

  • Fruit from southern farms: buy two, get one free
  • All bread after four o'clock: half price
  • Free sample of our new tea at the front desk (ask any question there)

Our market works with small farms, not with large companies, and we sell what they produce that same week. Each week we choose one farm and print its story on the wall near the basement stairs. This week the farm belongs to a family that has grown tea for four generations.

I saw your advertisement in yesterday's paper. It says that any customer who buys a fan this month gets a free meter of tape. I went to your shop on South Road this morning, but the worker told me that the deal ended last week.

ARTICLE
Why the Same Shirt Costs More

Two shops on the same street may sell the same shirt at different prices. Many people think one shop is simply trying to take more money, but the reason is often less simple.

A large firm buys ten thousand shirts at one time, so each shirt costs it less. A small shop buys fifty, and it also pays rent, which can cost it an arm and a leg, power, and the pay of the person who puts them on the shelf. The price card itself carries all of that.

This does not mean the higher price is always fair. It does mean that a price is a story about supply, not just a choice made by one owner who wants more money. Before you decide, it may help to ask what you are really paying for.

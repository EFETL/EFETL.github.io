My New Place

Hi Judy,

I moved into a small apartment near my school last week. There is only one bedroom, but I like it very much. The rent is about six thousand dollars a month.

My bed is beside the window, and a small bookcase stands next to it. I bought a used desk and an old chair from my neighbor for eight hundred dollars.

I still need a freezer. Do you know a cheap store? Please come and see my new place this Sunday.

Nina

我上星期搬進學校附近的一間小公寓。只有一間臥室，但我很喜歡。房租大約一個月六千元。

我的床在窗戶旁邊，旁邊立著一個小書櫃。我用八百元向鄰居買了一張二手書桌和一把舊椅子。

ARTICLE
Old Things, New Life

Do not throw away your old furniture. Those things can have a new life.

An old wooden box can become a small table beside your bed. A large case under the couch can hold your winter clothes. If a chair is broken, take it back to a shop. Most of the time, a worker can fix it in one day.

Some cities in Australia and Canada give old furniture to poor families. Taiwan is doing the same thing now, and more people are happy to help.

全文中譯

不要把舊家具丟掉。那些東西可以有新的生命。

一個舊木箱可以變成床邊的小桌子。沙發下面的大箱子可以用來收冬天的衣服。如果椅子壞了，把它送回店裡。大部分的情況下，師傅一天就能修好。

澳洲和加拿大有些城市會把舊家具送給貧窮的家庭。台灣現在也在做同樣的事，愈來愈多人樂意幫忙。

NOTICE
Messages and a Price List

Mom: The store on Second Road is selling old furniture this weekend. We need a chair for your desk.

Wei: How much is it? I saved two thousand dollars from my summer job.

Mom: Look at this paper.

SECOND ROAD FURNITURE
Item: Wooden Chair
Old price: 1,200
This weekend: 800
Free if you buy a desk

Wei: We already have a desk, so I will just take the chair.

Mom: Then bring your wallet. The store closes at six, and it is on the way back from the temple.

全文中譯

媽媽：二路上那家店這週末在賣二手家具。你的書桌需要一張椅子。

偉：多少錢？我打工存了兩千元。

媽媽：看看這張紙。

二路家具行
品項：木椅
原價：1,200
本週末：800
買書桌即免費

偉：我們已經有書桌了，所以我就只買椅子。

媽媽：那就帶著你的皮夾。店六點關門，而且就在從寺廟回來的路上。

Questions 19–21
19How much will Wei pay for the chair?
A800 dollars
BNothing
C1,200 dollars
D2,000 dollars
✔ 正確答案：(A)　800 dollars
中譯偉買這張椅子要付多少錢？
解析本週末價 800 元。免費的條件是同時買書桌，但他們已經有書桌，所以不適用；1,200 是原價，2,000 是他的存款。
20What does the price list NOT tell us?
AThe name of the item
BThe price this weekend
CThe time the store closes
DThe price before this weekend
✔ 正確答案：(C)　The time the store closes
中譯價目表上沒有告訴我們什麼？
解析關門時間是媽媽在訊息裡說的，不在價目表上。品名、本週價、原價表上都有。
21What is the purpose of Wei's mother's messages?
ATo ask Wei to lend her money
BTo help Wei buy a chair
CTo tell Wei to visit the temple
DTo sell an old desk
✔ 正確答案：(B)　To help Wei buy a chair
中譯偉的媽媽傳訊息的目的是什麼？
解析她告知特賣、提供價格標籤、提醒帶皮夾與關門時間，全都是為了幫偉買到椅子。寺廟只是順路提及。
NOTICE
Rules for the Reading Room

Welcome to the Reading Room. Walk up to the second floor and turn left.

  • Take off your shoes before you walk on the mat.
  • Every book must go back to the same bookcase.
  • Food and drinks, even strawberry or melon juice, stay in the box beside the door.
  • The video corner is open only after four o'clock.

Students may move a chair, but the couch and the long desk must stay where they are. If you need a quiet position, the seats near the window are best.

Please do not leave anything, not even a pencil case, on the carpet. Anything left after six will be put in the cabinet near the toilet, and you will have to wait until Monday to get it back.

全文中譯

歡迎來到閱覽室。請走上二樓後左轉。

  • 踏上地墊前請先脫鞋。
  • 每本書都必須放回原來的書櫃。
  • 食物與飲料，連草莓或哈密瓜汁也一樣，請放在門邊的箱子裡。
  • 影音區四點後才開放。

學生可以移動椅子，但沙發和長桌必須留在原位。如果你需要安靜的位子，靠窗的座位最好。

請不要把任何東西留在地毯上，連鉛筆盒也不行。六點後留下的物品會被放進廁所旁的櫥櫃，你必須等到星期一才能取回。

EMAIL
A Gift from Canada

Dear Aunt Mary,

Thank you for the box you sent from Canada. The spoons arrived without a single mark on them, and Mother has already put them in the kitchen cabinet. She says they are too nice to use every day.

I have one question. Inside the box there was a small round thing with numbers on it, like a dial. Nobody here knows what it is for. Could you tell me how to use it? I have a photo, but I do not want to send a big photo if your line is slow.

We are all well. Father hurt his neck last month, but he can walk again now. Please come and stay with us when you visit Taiwan.

Sincerely, Amy

全文中譯

親愛的瑪麗阿姨：

謝謝您從加拿大寄來的箱子。那些湯匙送達時一點刮痕都沒有，媽媽已經把它們放進廚房的櫥櫃了。她說那些太好看了，不適合每天使用。

我有一個問題。箱子裡有一個小小圓圓、上面有數字的東西，看起來像個轉盤。這裡沒有人知道它的用途。您能告訴我怎麼使用嗎？我有照片，但如果您的網路很慢，我不想寄太大的照片。

我們都很好。爸爸上個月弄傷了脖子，但現在已經可以走路了。您來台灣時請來我們家住。

敬上，艾咪

ARTICLE
Where Our Furniture Comes From

Look around your living room. The chair you sit on may have started as a tree in Australia, and the carpet under your feet may have come from a small town far away.

A hundred years ago, most families in Taiwan used furniture made in their own village. A bed or a bookcase was built by a person whose name everyone knew, and it was made to last for three or four families.

Today a chair costs less, but it often lasts only a few years. Some people now buy old furniture and fix it. They say that an old desk with a mark on it holds something a new one cannot: the story of the people who used it before.

全文中譯

看看你的客廳。你坐的椅子可能一開始是澳洲的一棵樹，腳下的地毯可能來自遙遠的小鎮。

一百年前，台灣大多數家庭使用的是自己村裡做的家具。一張床或一個書櫃是由大家都認識的人打造的，而且做得能用上三、四代。

今天椅子便宜多了，卻常常只能用幾年。現在有些人改買二手家具來修。他們說，一張有刮痕的舊書桌擁有新家具沒有的東西：那就是曾經使用它的人的故事。

Questions 28–30
28What is the main idea of this article?
ATrees from Australia make the best chairs
BEvery family should build its own bookcase
CFurniture today is cheaper but means less than before
DOld furniture is always stronger than new furniture
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Furniture today is cheaper but means less than before
中譯這篇文章的主旨是什麼？
解析文章對比過去「在地製作、能傳三四代」與今天「便宜但只用幾年」，最後指出舊家具承載故事，主旨是家具變便宜卻失去意義。
29According to the article, what was furniture like a hundred years ago?
AIt was bought from other countries
BIt was changed every few years
CIt was sold without any mark
DIt was made by someone in the village
✔ 正確答案：(D)　It was made by someone in the village
中譯根據本文，一百年前的家具是什麼樣子？
解析A bed or a bookcase was built by a person whose name everyone knew，也就是村裡的人做的。
30Why do some people buy old furniture?
AIt is light and easy to move
BIt never needs to be fixed
CIt carries the story of the people who used it
DIt costs nothing to keep
✔ 正確答案：(C)　It carries the story of the people who used it
中譯有些人為什麼買二手家具？
解析an old desk with a mark on it holds ... the story of the people who used it before。文中說是買來「修」，並非不需修理。

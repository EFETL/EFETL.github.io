Passage 1
The Garden on the Roof

Three years ago the roof of our building was an empty space where nobody went. Then Mrs. Huang, who is seventy, carried up two pots and planted some beans. (11) By the end of that summer, six families had pots of their own. Now there are tomatoes in one corner and flowers along the wall. A spider lives between two pots, and nobody takes it down, (12) it keeps the small insects away. Children come up after school to look for it. (13), the roof is the only place in the building where every floor meets. Her husband helped her carry the pots. She says his plan was only to use the space, (14) a garden turned out to be about people.

全文中譯三年前，我們大樓的屋頂是一片沒有人去的空地。後來七十歲的黃太太搬了兩個花盆上去，種了一些豆子。她的鄰居起初笑她。到那年夏天結束時，已經有六戶人家有了自己的花盆。現在一個角落種著番茄，牆邊種著花。有隻蜘蛛住在兩個花盆之間，沒有人把它拿掉，因為牠能趕走小蟲。孩子們放學後會上去找牠。如今，屋頂是這棟樓裡唯一每一層樓的人都會遇見彼此的地方。她先生幫她搬花盆。她說他的打算只是想利用那塊空地，但花園最後變成了關於人的事。

Passage 2
What a Seed Knows

A seed looks dead. You can keep one in a drawer for years and nothing will happen. (15) Water, heat, and dark together tell the seed that it is under the ground, and only then does it open. Some seeds wait far longer than a year. In England a seed found in an old building grew after two hundred years in a box. (16) nobody had given it water. Farmers have used this fact for a long time. They keep seeds dry so that the seeds stay asleep, (17) they can choose the day when growth begins. A seed is not really sleeping. It is waiting, (18) waiting is a kind of work.

全文中譯種子看起來像是死的。你可以把一顆種子放在抽屜裡好幾年，什麼事都不會發生。但只要給它三樣東西，它就會醒過來。水、溫度和黑暗合在一起，會告訴種子它正在地底下，這時它才會裂開。有些種子等待的時間遠超過一年。在英國，一顆在舊建築裡找到的種子，在盒子裡待了兩百年後仍然發芽。在那之前沒有人給過它水。農夫很久以前就懂得利用這個事實。他們把種子保持乾燥，讓種子繼續沉睡，這樣他們就能選擇讓它開始生長的日子。種子其實並不是在睡覺。它是在等待，而等待也是一種工作。

ARTICLE
The Quiet Work of Roots

When we look at a tree, we see the part above the ground. Yet in many trees, as much life lies below as above. Roots hold the tree in a storm, carry water, and store food for the winter.

Roots also talk to each other. Under a forest floor, thin lines join one tree to the next, and a strong tree can pass food to a weak one, even to a tree of another kind. A forest is less a crowd of single trees than one large living thing. In the end even a wolf in the hills or a shark in the sea depends on what plants make.

This is why moving a big tree so often fails. We can carry the body of the tree and the branches, but we cannot carry the lines it has built under the ground for fifty years. The tree that arrives at the new place is, in a way, not the same tree.

全文中譯

當我們看一棵樹時，看到的是地面以上的部分。然而在許多樹身上，地下的生命與地上一樣多。樹根在暴風中固定樹身、輸送水分，並為冬天儲存養分。

樹根之間也會交談。在森林的地面之下，細細的線把一棵樹連到下一棵，強壯的樹可以把養分傳給虛弱的樹，甚至傳給不同種類的樹。森林與其說是一群各自獨立的樹，不如說是一個巨大的生命體。說到底，連山上的狼或海裡的鯊魚，都依賴植物所製造的東西。

這就是為什麼移植大樹常常失敗。我們搬得動樹幹和枝條，卻搬不動它在地下建了五十年的那些線。抵達新地點的那棵樹，某種意義上已經不是同一棵樹了。

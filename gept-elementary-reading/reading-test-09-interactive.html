ARTICLE
Less Sugar, Better Health

Many students in Taiwan drink sweet tea and eat candy every day. Doctors say this is not good news for us. Sweet food can stop your hunger for a short time, but it comes back fast.

Too much sugar can make people fat, and it is also bad for the heart. It hurts our teeth, too.

Doctors do not ask us to say goodbye to all sweet food. They only ask us to eat less of it. Fruit is a much better choice than candy, because it has water and other good things in it.

Small steps can improve our health in a big way.

全文中譯

台灣有很多學生每天喝甜茶、吃糖果。醫生說這對我們來說不是好消息。甜食可以暫時止住飢餓，但飢餓感很快又會回來。

糖分太多會讓人變胖，也對心臟不好。它同樣會傷害我們的牙齒。

醫生並不是要我們和所有甜食說再見。他們只是要我們少吃一點。水果是比糖果好得多的選擇，因為水果含有水分和其他有益的成分。

小小的一步，就能大大改善我們的健康。

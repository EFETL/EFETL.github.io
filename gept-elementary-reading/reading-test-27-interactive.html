ARTICLE
Money in the Past

Long ago, people did not use money. They gave rice and got fish. This way was not easy. A farmer with a big cow could not always find the right person.

Later, people started to use small pieces of gold and silver. These were the first coins. Paper bills came much later, because they were lighter and safer to carry.

Today many people pay with a card or a phone. This way is very common now, and people do not use cash very often.

全文中譯

很久以前，人們不用錢。他們拿米去換魚。這種方式並不容易。有一頭大牛的農夫不見得總能找到合適的人交換。

後來，人們開始使用小塊的金和銀。這些就是最早的硬幣。紙鈔出現得晚得多，因為紙鈔比較輕、帶在身上也比較安全。

今天許多人用卡片或手機付款。這種方式現在非常普遍，人們也不太常用現金。

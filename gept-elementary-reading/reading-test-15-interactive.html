Passage 1
The Shop on the Corner

Mrs. Kao has run a small shop on our corner since I was a child. She knows which customer likes soft bread and who cannot eat sugar. When a child begins to cry at her door, she always comes out. Two years ago a big supermarket opened across the road. (11) Her shop grew quiet, and she began to close at seven. Then something strange happened. People who had moved to the supermarket started coming back, (12) they missed being known by name. Mrs. Kao never made her prices lower. (13), she wrote each regular customer's favorite item on a card behind the desk. Her shop is still small, and she does not worry about the supermarket anymore, (14) nobody in our street calls it old.

全文中譯高太太從我小時候就在我們街角開一家小店。她知道哪位客人喜歡軟麵包、誰不能吃糖。有小孩在她門口哭時，她總是會出來。兩年前，對面開了一家大超市。一開始，大家都跑去那裡。她的店變得冷清，她也開始七點就打烊。後來發生了奇怪的事。那些轉去超市的人開始回來了，因為他們懷念被人叫得出名字的感覺。高太太從來沒有降價。相反地，她把每位常客最愛的商品寫在櫃檯後的卡片上。她的店仍然很小，而她已經不再擔心那家超市，但我們這條街上沒有人說它老舊。

Passage 2
Where Does a Banana Come From?

A banana on a supermarket shelf looks simple, but it has already made a long trip. A farmer in the south picks it while it is still green, and cold does not kill it on the way. A company puts it in a cold box, and a truck brings it north along the highway. (15) By the time you pay for it, five or six businesses have each taken a small part of the price. Most people never think about this, (16) they only see the number on the shelf. Some markets now print the farmer's name beside the fruit. (17), people can see who grew it. It costs the market nothing, (18) many customers say they like the idea.

全文中譯超市架上的一根香蕉看起來很單純，但它已經走了很長一段路。南部的農夫在它還是綠色時採下它，而低溫不會讓它在路上壞掉。一家公司把它放進冷藏箱，卡車沿著高速公路把它運到北部。整趟路可能要花上三天。當你付錢時，已經有五、六家業者各自拿走了價格中的一小部分。多數消費者從不會想到這件事，因為他們只看到架上的數字。現在有些市場會在水果旁印上農夫的名字。這樣一來，消費者就能看到是誰種的。這對市場沒有任何成本，而許多顧客說他們喜歡這個做法。

ARTICLE
Why the Same Shirt Costs More

Two shops on the same street may sell the same shirt at different prices. Many people think one shop is simply trying to take more money, but the reason is often less simple.

A large firm buys ten thousand shirts at one time, so each shirt costs it less. A small shop buys fifty, and it also pays rent, which can cost it an arm and a leg, power, and the pay of the person who puts them on the shelf. The price card itself carries all of that.

This does not mean the higher price is always fair. It does mean that a price is a story about supply, not just a choice made by one owner who wants more money. Before you decide, it may help to ask what you are really paying for.

全文中譯

同一條街上的兩家店，可能用不同的價格賣同一件襯衫。很多人認為其中一家只是想多賺錢，但原因通常沒那麼單純。

大公司一次買一萬件襯衫，所以每件的成本較低。小店一次買五十件，還要付高昂的房租、電費，以及把衣服上架那個人的薪水。價格牌本身承載了這一切。

這不代表比較高的價格一定合理。但這確實表示，價格是一個關於供應的故事，而不只是某個想多賺錢的老闆的決定。在你決定之前，問問自己真正付的是什麼，或許會有幫助。

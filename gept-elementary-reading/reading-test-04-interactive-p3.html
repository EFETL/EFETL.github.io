ARTICLE
Colors and Feelings

Colors can change how we feel. Many people say blue is a quiet color. It makes them calm, but too much blue can also make them sad.

Red is a strong color. It can make people excited, and sometimes angry. That is why some restaurants use red walls. In some parts of Asia, red also means good luck.

Bright yellow is like the sun. Doctors say it can make a dark room feel warm and happy.

全文中譯

顏色可以改變我們的感覺。許多人說藍色是安靜的顏色。它讓人平靜，但太多的藍色也會讓人變得憂鬱。

紅色是強烈的顏色。它會讓人興奮，有時候還會讓人生氣。這就是為什麼有些餐廳用紅色的牆。在亞洲的一些地方，紅色也代表好運。

明亮的黃色就像太陽。醫生說它可以讓昏暗的房間感覺溫暖又快樂。

Questions 21–24
21What is this article about?
AHow colors change our feelings
BHow to choose clothes
CHow to paint a room
DHow doctors work
✔ 正確答案：(A)　How colors change our feelings
中譯這篇文章在講什麼？
解析第一句 Colors can change how we feel 就點出主題，後面分別談藍、紅、黃三種顏色帶來的感受。
22What can too much blue do?
AMake people angry
BMake people hungry
CMake people sleepy
DMake people sad
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Make people sad
中譯太多藍色會造成什麼？
解析too much blue can also make them sad；讓人生氣的是紅色。
23Why do some restaurants use red walls?
ARed is a quiet color
BRed is easy to clean
CRed is cheap
DRed makes people excited
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Red makes people excited
中譯為什麼有些餐廳用紅色的牆？
解析It can make people excited… That is why some restaurants use red walls；安靜的顏色指的是藍色。
24What do doctors say about bright yellow?

BIt is bad for the eyes
CIt is the same as blue
DIt makes people angry
✔ 正確答案：(A)　It makes a dark room feel warm

解析Doctors say it can make a dark room feel warm and happy。
EMAIL
I Am Sorry

Dear Kevin,

I am writing to say sorry. Last Friday I was mad at you in the classroom, and I said something bad. I was not angry with you. I got a low grade that morning, and I felt down all day.

You are my best friend, and I do not want to lose you. Can we eat hamburgers together after school tomorrow?

Please write back.

Ted

全文中譯

親愛的凱文：

我寫這封信是要跟你說對不起。上星期五我在教室對你發脾氣，還說了不好聽的話。我其實不是在生你的氣。那天早上我考了低分，一整天心情都很低落。

你是我最好的朋友，我不想失去你。我們明天放學後可以一起去吃漢堡嗎？

請回覆我。

泰德

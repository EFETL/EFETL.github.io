1My grandfather is over eighty, but he is still very .
Aprimary
Bwell
Ccrowded
Dexact
✔ 正確答案：(B)　well
中譯我爺爺八十多歲了，但他身體還是很健康。
解析well 除了「很好地」，也可當形容詞表示「健康的」，用來說人身體好。exact 確切的、primary 初級的、crowded 擁擠的都不能形容一個人的身體狀況。
單字well 健康的；很好地、exact 確切的、primary 初級的

Passage 1
Sports Day at Our School

Every spring our school holds a sports day, when students can run in a race, swim in the pool, or play soccer on the field. My friend Amy, who runs faster than anyone else in our class, gets up at six so that she can (11) on the field before school. Last year she got the best score in the 100-meter race and broke the school record. (12) This year she wants to win again. (13), she has caught a bad cold, and her mother has told her to stay in bed until she feels better. I am too slow to win a running race, (14) I will join the swimming race. I hope Amy will be well soon.

全文中譯每年春天我們學校都會舉辦運動會，學生可以參加賽跑、在泳池游泳，或在操場踢足球。我的朋友艾美是我們班跑最快的人，她每天六點起床，好在上學前到操場練習。去年她在一百公尺賽跑中拿到最好的成績，還打破了學校紀錄。全班都為她歡呼。今年她想再贏一次。然而，她得了重感冒，媽媽要她躺在床上休息，直到身體好一點為止。我跑太慢，贏不了賽跑，所以我會參加游泳比賽。我希望艾美早日康復。

Dwiden
✔ 正確答案：(A)　practice

Passage 2
My Uncle Stays Healthy

My uncle is fifty years old, but he still looks young and (15). Ten years ago he was so heavy that he could not walk up the stairs without stopping, and a doctor told him that he had to take better care of his health. (16), he did not like exercise at all and often made excuses. Now he goes to the gym in his office building three times a week after work, and on Sunday mornings he plays golf with old friends whom he has known since high school. He also eats less steak and more fresh vegetables. (17) My uncle never stops exercising, (18) he knows his old problems could come back if he gets lazy.

全文中譯我叔叔五十歲了，但看起來仍然年輕又健康。十年前他胖到爬樓梯沒辦法不停下來休息，醫生告訴他一定要更照顧自己的健康。一開始，他一點也不喜歡運動，還常常找藉口。現在他每週三次下班後到辦公大樓裡的健身房，星期天早上還和高中就認識的老朋友打高爾夫球。他也少吃牛排、多吃新鮮蔬菜。他的醫生現在對他很滿意。我叔叔從不停止運動，因為他知道如果變懶，以前的問題可能會再回來。

ARTICLE
Walking Is Enough

Many people say they are too busy for sports, and they think they must go to a gym or join a team if they want to stay healthy. In fact, walking is good exercise, and it is free. Doctors say that thirty minutes of walking a day is enough for most people, even for those who have never played a sport.

You do not need a ball, a court, or special clothes; you only need a pair of good sneakers. Some people walk to the train station when they could take a taxi, and others use the stairs at work. In this way, they get exercise and save money, too.

If you think walking is boring, walk with a friend or listen to music. Even dancing in your living room is a good start.

全文中譯

很多人說他們太忙，沒時間運動，還以為想保持健康就一定要上健身房或加入球隊。事實上，走路就是很好的運動，而且不用錢。醫生說，對大多數人來說，一天走三十分鐘就夠了，就連從沒做過運動的人也一樣。

你不需要球、球場或特別的衣服，只需要一雙好的運動鞋。有些人明明可以搭計程車，卻走路去火車站；也有人在公司走樓梯。這樣一來，他們既能運動，也能省錢。

如果你覺得走路很無聊，可以和朋友一起走或聽音樂。甚至在客廳跳舞也是很好的開始。

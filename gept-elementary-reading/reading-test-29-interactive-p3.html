NOTICE
City Swimming Pool Rules

City Swimming Pool Rules

  • Open from six in the morning to nine at night.
  • Ticket: 60 dollars. Students pay only a quarter of the price.
  • Do not run beside the pool. Do not bring food or drinks.
  • Beginners must swim in Pool A with a teacher.
  • The city government pays for the water and the lights.

The pool is closed on the first Monday of each month. If you lose something, please call the office number on the wall.

全文中譯

市立游泳池規定

  • 開放時間：早上六點到晚上九點。
  • 票價：60 元。學生只需付四分之一的價錢。
  • 不可在池邊奔跑。不可攜帶食物或飲料。
  • 初學者必須在 A 池由老師陪同游泳。
  • 水費與電費由市政府負擔。

游泳池每月第一個星期一休館。若遺失物品，請撥打牆上的辦公室電話。

ARTICLE
Voting Day in My Country

In our country, people over twenty can vote. Every four years they elect a new president. Before that day, each side talks about its policy on TV and in the streets.

On voting day, most shops are closed. For many people, it is a great day. Fathers and mothers take their children with them. The children cannot vote, but they can watch and learn.

At night, everybody sits in front of the TV and waits for the numbers.

全文中譯

在我們國家，二十歲以上的人可以投票。每四年他們選出一位新總統。在投票日之前，每一方都會在電視上和街頭談自己的政策。

投票當天，大部分的店都關門。對許多人來說，那是重要的一天。爸爸媽媽會帶著孩子一起去。小孩不能投票，但可以在旁邊看、在旁邊學。

到了晚上，大家都坐在電視機前等開票數字。

EMAIL
An Invitation to a Talk

Dear Mr. Chen,

I am a student at Central Junior High School. Our English club will hold a talk next month, and we hope you can come.

You are a diplomat, and you have worked in Korea, China and Britain. Many of us want to know what your job is like.

The talk is on May 8, from two to three in the afternoon. We can pay you a small fee.

Please let us know before April 20.

Lisa Wang

全文中譯

親愛的陳先生：

我是中央國中的學生。我們英語社下個月要舉辦一場演講，希望您能來。

您是一位外交官，曾在韓國、中國和英國工作。我們很多人想知道您的工作是什麼樣子。

演講在 5 月 8 日下午兩點到三點。我們可以支付您一筆小額費用。

請在 4 月 20 日前告訴我們您的答覆。

王莉莎

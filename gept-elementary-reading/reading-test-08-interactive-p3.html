全文中譯

星光有線電視——全新春季方案！

  • 基本型——80 個頻道，每月新台幣 399 元
  • 家庭型——150 個頻道加免費電影，每月新台幣 599 元

每一戶新用戶都可獲得免費機上盒，我們的師傅一小時內就幫你把纜線裝好。

您可以隨時撥打 0800-123-456，或寄電子郵件給我們。如果電話忙線中，請留言，我們會在十分鐘內回電給您。

Questions 21–24
21How much does the Family plan cost each month?
ANT$80
BNT$150
CNT$399
DNT$599
✔ 正確答案：(D)　NT$599
中譯家庭型方案每個月要多少錢？
解析廣告寫 Family — 150 channels and free movies for NT$599 a month，每月 599 元。NT$399 是 Basic 方案；80 和 150 是頻道數，不是價錢。
22What does every new house get?
AA free magazine
BA free phone
CA free box
DTen free movies
✔ 正確答案：(C)　A free box
中譯每一戶新用戶可以得到什麼？
解析Every new house gets a free box。免費電影是家庭型方案的內容，不是每戶都送；文中沒提到送電話或雜誌。
23How long does it take to put in the cable?
ATen minutes
BOne hour
CTwo hours
DOne day
✔ 正確答案：(B)　One hour
中譯裝設纜線要花多久時間？
解析our workers will put in the cable in one hour。ten minutes 是忙線後回電的時間，不是裝機時間。
24What should you do when the line is busy?
ALeave a message
BGo to the office
CCall another day
DSend a letter
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Leave a message
中譯電話忙線時你應該怎麼做？
解析If the line is busy, please leave a message. We will call you back in ten minutes。所以是留言等回電，不必改天再打或寫信。
EMAIL
A Letter to a Journalist

Dear Mr. Lin,

I am a student at Green Junior High School. Our class has a school newspaper, and I write for it. Next month we want to make a report about your job as a journalist.

Can we visit your office on May 18? We would like to ask you about ten questions. If that day is not good, please tell us another day. Is there anything else we should bring?

You can call my teacher at 04-2211-3366, or send an email to me.

Thank you,
Kevin Chang

全文中譯

親愛的林先生：

我是綠園國中的學生。我們班有一份校刊，我負責寫稿。下個月我們想做一篇關於您新聞記者這份工作的報導。

我們可以在 5 月 18 日到您的辦公室拜訪嗎？我們想請教您大約十個問題。如果那天不方便，請告訴我們另一個日子。還有其他我們該帶的東西嗎？

您可以打 04-2211-3366 給我的老師，或寄電子郵件給我。

謝謝您
張凱文

Questions 25–28
25Why does Kevin write this email?
ATo ask for a visit
BTo sell a newspaper
CTo say sorry to Mr. Lin
DTo buy a magazine
✔ 正確答案：(A)　To ask for a visit
中譯凱文為什麼寫這封電子郵件？
解析信中說 Next month we want to make a report... Can we visit your office on May 18?，目的是請求前往採訪。信裡沒有賣報紙、道歉或買雜誌的意思。
26What does Kevin do at school?
AHe writes for the school newspaper
BHe teaches English
CHe sells snacks
DHe works at a TV station
✔ 正確答案：(A)　He writes for the school newspaper
中譯凱文在學校做什麼？
解析Our class has a school newspaper, and I write for it。在電視台工作的是林先生那一類的新聞工作者，不是凱文。
27What is Mr. Lin's job?
AA teacher
BA waiter
CA doctor
DA journalist
✔ 正確答案：(D)　A journalist
中譯林先生的工作是什麼？
解析a report about your job as a journalist 直接點出林先生是新聞記者。teacher 指的是凱文的老師。
28What does Kevin ask Mr. Lin to do if May 18 is not good?
ACall his mother
BCome to the school
CTell them another day
DWrite a report
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Tell them another day
中譯如果 5 月 18 日不方便，凱文請林先生怎麼做？
解析If that day is not good, please tell us another day。要打電話是給老師而不是媽媽；報導是學生要寫的。

32What must students do before they enter the news room?
ATurn off the phone
BBuy a ticket

DBring some drinks

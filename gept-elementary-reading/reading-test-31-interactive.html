ARTICLE
Thunder and Lightning

Imagine you are outside and a storm begins. Thunder and lightning often come together in summer. In fact, they happen at the same time, but we see the lightning first because light travels faster than sound.

Dark clouds usually mean that rain is coming. Lightning is very hot and can be dangerous. When you hear thunder, go inside quickly. Do not stand under a tall tree, and do not touch anything electric.

Many people are careless during a storm. A few minutes indoors can keep you safe.

全文中譯

想像你正在外面，然後暴風雨開始了。夏天打雷和閃電常常一起出現。事實上，它們是同時發生的，但我們會先看到閃電，因為光傳得比聲音快。

烏雲通常表示要下雨了。閃電非常熱，可能很危險。聽到雷聲時要趕快進到室內。不要站在高大的樹下，也不要碰任何電器。

很多人在暴風雨中很粗心。進到室內幾分鐘，就能保護你的安全。

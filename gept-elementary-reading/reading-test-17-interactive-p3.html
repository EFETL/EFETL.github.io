Messages and a Bus Ticket

Ann: I am taking the bus to Hualian tonight. Mom asked me to send you this.

NORTHERN BUS · Line 3
Taipei to Hualian
Leaves 20:10 Arrives 23:40
Seat 14A · Gate 2
Student 360

Ken: That is a long ride. Is there a restroom on the bus?

Ann: Yes, at the back. The bus also stops once at a rest area near the tunnel.

Ken: Good. Send me a message when you get on, and again when you arrive.

全文中譯

安：我今晚要搭公車去花蓮。媽媽要我把這個傳給你。

北方客運 · 3 線
台北往花蓮
20:10 發車　23:40 抵達
14A 座 · 2 號門
學生票　360

肯：那是一段很長的車程。車上有廁所嗎？

安：有，在後面。公車也會在隧道附近的休息站停一次。

肯：好。上車時傳訊息給我，到了再傳一次。

Questions 19–21

AThree hours and thirty minutes

✔ 正確答案：(A)　Three hours and thirty minutes

解析20:10 發車、23:40 抵達，共三小時三十分鐘。

Sun Road will be closed from June 1 to June 20.

  • Cars: use the highway or the road north of the park
  • Buses 12 and 30: the stop moves to the parking lot beside the bank
  • People walking: use the overpass, not the underpass

Work will stop between noon and one o'clock so that the street is quiet while the school next door eats lunch. Please call 04-2211-3388 if the noise is a problem at night.

中午十二點到一點會停工，讓隔壁學校用餐時街道保持安靜。若夜間噪音造成困擾，請電 04-2211-3388。

EMAIL
A Note to the Bus Company

Dear Sir,

I ride Bus 12 to work every day, and I want to tell you about your driver on the 7:40 bus, Mr. Chang.

Last Tuesday an old man got on with a heavy bag and could not find his card. He had a toothache and looked pale. The bus was full and already late. Mr. Chang did not hurry him. He told the man to sit down first and pay the next day, and he asked a young passenger to give up a seat. Nobody said a word, but I saw three people smile.

I know you hear about drivers only when something goes wrong. I thought you should hear this too. Please pass my thanks to him.

Sincerely, Wang Li

全文中譯

敬啟者：

我每天搭 12 路公車上班，想告訴您關於 7:40 那班車的司機張先生的事。

上星期二，一位老先生提著很重的袋子上車，卻找不到他的卡。他牙痛，臉色蒼白。車上很滿，而且已經誤點。張先生沒有催他。他請老先生先坐下，隔天再付錢，並請一位年輕乘客讓座。沒有人說話，但我看到三個人露出微笑。

我知道您通常只有在出事時才會聽到關於司機的事。我覺得這件事您也應該知道。請代我向他致謝。

敬上，王莉

ARTICLE
The Road Is Never Only a Road

A road looks like a simple thing: a flat line that takes you from one place to another. The longest one in Russia runs for thousands of kilometers, and its story is the story of a country. But every road is also a choice about who matters.

When a city makes a road wider, cars move faster, and the people who live along it cross less often. Children stop playing outside and stay in the living room. An old woman may keep herself at home for days. The road has not become worse for driving; it has become worse for living.

Some cities now work the other way. They make one road narrow again, plant a line of flowers, and let buses and bicycles use the middle. Cars still pass, only more slowly. The truth is that a street belongs to everyone who stands on it, not only to the person behind the wheel.

全文中譯

道路看起來很單純：一條把你從一個地方帶到另一個地方的平直線。俄羅斯最長的一條綿延數千公里，它的故事就是一個國家的故事。但每一條路也是一個關於「誰重要」的選擇。

當城市把路拓寬，車子開得更快，住在路旁的人就更少穿越。孩子不再到外面玩，只待在客廳。老太太可能好幾天把自己關在家裡。這條路對開車來說沒有變差，卻對生活變差了。

有些城市現在反其道而行。他們把一條路再度變窄，種上一排花，讓公車和腳踏車使用中間。車子仍然通行，只是慢一些。事實是，街道屬於每一個站在上面的人，而不只是方向盤後面的那一位。

✔ 正確答案：(B)　A road should serve everyone, not only cars

解析the people who live along it cross less often，孩子也不再到外面玩。文中明說對開車並沒有變差。

NOTICE
Road Work on Chung Shan Road

Chung Shan Road will be closed from noon on May 5 to May 20. Workers will build a new overpass near the park.

Bus 12 and Bus 30 will not stop at the school stop during this time. Please take Bus 18 instead. It stops in front of the bookstore.

Drivers, please use the highway or the small road behind the market. Do not park your car in front of the tunnel.

We are sorry for the trouble.

全文中譯

中山路將自 5 月 5 日中午起封閉至 5 月 20 日。工人將在公園附近興建一座新的天橋。

這段期間 12 路和 30 路公車不會停靠學校站。請改搭 18 路公車，它停在書店前面。

各位駕駛人，請改走公路或市場後面的小路。請勿把車停在隧道前面。

造成不便，敬請見諒。

EMAIL
Coming to See You

Hi Grandma,

I will visit you next Saturday. I am going to take the train because the highway is always full of cars on weekends.

My train leaves Taipei at 8:20 in the morning and arrives at 11:00. Please do not come to the station. I can walk to your house from there. It only takes ten minutes.

Mother says she will make your favorite fish soup that day. I will bring some flowers, too. I will call you tonight after dinner.

Love,
Anna

ARTICLE
Bicycles in the City

More and more people in Taiwan ride bicycles to work. A bicycle is cheap, and it does not make the air dirty. It is also good for your health.

Our city is proud of its new bike paths along the river. In the northern part of the city, the paths are even wider. Riders do not need to share the road with cars, so the ride is safe and quiet.

Of course, there are problems. After a heavy rain, riding is not fun. And a flat tire is a big problem on a busy day. Still, most riders say they will keep riding.

全文中譯

臺灣越來越多人騎腳踏車上班。腳踏車很便宜，也不會汙染空氣，而且對健康有益。

我們的城市以沿著河邊的新自行車道為傲。在城市的北邊，車道甚至更寬。騎士不必和汽車共用道路，所以騎起來又安全又安靜。

當然，也有問題。下大雨過後騎車就不好玩了。忙碌的日子裡爆胎也是個大麻煩。儘管如此，大多數騎士還是說他們會繼續騎。

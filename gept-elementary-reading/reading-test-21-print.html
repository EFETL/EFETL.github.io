What Our Clothes Tell Us

What people wear tells us a lot. In cold countries, people wear heavy coats and hats in winter. Near the shore, people often wear light shirts because of the warm wind.

Clothes also change with time. Fifty years ago, few women in Taiwan wore trousers to work. In recent years, many young people use clothes to show who they are.

Some people think fashion is only for the rich. But a clean shirt and a big smile can make anyone look nice.

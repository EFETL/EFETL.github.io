21When can people take the tennis class?
AOn Saturday morning

23How much does a 14-year-old student pay for one month?

Good News from Ben

Dear Mike,

Our volleyball team won the game last Friday! We played against a big school, and the score was 25 to 21. We practiced every day after school for two months.

Our best player is Sandy. She is not tall, but she jumps very well. The next match is on May 8 at the city gym. Can you come and cheer for us?

Your friend,
Ben

我們最厲害的球員是珊蒂。她不高，但她跳得很好。下一場比賽在五月八日，地點在市立體育館。你可以來為我們加油嗎？

你的朋友

ARTICLE
Walking Is Enough

Many people think they have no time for sports. In fact, walking is good exercise, and it is free. Doctors say thirty minutes of walking a day is enough for most people.

You do not need a ball, a court, or a gym. You only need a pair of good sneakers. Some people walk to the train station instead of taking a taxi. This way, they stay healthy and save money, too.

全文中譯

很多人以為自己沒有時間運動。其實走路就是很好的運動，而且不用花錢。醫生說，大多數人一天走三十分鐘就夠了。

你不需要球、球場或體育館。你只需要一雙好球鞋。有些人不搭計程車，改成走路到火車站。這樣一來，他們既保持健康，也省下了錢。

Questions 29–32
29What is this article mainly about?
ASports are only for young people
BTaxis are cheaper than trains
CWalking is an easy and good exercise
DDoctors do not like sports
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Walking is an easy and good exercise
中譯這篇文章主要在談什麼？
解析全文都在說走路免費、簡單，是很好的運動。
30How long should most people walk each day?
ATwo hours
BThree hours
CThirty minutes
DTen minutes
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Thirty minutes
中譯大多數人每天應該走多久？
解析Doctors say thirty minutes of walking a day is enough。
31What do people need for walking?
AA pair of good sneakers
BA gym near their house
CA car and a driver
DA ball and a court
✔ 正確答案：(A)　A pair of good sneakers
中譯走路運動需要什麼？
解析You only need a pair of good sneakers，前一句還說不需要球、球場或體育館。
32What is another good point of walking to the train station?
APeople can drive faster
BPeople can save money
CPeople can sleep more
DPeople can eat more
✔ 正確答案：(B)　People can save money

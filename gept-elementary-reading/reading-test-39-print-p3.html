A Letter to a Neighbor

Dear Mr. Lin,

Thank you for helping my mother last Saturday. She could not carry the heavy boxes to the sixth floor. You came at once, and you did not take any money.

My mother wants to say sorry, too. Our dog barked all night and woke you up. She will keep him inside after ten.

We will have a small party next Sunday. You are very welcome to come.

Yours,
Amy Chen

Life in a Big City

Taichung is one of the biggest cities in Taiwan. Its population is now more than two million. Fifty years ago, most people there were farmers. Today many of them work in factories, shops and offices.

A big city gives people more jobs, but life there is not always easy. Houses are expensive, and the roads are busy. Some poor families live in very small rooms. For them, city life is not as easy as it looks. Social workers say the city must help them more.

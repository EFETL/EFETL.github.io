Passage 1
A Letter to My Old School

Dear Mr. Lin, I left your class ten years ago, but I still remember the (11) you taught me about being honest, and I still remember your smile. At that time I was a slender boy who sat in the back row and said nothing. (12) You waited for me after school, and you never once blamed me. Today I study education at a university, and I aim to become a teacher too. (13), I will go back to the campus and thank you in person on Teacher's Day. I know you are busy, (14) I hope you can give me ten minutes.

全文中譯親愛的林老師：我離開您的班級已經十年了，但我仍然記得您教我關於誠實的那一課，也仍然記得您的笑容。當時我是個坐在後排、什麼都不說的瘦弱男孩。您總是先問我有什麼想法。您放學後等我，而且從來沒有責備過我。今天我在大學讀教育，我的目標也是成為一位老師。最後，我會在教師節回到校園當面向您道謝。我知道您很忙，但我希望您能為我空出十分鐘。

The School Garden Project

Our class started a garden (15) on the empty ground behind the garage last season. Each group chose one plant and had to water it every morning before the first lesson, so we all got some fresh air. At first the work looked easy. (16) The ground was hard, and some plants died in the cold. Mr. Chen told us that nature does not follow our plan, and that a good gardener must wait. (17), we picked our first lemons in May. Everyone got an equal share, (18) the two students who had watered the plants every single day got one more.

全文中譯上一季，我們班在車庫後面的空地開始了一個花園專題。每一組選一種植物，必須在第一堂課前每天早上澆水，所以我們都呼吸到一些新鮮空氣。一開始這工作看起來很容易。但我們很快就發現自己錯了。土很硬，有些植物在寒冷中死掉了。陳老師告訴我們，大自然不會照著我們的計畫走，好的園丁必須懂得等待。最後，我們在五月摘下第一批檸檬。每個人都分到相同的份量，但那兩位每天都澆水的同學多拿了一份。

ARTICLE
Why We Still Need Teachers

Today a student can find almost any fact anywhere, on a phone, in a few seconds. Some people therefore guess that schools will soon close and that no one will need a teacher.

But a fact is not the same as an education. A machine can tell you the year of a war, yet it cannot see that you are afraid to speak, or notice that you have not eaten supper. A good teacher watches the person, not only the answer; that is the key to a real education, and no online course can do it alone.

Learning is also something we do together. In class we hear other students say things we never thought of, and we learn to listen even when we do not agree. That is a lesson no phone can deliver.

全文中譯

今天學生不論身在何處，只要幾秒鐘就能在手機上找到幾乎任何事實。因此有些人猜測學校很快就會關閉，再也沒有人需要老師。

但事實不等於教育。機器可以告訴你一場戰爭發生在哪一年，卻看不出你害怕開口，也不會注意到你沒吃晚餐。好老師看的是人，不只是答案；那才是真正教育的關鍵，任何線上課程都無法獨力做到。

學習也是我們一起做的事。在課堂上我們會聽到其他同學說出我們從沒想過的看法，也學會在不同意時仍然傾聽。那是手機無法給予的一課。

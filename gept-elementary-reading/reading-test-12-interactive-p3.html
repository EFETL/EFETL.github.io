EMAIL
Life at the University

Dear Amy,

How are you? I started my first semester at the university last month. About ten thousand students study on this campus, and it takes me ten minutes to walk to my classroom.

I take six courses this year. My favorite one is about sea animals, and the professor often shows us videos of dolphins. There is an old wooden desk in my room, and my roommate is tall and slender. We study together at night.

Come and visit me in the winter.

Ted

全文中譯

你好嗎？我上個月開始了大學的第一個學期。這個校園裡大約有一萬名學生就讀，我要走十分鐘才到教室。

我今年修六門課。我最喜歡的一門是關於海洋動物的，教授常常放海豚的影片給我們看。我房間裡有一張舊的木頭書桌，我的室友又高又苗條。我們晚上會一起讀書。

冬天來看我吧。

泰德

ARTICLE
Study Less, Learn More

Many students study all night before an exam. They think more hours mean better grades. However, this is not always true.

Doctors say sleep is important for the brain. A tired student cannot remember new lessons well. Many students struggle with math, but the answer is not staying up late. It is better to learn a little every day.

Fresh air helps, too. Open the window, or take a short walk outside. The goal of studying is a clear mind, not a long night.

全文中譯

許多學生在考試前熬夜整晚讀書。他們以為時間愈多、成績就愈好。然而，事情並非總是如此。

醫生說睡眠對大腦很重要。疲倦的學生記不住新的課程內容。很多學生對數學感到吃力，但答案並不是熬夜。每天讀一點才是比較好的方法。

新鮮空氣也有幫助。打開窗戶，或到外面短短散個步。讀書的目標是頭腦清楚，而不是熬過漫漫長夜。

NOTICE
Safety Rules for the School Trip

We will go to Green Mountain next Friday. Please read these rules.

  • Stay with your group. Do not walk away alone.
  • The path near the river is dangerous. Do not go there.
  • Bring a hat, water and enough food.
  • If you feel dizzy or sick, tell your teacher at once.

We will meet at the school gate at seven in the morning. Students who are late must stay at school.

全文中譯

下星期五我們要去青山。請閱讀以下規定。

  • 跟著你的小組，不要一個人走開。
  • 河邊的小路很危險，不要過去。
  • 請帶帽子、水和足夠的食物。
  • 如果覺得頭暈或不舒服，請馬上告訴老師。

我們早上七點在校門口集合。遲到的學生必須留在學校。

EMAIL
Are You All Right?

Dear Ken,

Are you all right? My mother told me about the accident on your street. She said a jeep hit a wall and a tree fell down. Luckily, nobody was badly hurt. The police arrived in ten minutes.

My father says the corner is dangerous because cars drive too fast there. Everyone in our town should write to the city office about it.

Please call me this evening. Would you like to meet at the park on Sunday?

Your friend,
Lisa

全文中譯

親愛的阿肯：

你還好嗎？我媽媽跟我說了你們那條街上的意外。她說一台吉普車撞上牆，還有一棵樹倒了。幸好沒有人受重傷。警察十分鐘內就到了。

我爸爸說那個轉角很危險，因為車子在那裡開得太快。我們鎮上的每個人都應該寫信給市政府反映這件事。

今天傍晚請打電話給我。星期天你想不想在公園碰面？

你的朋友
麗莎

ARTICLE
The Work of the Police

Every big city needs police. Policemen work day and night to keep people safe. When someone robs a shop, they must find the person and arrest him. The man may then go to prison for a few years.

But police work is not only about danger. Policemen do much more. They help old people cross the road and give first aid to people who are hurt. Many children think they are real heroes and want to thank them.

全文中譯

每個大城市都需要警察。警察日夜工作，保護人們的安全。有人搶了商店，他們就必須找出那個人並逮捕他。那個人可能因此坐幾年牢。

但警察的工作不只和危險有關，他們做的事情多得多。他們幫老人過馬路，也為受傷的人做急救。許多小朋友覺得他們是真正的英雄，想向他們道謝。

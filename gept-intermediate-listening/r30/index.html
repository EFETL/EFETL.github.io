答案：B
What is the speaker's conclusion?講者的結論是什麼？
not what is oldest but what people still enjoy doing together。convention 是主題字。

W: Customs change more quickly than people admit. Fifty years ago, a funeral here lasted three days and the whole street took part; today it is usually one afternoon, and neighbours send a message instead of attending. The lantern parade went the other way: it nearly died out in the nineteen eighties, then grew larger than ever once the town began to advertise it. What survives, it seems, is not what is oldest but what people still enjoy doing together.習俗改變的速度比人們願意承認的更快。五十年前，這裡的葬禮要辦三天，整條街都會參與；今天通常只有一個下午，鄰居改用訊息表達心意，而不是到場。燈籠遊行則走了相反的路：它在一九八〇年代幾乎消失，等到鎮上開始宣傳後，反而比以往更盛大。看來能存續下來的，不是最古老的，而是人們仍樂於一起做的事。

34 （題目由錄音播出，作答時請用聽的）
答案：A
What should readers always ask for?讀者應該永遠要求什麼？
Always ask for the range。proportion、quantity 都是主題字。

W: Averages deserve more suspicion than they get. If nine people earn twenty thousand and one earns two hundred thousand, the average is thirty-eight thousand, and it describes nobody in the room. The middle value would be twenty thousand, which describes nine of them. So when a report gives you an average without the spread, it has told you a number but not a fact. Always ask for the range, or at least the highest and the lowest.平均值值得比現在更多的懷疑。若九個人賺兩萬、一個人賺二十萬，平均是三萬八，而它描述不了在場的任何人。中位數會是兩萬，那描述了其中九個人。所以當一份報告只給你平均值卻沒有分布時，它給了你一個數字，但不是一個事實。永遠要問全距，或至少問最高與最低。

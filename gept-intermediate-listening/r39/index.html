34 （題目由錄音播出，作答時請用聽的）
答案：B
What advice does the speaker give?講者給了什麼建議？
keep the shifts short, keep the group stable。stable 是本回單字。

W: People assume volunteers give up their time out of pure sacrifice, but the research says something more useful. Those who stay longest are the ones who get something back: a skill, a friendship, a routine that gives the week shape. Volunteers who are thanked in general terms drift away; those who are told exactly what their work achieved stay. So if you run a project, keep the shifts short, keep the group stable, and tell people the number they fed by name of the day.人們以為志工付出時間純粹是犧牲，但研究指出更有用的事實。待得最久的，是那些也有所得的人：一項技能、一段友誼、一個讓一週有節奏的固定行程。只被籠統道謝的志工會漸漸離開；被明確告知自己的付出達成了什麼的人會留下來。所以若你經營一個計畫，班次要短、團隊要穩定，並且要告訴大家他們那天餵飽了多少人。

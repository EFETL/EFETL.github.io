答案：B
What does a good scientist value most?好的科學家最重視什麼？
a finding that keeps coming back。scientific 是主題字。

W: I want to explain why one experiment is never enough. A single result can look convincing and still be wrong, because chance alone produces surprising numbers now and then. That is why we repeat the work, and why other laboratories repeat it too. If a result cannot be produced twice, it is not evidence; it is a story. A good scientist is therefore less interested in a striking finding than in a finding that keeps coming back.我想說明為什麼一次實驗永遠不夠。單一結果看起來可能很有說服力卻仍然是錯的，因為光靠機率偶爾就會產生令人驚訝的數字。這就是我們要重複實驗、也要讓其他實驗室重複的原因。若一個結果無法被做出第二次，它就不是證據，而是故事。因此好的科學家對驚人的發現不那麼感興趣，反而更在意一再重現的發現。

34 （題目由錄音播出，作答時請用聽的）
答案：D
What does the speaker recommend?講者建議什麼？
spends money where the numbers are, not where the fear is。risk 是本回主題字。

W: People judge risk badly, and crime is the clearest example. Murder is rare and reported everywhere, so it feels common; theft is common and reported nowhere, so it feels rare. Ask people what they fear and they name the first; ask what has actually happened to them and they describe the second. Good prevention therefore spends money where the numbers are, not where the fear is, which is why street lighting does more good than any camera.人們判斷風險的能力很差，犯罪就是最明顯的例子。謀殺很罕見卻到處被報導，所以感覺很常見；竊盜很常見卻沒人報導，所以感覺很罕見。問人們害怕什麼，他們說前者；問他們實際遇過什麼，他們描述後者。因此好的預防措施把錢花在數字所在之處，而不是恐懼所在之處，這就是為什麼路燈的效益大於任何監視器。

答案：D
What is the speaker's main point?講者的重點是什麼？
none of them is fixed by donating tins of food。donate、wealth 都是本回主題字。

W: People often picture poverty as an empty cupboard, but in a wealthy country it usually looks like a full timetable. If you have no car, a job across town costs you two hours a day. If you cannot pay a deposit, you rent by the week at a higher rate. If you have no savings, a broken washing machine becomes a loan. Each of these is a tax on being poor, and none of them is fixed by donating tins of food.人們常把貧窮想像成空蕩的櫥櫃，但在富裕國家，它通常看起來像一張排滿的時間表。若你沒有車，一份在城市另一頭的工作每天要花掉你兩小時。若你付不出押金，你就得按週租，租金更高。若你沒有存款，一台壞掉的洗衣機就變成一筆貸款。這每一項都是對貧窮的課稅，而沒有一項能靠捐罐頭解決。

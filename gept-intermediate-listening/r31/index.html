31 （題目由錄音播出，作答時請用聽的）
答案：B
What is the speaker's main point?講者的重點是什麼？
very often a nineteenth or twentieth century idea of ancient。civilization 是主題字。

M: This monument has been rebuilt three times, and each version tells us more about its own age than about the first builders. The original was wood and burned down. The second was stone but far larger, because the town had grown prosperous and wanted to show it. The third, which you see now, is a careful copy of the second, put up after the flood of 1954. What we call ancient is very often a nineteenth or twentieth century idea of ancient.這座紀念碑重建過三次，而每一個版本告訴我們的，比起最初的建造者，更多是關於它自己那個時代。最初的是木造，後來燒毀。第二座是石造但大得多，因為當時鎮上變得繁榮，想要展現出來。第三座，也就是各位現在看到的，是一九五四年水災後依第二座仔細複製的。我們所稱的古老，往往是十九或二十世紀對古老的想像。

34 （題目由錄音播出，作答時請用聽的）
答案：B
What does the speaker say about the work?講者怎麼形容這些工作？
it only slows what is still going。restore 是本回衍生字。

W: A note about how we look after the painted ceiling. Light is the enemy, so the hall stays dim and no flash is permitted. Damp is the second enemy: after the flooding two winters ago we installed pumps, and the walls are measured every month. Visitors themselves are the third. Each person brings warmth and moisture, so we now limit the room to thirty people at a time. None of this restores what is already lost; it only slows what is still going.說明我們如何維護那片彩繪天花板。光是敵人，所以大殿保持昏暗，禁用閃光燈。濕氣是第二個敵人：兩個冬天前的水災之後我們裝了抽水泵，牆面每月測量一次。第三個敵人是遊客自己。每個人都帶來熱與濕氣，所以我們現在限制室內一次三十人。這些都無法讓已經失去的復原；只能減緩仍在進行的損壞。

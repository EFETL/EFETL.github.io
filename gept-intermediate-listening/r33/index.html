34 （題目由錄音播出，作答時請用聽的）
答案：B
What number should readers look at?讀者該看哪個數字？
not the lead but the share who are still undecided。politics 是本回主題字。

W: Polls are useful and easy to misread. A poll does not predict an election; it measures opinion on the day it was taken, among the people who answered. If a poll was carried out before a televised debate, it tells you nothing about the effect of that debate. And a lead of two points, in a poll of a thousand people, is smaller than the poll's own margin of error. The number to look at is not the lead but the share who are still undecided.民調有用，也很容易被誤讀。民調不是預測選舉；它測量的是進行當天、在受訪者之中的意見。若民調是在電視辯論前做的，它就完全說明不了那場辯論的效果。而在一千人的民調中，兩個百分點的領先小於該民調本身的誤差範圍。真正該看的數字不是領先幅度，而是仍未決定者的比例。

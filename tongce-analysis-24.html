📖 A. 海洋塑膠垃圾與減塑吸管政策第 21–24 題　點任一句展開解析
全篇大意　本篇說明海洋塑膠汙染：塑膠碎裂成微塑膠進入食物鏈，海龜誤食受害；台灣限制塑膠吸管、業者跟進減塑，雖起初不受歡迎，卻逐漸改變消費習慣。文體為說明文，因果與對比（Whereas／As a result）交錯。
本篇答案21put22because23limited24can keep our oceans clean and safe for life
▶A-1Every year, a huge amount of plastic waste flows into the ocean.
每年都有大量的塑膠廢棄物流入海洋。
🔍 句構分析①Every year修飾語頻率片語
②a huge amount of plastic wasteS 主詞主詞；a huge amount of + 不可數名詞
③flowsV 動詞不及物動詞，第三人稱單數
④into the ocean修飾語介系詞片語表方向
文法重點　a huge amount of 只能接不可數名詞（waste），接可數要用 a huge number of；動詞單複數看 amount（單數）。
📚 單字a huge amount of phr. 大量的（接不可數）plastic n. 塑膠waste n. 廢棄物flow vi. 流動
▶A-2Scientists estimate that plastic now reaches even the deepest and most remote corners of the sea.
科學家估計，塑膠如今甚至已抵達海洋最深、最偏遠的角落。
🔍 句構分析①ScientistsS 主詞主詞
②estimateV 動詞及物動詞
③that plastic now reaches even the deepest and most remote corners of the seaO 受詞that 名詞子句作受詞
④S: plastic / V: reaches / O: the deepest and most remote corners└ 子句兩個最高級並列，共用一個 the
文法重點　短形容詞最高級加 -est（deepest），長形容詞用 most（most remote）；兩個最高級並列時 the 只需一個。
📚 單字estimate vt. 估計reach vt. 抵達remote adj. 偏遠的corner n. 角落
▶A-3第 21 題Plastic straws, bags, and bottles float on the water and 21put sea animals in great danger.

🔍 句構分析①Plastic straws, bags, and bottlesS 主詞三個名詞並列作主詞
②floatV 動詞不及物動詞；on the water 為介片
③putV 動詞第二個動詞，與 float 並列共用主詞
④sea animalsO 受詞受詞
⑤in great danger修飾語put sb./sth. in danger「使…陷入危險」
文法重點　空格 21 選 put：put sb./sth. in (great) danger 為固定搭配；and 連接的兩動詞時態須一致（float／put 皆現在式原形）。
📚 單字straw n. 吸管float vi. 漂浮put…in danger phr. 使…陷入危險
▶A-4Over time, sunlight and waves break these items into tiny fragments known as microplastics.

🔍 句構分析①Over time修飾語時間片語「久而久之」

④these itemsO 受詞受詞

📚 單字over time phr. 久而久之wave n. 海浪item n. 物品fragment n. 碎片known as phr. 被稱為

③can travelV 動詞情態助動詞 + 原形；up the food chain 為介片

文法重點　空格 26 選 but：not only… but also… 為固定關聯連接詞，前後結構須對等（good for… ↔ fun to do…）。

🔍 句構分析①Beyond cleaning the shore修飾語beyond 為介系詞，後接動名詞

文法重點　介系詞（beyond／about）後接動名詞或名詞子句；名詞子句用直述句語序（where…waste ends up）。

▶B-8第 27 題Some of the trash they collect can be recycled and 27turned into new products, such as bags or chairs.

③can be recycledV 動詞情態助動詞的被動：can be + p.p.
④(can be) turned intoV 動詞與 recycled 並列，共用 can be，故須填過去分詞
⑤new products, such as bags or chairs修飾語such as 舉例

▶B-9Whereas the old economy simply threw things away, a circular model treats waste as a valuable resource.

🔍 句構分析①Whereas the old economy simply threw things away副詞子句S: the old economy / V: threw away / O: things
②a circular modelS 主詞主詞

⑤as a valuable resource修飾語treat A as B「把 A 視為 B」
文法重點　treat／regard／view A as B 都用 as；throw away 為可分片語動詞，受詞為代名詞時須放中間（throw them away）。

▶B-11This idea is called a circular economy, in which things are used again and again.

🔍 句構分析①This ideaS 主詞主詞
②is calledV 動詞被動語態
③a circular economyC 補語主詞補語（由 call O OC 轉來）
④in which things are used again and again關係子句「介系詞 + 關代」＝ where；補述用法
文法重點　in which＝where，用於指「範圍、情境之中」；補述用法的關代不可用 that、不可省略。

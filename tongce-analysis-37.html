④two-step verificationO 受詞受詞
⑤which adds an extra layer of protection to your accounts關係子句補述用法，先行詞為 verification
文法重點　whenever／if + possible 可省略「主詞 + be」；補述用法的關代不可用 that。
📚 單字whenever conj. 每當；只要turn on phr. 開啟verification n. 驗證extra adj. 額外的layer n. 層
▶B-8第 27 題It is also a good idea to use a strong password 27that mixes both letters and numbers, and to change it from time to time.
使用英數混合的強密碼、並不時更換，也是個好主意。
🔍 句構分析①ItS 主詞虛主詞
②is also a good ideaV/Cbe 動詞 + 補語
③to use a strong password … and to change it from time to time真主詞兩個不定詞並列
④that mixes both letters and numbers關係子句空格 27；主格關代修飾 password
文法重點　空格 27 選 that：先行詞 password 為「物」且關代在子句中當主詞，用 that／which（不可用 who）。from time to time「不時」。
📚 單字idea n. 主意strong adj. 強的mix vt. 混合letter n. 字母from time to time phr. 不時
▶B-9Reusing the same password across many websites, security experts warn, is one of the most dangerous habits of all.

🔍 句構分析①Reusing the same password across many websitesS 主詞動名詞片語作主詞，視為單數
②security experts warn插入插入語，前後加逗號
③is / one of the most dangerous habits of allV/Cone of + 最高級 + 複數名詞
文法重點　動名詞主詞用單數動詞（is）；插入語不影響主詞與動詞的一致。
📚 單字reuse vt. 重複使用security n. 資安；安全habit n. 習慣of all phr. 所有之中
▶B-10Moreover, connecting to free public Wi-Fi without care may allow others to watch what you are doing.

🔍 句構分析①Moreover修飾語轉折副詞
②connecting to free public Wi-Fi without careS 主詞動名詞片語作主詞
③may allowV 動詞情態助動詞 + 原形
④others / to watch what you are doingO/OCallow sb. to V；what 名詞子句作 watch 的受詞
文法重點　allow sb. to V；without care＝carelessly（without + 抽象名詞可代替副詞）。

▶B-11Remember, once your data is stolen, getting it back is very hard.

🔍 句構分析①Remember祈使句省略主詞 you，動詞用原形
②once your data is stolen副詞子句once＝「一旦」；被動語態
③getting it backS 主詞動名詞片語作主詞，視為單數
④is / very hardV/Cbe 動詞 + 形容詞
文法重點　once 當連接詞＝「一旦」，子句用現在式代替未來；動名詞主詞用單數動詞。
📚 單字remember vt. 記住once conj. 一旦steal vt. 偷（steal-stole-stolen）get back phr. 取回

②may spendV 動詞情態助動詞 + 原形

④trying to recover their money and repair their damaged reputation修飾語spend + 時間 + V-ing
文法重點　spend + 時間／金錢 + V-ing；damaged 為過去分詞當形容詞「受損的」。

▶B-13Given that threats keep changing, staying informed about the latest scams is just as important as using good software.

🔍 句構分析①Given that threats keep changing副詞子句given that＝「由於」；keep + V-ing

▶B-15第 28 題If you receive a strange email asking for your private information, 28do not click any link inside it right away.

ARTICLE
The Noise Beneath the Waves

The ocean was once thought to be a silent world, but it is actually full of sound. Whales sing to find mates, and dolphins use clicks to locate food in dark water. Because sound travels far underwater, these calls can cross hundreds of kilometers.

Today, however, ships, drills, and underwater engines fill the sea with noise. Scientists say this makes it harder for whales to hear one another, so families may become separated. During the quiet months of 2020, when fewer ships sailed, researchers recorded calmer waters and healthier whale behavior. Slower ships, they argue, would cost little yet help a great deal.

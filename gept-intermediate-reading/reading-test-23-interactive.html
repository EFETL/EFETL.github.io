ARTICLE
The Trouble with Fake Reviews

Before buying anything online, most of us look at the star ratings first. Unfortunately, not every review is honest. Some sellers pay strangers to write glowing comments, while others send small gifts to buyers who promise five stars.

Experts say a few signs can help. Reviews that all appear on the same day, use very similar wording, or praise a product without mentioning a single detail are worth doubting. It is also wise to read the three-star comments, which are often the most balanced.

Shopping platforms delete millions of false reviews every year, yet new ones keep appearing. In the end, a careful reader is still a shopper's best protection.

全文中譯

在網路上買東西之前，我們多數人會先看星等評分。可惜的是，並非每則評論都誠實。有些賣家花錢請陌生人寫下讚不絕口的留言，也有些賣家送小禮物給承諾給五星的買家。

專家說，有幾個跡象可以幫忙判斷。全部在同一天出現、用字非常相似，或是通篇稱讚卻沒提到任何細節的評論，都值得懷疑。此外，讀讀三星評論也是明智之舉，因為它們往往最為持平。

購物平台每年刪除數百萬則假評論，但新的仍不斷冒出。歸根究柢，一位仔細閱讀的讀者，仍是消費者最好的保護。

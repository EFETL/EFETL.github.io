An Invitation to a Science Fair

Dear Ms. Chen,

On behalf of Greenfield Junior High, I would like to invite you to be a guest judge at our annual Science Fair on Saturday, October 18, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. in the school gym.

This year, more than sixty students will present projects on space, robots, and clean energy. As a well-known engineer, your comments would greatly encourage our young scientists. Judges will score the projects and choose three winners. Lunch and a small gift will be provided.

Please reply by October 5 so that we can prepare your name badge. We truly hope you can join us.

Best regards,
David Wu, Science Teacher

ARTICLE
The Deepest Place on Earth

Far beneath the Pacific Ocean lies the Mariana Trench, the deepest known point on our planet. At almost 11,000 meters down, it is deeper than Mount Everest is tall. The water there is freezing cold, and the pressure is strong enough to crush most machines.

For a long time, people thought nothing could live in such a dark, harsh place. Yet modern cameras have shown strange fish and tiny creatures moving along the sea floor. These discoveries remind us that life can survive in surprising conditions, and that much of the ocean is still waiting to be explored.

全文中譯

在太平洋深處，坐落著馬里亞納海溝，這是我們星球上已知最深的地點。它深達將近一萬一千公尺，比聖母峰的高度還深。那裡的海水冰冷刺骨，水壓強大到足以壓垮大多數機器。

長久以來，人們認為在如此黑暗、嚴酷的地方不可能有生物存活。然而現代攝影機卻拍到奇特的魚類與微小生物在海床上移動。這些發現提醒我們，生命能在令人意想不到的環境中存活，而海洋大部分地方仍等待著被探索。

Planet Facts

Students in the astronomy club made a simple chart to compare four planets in our solar system. Study the numbers below.

PlanetMoonsDay Length
Earth124 hours
Mars225 hours
Jupiter9510 hours
Venus05,832 hours

As the chart shows, a “day” is very different from planet to planet. Jupiter spins the fastest, while Venus turns so slowly that one day there lasts months.

全文中譯

天文社的學生製作了一張簡單的表格，比較我們太陽系中的四顆行星。請研究下方的數字。

行星衛星數一天長度
地球124 小時
火星225 小時
木星9510 小時
金星05,832 小時

如表所示，各行星的「一天」差異很大。木星自轉最快，而金星轉得極慢，那裡的一天長達數個月。

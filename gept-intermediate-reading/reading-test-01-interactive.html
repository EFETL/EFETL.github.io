ARTICLE
The Quiet Rise of the Four-Day Week

Several firms in Europe have moved their staff to a four-day week without cutting pay. Early results are encouraging. In one study, a researcher found that output stayed the same while sick leave fell by almost a fifth.

Not every sector can copy the idea. A hospital or a factory cannot simply close on Fridays. Still, offices where work is measured by results rather than by hours have found the change surprisingly easy.

The biggest problem is not productivity but meetings. Firms that succeeded had to cut the number of meetings sharply, and give each remaining one a clear purpose.

全文中譯

歐洲數家公司已將員工改為每週工作四天且不減薪。初步結果令人鼓舞。在一項研究中，一位研究人員發現產出維持不變，病假卻減少了將近五分之一。

並非每個產業都能複製這個做法。醫院或工廠無法週五直接關門。不過，以成果而非工時衡量工作的辦公室，發現這項改變出奇容易。

最大的問題不是生產力，而是會議。成功的公司必須大幅減少會議數量，並讓每一場保留下來的會議都有明確目的。

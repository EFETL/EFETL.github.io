單字sufficient 充足的、足夠的、proper 正確的、恰當的、elastic 有彈性的

The Farming Year

Modern agriculture still follows the seasons, however powerful the machines become. In early spring farmers 11 seeds in fields that have rested all winter, hoping the soil is 12 enough to feed them. If it is not, they add 13, though too much can harm the river nearby. Summer brings weeks of waiting and worrying about rain. Then comes the 14, the busiest month of all, when the whole family works from dawn until dark. Afterwards the grain is dried and stored in the 15 behind the house, and the quiet season begins again for a few short weeks.

✔ 正確答案：(B)　sow
解析sow seeds 是「播種」的固定搭配，且與 in early spring 相符。scrub 刷洗、insert 插入、extend 延長，都不能以 seeds 為受詞。

Bappreciation

Dlocation
✔ 正確答案：(C)　harvest
解析夏天之後、全家從早忙到晚的一個月，指的是「收穫期 harvest」。consequence 後果、appreciation 感激、location 地點，都不是一段農忙時節。
15
Alobby
Bcanoe
Cbarn
Dframe
✔ 正確答案：(C)　barn
解析穀物曬乾後存放在屋後的「穀倉 barn」。lobby 大廳、canoe 獨木舟、frame 框架，都不是儲放穀物的地方。
全文中譯不論機器變得多強大，現代農業仍然依循著季節。初春時，農民在整個冬天都在休養的田裡播種，希望土壤夠肥沃，足以養活這些種子。若土壤不夠肥沃，他們就會施肥，不過施得太多會傷害附近的河流。夏天帶來的是好幾週的等待，以及對雨水的擔憂。接著就是收穫期，一年中最忙的一個月，全家人從天亮做到天黑。之後穀物曬乾，存放在屋後的穀倉裡，短暫的農閒季節又再度開始幾個星期。

ARTICLE
Young Farmers Come Home

For thirty years the young people of Ho-ping left for the cities, and the fields slowly turned to grass. Recently something has changed.

A small number of people in their thirties have come back, but they do not farm the way their grandparents did. They grow fewer kinds of crops and sell them directly to restaurants, which pay far more than a wholesale market. Several of them share one expensive machine instead of each buying one.

Those who have returned admit that the work is hard and the first two years brought almost no income. Still, the village school, which was due to close, now has enough children to stay open for another decade.

全文中譯

三十年來，和平村的年輕人紛紛前往城市，田地慢慢長滿了草。最近情況有了改變。

少數三十多歲的人回來了，但他們務農的方式和祖父母那一輩不同。他們種的作物種類較少，並直接賣給餐廳，價格遠高於批發市場。其中好幾個人共用一台昂貴的機器，而不是各買一台。

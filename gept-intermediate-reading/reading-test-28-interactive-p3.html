Things I Left in the Flat

Hi Ray,

Thanks again for taking the flat. I left a few things that may be useful. The small fridge in the kitchen is only two years old and still works well. There is also a wooden tray on top of it and a clean tablecloth in the second drawer.

The tool kit under the sink belongs to the building, so please leave it there when you move out. The knob on the back-room door sticks a little, but a drop of oil solves it.

My key is with the guard downstairs. Call me if anything is missing.

Best,
Ellen

祝好
艾倫

ARTICLE
A Product That Found Its Own Use

The paper handkerchief began by accident. During the First World War one company made a soft paper to replace cotton in bandages. When the war ended, boxes of the material lay unused in a warehouse.

In the 1920s the firm sold it to women for removing face cream. Buyers, however, kept writing to say that they used the tissue for something else: they blew their noses into it and threw it away.

The company listened, changed its advertisements, and sales rose sharply. Today the paper handkerchief is sold in almost every country, and few users know it was first designed for a battlefield.

全文中譯

紙手帕是意外誕生的。第一次世界大戰期間，某家公司做出一種柔軟的紙，用來取代繃帶裡的棉花。戰爭結束後，一箱箱的材料就閒置在倉庫裡。

一九二○年代，這家公司把它賣給女性用來卸除面霜。然而，購買者不斷來信表示，她們把這種面紙拿來做別的事：擤鼻涕，然後丟掉。

公司聽進去了，改變了廣告內容，銷量因此大幅上升。今天，紙手帕幾乎在每個國家都買得到，卻很少有人知道它最早是為戰場設計的。

詞彙 Vocabulary

請由四個選項中，選出一個最適合句意的字或詞。
1The company decided to its old computer system because it kept breaking down and slowing everyone's work.
Aupgrade
Bdecline
Cassemble
Dimitate
✔ 正確答案：(A)　upgrade
中譯由於舊電腦系統一直當機、拖慢大家的工作，公司決定升級它。
解析由 kept breaking down（一直故障）可知要「升級 upgrade」系統。decline 下降、assemble 組裝、imitate 模仿皆不合句意。
單字upgrade 升級、decline 下降、婉拒、assemble 組裝、集合
2Although the two brothers look almost identical, their personalities are completely .
Aopposite
Bsimilar
Cequal
Dfamiliar
✔ 正確答案：(A)　opposite
中譯雖然這對兄弟長得幾乎一模一樣，他們的個性卻完全相反。
解析Although（雖然）表前後對比：長相相同 vs 個性「相反 opposite」。similar 相似、equal 相等、familiar 熟悉皆不符轉折語氣。
單字identical 完全相同的、opposite 相反的、personality 個性
3The doctor advised the patient to from smoking if he wanted to recover quickly.
Aconsist
Brefrain
Cdepend
Dsuffer
✔ 正確答案：(B)　refrain
中譯醫生建議病人如果想快點康復，就要戒除抽菸。
解析refrain from + V-ing 表「克制、避免做…」。consist of 由…組成、depend on 依賴、suffer from 罹患，皆不搭配此語意。
單字refrain 克制、避免、recover 康復、patient 病人
4The scientist's discovery changed the way people understand the human brain.
Atemporary
Breluctant
Cremarkable
Dprevious
✔ 正確答案：(C)　remarkable
中譯這位科學家非凡的發現，改變了人們對人腦的理解。
解析changed the way people understand 暗示此發現重大，故選「非凡的 remarkable」。temporary 暫時的、reluctant 不情願的、previous 先前的皆不合。
單字remarkable 非凡的、驚人的、discovery 發現、reluctant 不情願的
5Please your seat belt before the airplane takes off; it is required for your safety.
Aremove
Bloosen
Cfasten
Dstretch
✔ 正確答案：(C)　fasten
中譯飛機起飛前請繫好安全帶；這是為了你的安全所必需的。
解析seat belt 安全帶要「繫上 fasten」。loosen 鬆開、remove 移除、stretch 伸展皆與安全帶起飛前的動作相反或無關。
單字fasten 繫緊、扣上、loosen 鬆開、required 必需的
6The manager praised Amy for her attitude; she never gives up even when a task seems impossible.
Apassive
Bnervous
Ccareless
Dpositive
✔ 正確答案：(D)　positive
中譯經理稱讚 Amy 積極的態度；即使任務看似不可能，她也從不放棄。
解析never gives up（從不放棄）對應「積極的 positive」態度。passive 消極的、careless 粗心的、nervous 緊張的皆與稱讚不符。
單字attitude 態度、positive 積極的、正面的、passive 被動的、消極的
7Because of the heavy traffic, the delivery was by almost two hours.
Ainvited
Breduced
Cdelayed
Drepeated
✔ 正確答案：(C)　delayed
中譯由於交通壅塞，這批貨的送達延誤了將近兩個小時。
解析heavy traffic（交通壅塞）造成送貨「延誤 delayed」。reduced 減少、invited 邀請、repeated 重複皆不合因果邏輯。
單字delay 延誤、耽擱、delivery 遞送、送貨、traffic 交通、車流
8The new policy aims to the gap between rich and poor by offering free education to everyone.
Awiden
Bnarrow
Cignore
Dcreate
✔ 正確答案：(B)　narrow
中譯這項新政策希望透過提供人人免費教育，來縮小貧富差距。
解析提供免費教育的目的在「縮小 narrow」貧富差距。widen 擴大、ignore 忽視、create 製造皆與政策善意目標相反。
單字narrow 縮小；狹窄的、gap 差距、缺口、policy 政策
9She spoke so that everyone in the room could clearly hear every word.
Asilently
Brarely
Ccarelessly
Ddistinctly
✔ 正確答案：(D)　distinctly
中譯她說得如此清楚，房間裡的每個人都能清楚聽見每個字。
解析could clearly hear every word（每個字都聽得清楚）對應「清晰地 distinctly」。silently 安靜地、carelessly 粗心地、rarely 很少皆矛盾。
單字distinctly 清楚地、明顯地、clearly 清楚地、rarely 很少
10The volunteers worked hard to food and clothing to the families affected by the flood.
Acomplain
Bpurchase
Cdistribute
Dcelebrate
✔ 正確答案：(C)　distribute
中譯志工們努力地將食物和衣物分發給受水災影響的家庭。
解析to + families（給家庭）搭配「分發 distribute」食物衣物。purchase 購買、complain 抱怨、celebrate 慶祝皆不合救災情境。
單字distribute 分發、分配、volunteer 志工、affect 影響

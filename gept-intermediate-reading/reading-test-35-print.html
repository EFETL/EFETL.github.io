ARTICLE
Finish the Whole Course

When the fever drops and the throat stops hurting, many patients stop taking their pills at once. Pharmacists say this common habit can be risky. Antibiotics kill the weakest germs first, so the stronger ones may still be alive on the third or fourth day, even though the patient already feels fine.

If the course is stopped too early, those survivors can grow again, and the same medicine may no longer work the next time. Doctors therefore ask patients to finish every tablet, unless a serious side effect appears. If something feels wrong, call the clinic rather than quietly throwing the bottle away.

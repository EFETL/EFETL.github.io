ARTICLE
The Price of One Coffee

Ask people why they give nothing to charity and most answer the same way: they are not wealthy enough to make any difference. The figures disagree.

In one city, a fund run by Christian and Buddhist volunteers asked 9,000 people for the price of one coffee each month. Nobody gave much. Together they paid for a night clinic that now treats about forty patients a week.

The lesson is not that small gifts work magic. It is that poverty is seldom ended by one rich donor, and often eased by many ordinary ones.

ARTICLE
Why We Keep Taking the Same Photo

Stand at any famous viewpoint and you will see a line of visitors holding up their phones at exactly the same angle. Researchers at a Dutch university call this the copy effect: once an image becomes popular online, travelers feel they must bring home the very same shot.

The habit is not harmless. In several small villages, crowds gathering for a single view have worn down narrow paths and annoyed residents. A few towns now put up signs asking visitors to spread out.

Yet the same researchers found a cure. Tourists who were asked to photograph something ordinary — a doorway, a market basket — reported enjoying the trip more, and their pictures were far more varied.

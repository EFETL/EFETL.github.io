How Long Does It Take to Make a Friend?

How long does it take to make a friend? A study followed 400 adults who had just moved to a new city. It found that about fifty hours of shared time turned a stranger into a casual friend, while a close bond needed more than two hundred.

What mattered most was not the number of hours but what people did with them. Talking about ordinary daily life built trust far faster than sitting side by side in a cinema.

The researchers also warn that a relation left alone will weaken. Even a short message every few weeks, they say, keeps the connection alive.

全文中譯

交一個朋友要花多久時間？一項研究追蹤了四百位剛搬到新城市的成年人。研究發現，大約五十小時的共處時間可以把陌生人變成點頭之交，而深厚的情誼則需要超過兩百小時。

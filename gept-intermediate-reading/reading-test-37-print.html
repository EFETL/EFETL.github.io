ARTICLE
Take Photos Before You Move In

Most arguments between owners and tenants happen on the last day, not the first. A tenant moves out, and the owner points at a scratch on the floor or a mark on the wall. Who caused it? Nobody can prove anything, so part of the deposit is kept.

Housing groups offer a simple answer: spend twenty minutes with a phone camera on moving-in day. Photograph every room, every scratch, and the water and power meters. E-mail the pictures to the owner the same day, and save the reply. That short message has saved thousands of tenants a great deal of money.

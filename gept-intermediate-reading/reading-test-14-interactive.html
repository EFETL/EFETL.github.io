Passage 1
The Work of a Translator

Turning a novel into another language is harder than most readers imagine. A good 11 does not simply replace one word with another. First she reads the whole book, listens to the voice of the 12, and marks every joke that will not travel well. Then she must decide how much of the original 13 she may change without hurting the meaning. A long English 14 often becomes two short ones in Chinese. Publishers usually allow six months for a full 15, and many say the second half of that time is spent reading the text aloud.

Why Students Still Read Poems

Some students groan when the teacher opens a book of 16. Poems look short, yet every line seems to hide something. Their teachers answer that a poem trains the ear: it teaches you to hear whether a 17 sound is long or short, and why one word feels heavier than another. A famous 18 once said that he wrote in order to slow his readers down. That is also why many schools ask students to write a short 19 about a poem they dislike. Explaining your own taste, teachers say, is the fastest way to 20 new ideas.

ARTICLE
Does Grammar Still Matter?

Every few years a newspaper announces that grammar teaching is dead. Language, such writers argue, is picked up the way music is: by listening, and by reading enough literature.

The truth is less tidy. Beginners really do learn many patterns without being taught. Yet adults who never study the rules often stop improving after two or three years. Their speech stays clear but simple, and their real strength, a large vocabulary, is wasted.

A short weekly lesson on one point, say the auxiliary verbs used in questions, seems to work best. What fails is a whole term spent naming parts of speech while nobody writes a single paragraph.

全文中譯

每隔幾年就有報紙宣告文法教學已死。這些作者主張，語言是像音樂那樣自然學會的：靠聆聽，以及讀夠多的文學作品。

事實沒有那麼乾淨俐落。初學者的確能在沒人教的情況下學會許多句型。可是從不研究規則的成人，往往在兩三年後就不再進步。他們的口語清楚卻簡單，而真正的優勢——龐大的字彙量——反而被浪費掉。

每週針對單一重點上一堂短課，例如疑問句所使用的助動詞，效果似乎最好。真正沒用的，是整個學期都在為詞類命名，卻沒有人寫過一個段落。

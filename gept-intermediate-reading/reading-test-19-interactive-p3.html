Your Shipment Has Sailed

Dear Ms. Alvarez,

Your cargo of 240 boxes left Keelung Port on 3 October on board the vessel Blue Star.

The ship is expected to reach Rotterdam on 28 October. Because of repair work at the harbor there, unloading may begin one day later than planned.

Please send the customs papers to our Amsterdam office before 20 October. Without them, your boxes cannot leave the dock.

You can follow the whole voyage on our website by entering the booking number BS-4471.

Best regards,
Karl Meyer, Ocean Line

全文中譯

親愛的阿爾瓦雷茲女士：

您那批 240 箱的貨物已於十月三日搭乘「藍星號」貨船離開基隆港。

該船預計於十月二十八日抵達鹿特丹。由於當地港口正在整修，卸貨可能比原訂晚一天開始。

請於十月二十日前將報關文件寄到我們的阿姆斯特丹辦公室。少了這些文件，您的貨物無法離開碼頭。

您可以在我們的網站輸入訂艙編號 BS-4471，追蹤整段航程。

敬祝安好
海洋航運 卡爾‧邁爾

Island Ferry: Please Read Before Sailing

Island Ferry — Please Read Before Sailing

  • Life jackets are stored under every seat on the lower deck.
  • When the sea is rough, the upper deck is closed and all passengers must stay inside.
  • Bicycles travel free, but they must be tied to the rail at the rear of the vessel.
  • The crossing takes 45 minutes. Hot drinks are sold at the little shop near the stairs.

In an emergency, follow the crew and go to the meeting point at the front of the ship. Do not return to your car.

全文中譯

離島渡輪 —— 開航前請詳閱

  • 救生衣放在下層甲板每個座位下方。
  • 海象不佳時，上層甲板將關閉，所有乘客必須留在室內。
  • 腳踏車免費載運，但必須綁在船尾的欄杆上。
  • 航程需時四十五分鐘。樓梯旁的小商店有販售熱飲。

發生緊急狀況時，請跟隨船員前往船首的集合點。請勿返回您的車輛。

ARTICLE
Still Talking After Fifty Years

In 1977 two small spacecraft left Earth to study the outer planets. Nobody expected them to work for more than five years.

Almost fifty years later, both are still sending signals from beyond our solar system, though each message now takes more than twenty hours to arrive. Their power comes from heat rather than sunlight, and it grows weaker every year.

Engineers therefore switch off one instrument at a time. They believe the last instrument will fall silent around 2030. Even then the two craft will travel on, each carrying a gold record of human music and greetings.

全文中譯

一九七七年，兩艘小型太空船離開地球，前往研究外太陽系的行星。當時沒有人預期它們能運作超過五年。

將近五十年後，兩者仍從太陽系之外傳回訊號，只是每則訊息如今要花二十多個小時才會抵達。它們的電力來自熱能而非陽光，而且逐年減弱。

因此工程師一次關閉一項儀器。他們相信最後一項儀器會在二○三○年前後停止運作。即使到了那時，這兩艘飛行器仍會繼續航行，各自帶著一張錄有人類音樂與問候語的金唱片。

ARTICLE
The Team Nobody Expected

Three years ago, the Nanfeng women's volleyball team finished last in their league. Few fans came to the games, and the club almost closed. Then a young coach, Lin Pei-chun, tried something unusual: she asked every player to study video of her own mistakes for twenty minutes after each practice.

At first the players hated it. Slowly, though, they began to notice small habits — a slow first step, a late call to a teammate. Last season Nanfeng won the league for the first time. 「We did not become stronger overnight,」 the captain said. 「We simply stopped repeating the same errors.」

全文中譯

三年前，南豐女子排球隊在聯盟中敬陪末座。來看比賽的球迷寥寥可數，球團幾乎要解散。後來一位年輕教練林珮均嘗試了不尋常的做法：她要求每位球員在每次練習後，花二十分鐘看自己失誤的影片。

一開始球員們很討厭這件事。不過她們慢慢開始注意到一些小習慣——起步慢了半拍、太晚呼叫隊友。上個球季，南豐首度拿下聯盟冠軍。「我們並不是一夜之間變強的，」隊長說，「我們只是不再重複同樣的錯誤。」

ARTICLE
The Power of Film Sound

Ask people what makes a scene frightening, and most will talk about the picture. Sound designers disagree. In one well-known test, viewers watched the same short scene twice, first with soft piano music and then with low, uneven noise. Almost everyone called the second version 「dangerous」, though not a single image had changed.

Most film sounds are not recorded on set. Footsteps, rain, and closing doors are created later in a quiet studio, often with strange tools: a bag of cornstarch can become snow. Good sound is rarely noticed, and that is exactly the point. When it works, the audience simply believes.

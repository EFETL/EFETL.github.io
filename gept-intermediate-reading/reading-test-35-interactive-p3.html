Changing an Appointment

Dear Ms. Hsieh,

I have an appointment with Dr. Pan at the Ho-An Family Clinic this Thursday at 3 p.m., but my company has suddenly sent me to Tainan for a two-day meeting. Could I move the visit to Monday morning instead?

Also, I had a blood test last week. The nurse said the report would be ready in five working days. If it has already arrived, please keep it for me until I come.

One more thing: the cream Dr. Pan gave me has run out, and my skin still itches at night. Please ask him whether I should keep using it.

Thank you,
Chen Wei-ting

全文中譯

謝小姐您好：

另外，我上週做了抽血檢查。護理師說報告五個工作天會好。如果報告已經送來了，請幫我保留到我過去為止。

還有一件事：潘醫師給我的藥膏已經用完了，我的皮膚晚上還是會癢。請幫我問他，我是否應該繼續使用。

謝謝您，

NOTICE
Visiting Rules at Ren-Ai General Hospital

To protect our patients, please follow the rules below when you visit the wards.

  • Visiting hours: 11:00–13:00 and 17:00–20:00 daily.
  • No more than two visitors may stay with one patient at a time.
  • Children under 12 are not allowed in the wards.
  • Fresh flowers are not permitted, since they may carry insects.

Please wash your hands at the door and wear a mask if you have a cough. Visitors who feel unwell should stay at home. During the flu season, the hospital may shorten visiting hours; a notice will be posted at the main entrance and on our website.

ARTICLE
Finish the Whole Course

When the fever drops and the throat stops hurting, many patients stop taking their pills at once. Pharmacists say this common habit can be risky. Antibiotics kill the weakest germs first, so the stronger ones may still be alive on the third or fourth day, even though the patient already feels fine.

If the course is stopped too early, those survivors can grow again, and the same medicine may no longer work the next time. Doctors therefore ask patients to finish every tablet, unless a serious side effect appears. If something feels wrong, call the clinic rather than quietly throwing the bottle away.

全文中譯

當燒退了、喉嚨也不痛了，許多病人就立刻停止服藥。藥師表示，這個常見的習慣可能有風險。抗生素會先殺死最弱的細菌，因此即使病人已經覺得舒服了，較強的細菌到第三、第四天可能仍然活著。

如果療程停得太早，這些存活下來的細菌會再度生長，同一種藥下次可能就不再有效。因此醫生要求病人吃完每一顆藥，除非出現嚴重的副作用。若覺得哪裡不對勁，應打電話到診所，而不是默默把藥丟掉。

ARTICLE
The Rise of Repair Cafés

On the first Saturday of every month, a community hall in Taichung fills with broken toasters, torn coats and silent radios. This is a repair café, where volunteers with tools help visitors mend their own belongings for free. The idea began in Amsterdam in 2009 and has since spread to more than two thousand towns.

Organizers say the goal is not only to save objects from the rubbish. Visitors learn simple skills, meet their neighbors, and start to see repair as normal rather than strange. About seventy percent of the items brought in leave the hall working again.

全文中譯

主辦者表示，目標不只是把東西從垃圾堆中救回來。來訪者學會簡單的技能、認識鄰居，並開始把修理視為理所當然而非奇怪的事。送來的物品中，約有七成離開會場時已能再次運作。

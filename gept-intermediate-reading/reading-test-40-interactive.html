ARTICLE
The Cost of a Short Commute

Flats near the city center cost more, but the extra rent is not always wasted. A study of 900 workers found that people who travel under twenty minutes each way sleep about forty minutes longer than those who travel over an hour.

Money is only part of the story. Long journeys also make people eat later, exercise less, and see friends only at weekends. Several workers in the study said they had turned down promotions because the new office lay further from home.

The researchers suggest a simple test: before signing a lease, travel to the new office from that address at eight on a Monday morning.

全文中譯

靠近市中心的公寓比較貴，但多付的租金未必浪費。一項針對 900 名上班族的研究發現，單程通勤不到二十分鐘的人，比通勤超過一小時的人多睡約四十分鐘。

金錢只是故事的一部分。長途通勤也使人吃得更晚、運動更少，而且只有週末才見得到朋友。研究中有好幾位受訪者表示，他們婉拒了升遷，因為新辦公室離家更遠。

研究者建議一個簡單的測試：簽租約前，先在某個星期一早上八點，從那個地址通勤到新辦公室一次。

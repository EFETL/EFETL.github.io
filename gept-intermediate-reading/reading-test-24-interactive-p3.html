ARTICLE
Old Factories, New Life

When a factory closes, the easiest choice is to knock it down. Yet many cities are discovering that old industrial buildings can be worth far more alive than gone. Their high ceilings, wide floors, and large windows suit art studios, bookstores, and offices surprisingly well.

Reusing a building also saves material and reduces waste. Experts estimate that keeping the main structure of an old building can cut the carbon produced by construction by about half.

There are difficulties, of course. Old walls often need strengthening against earthquakes, and rents in a renewed area tend to rise, pushing out the very neighbors who lived there for years.

全文中譯

當一座工廠關門，最簡單的選擇就是把它拆掉。然而許多城市正發現，老舊的工業建築活著時的價值，遠比拆掉之後高得多。它們挑高的天花板、寬闊的樓地板與大面窗戶，出乎意料地適合藝術工作室、書店與辦公室。

再利用建築也能節省材料、減少廢棄物。專家估計，保留老建築的主體結構，可將營建過程所產生的碳排放減少約一半。

當然也有困難之處。老牆往往需要補強以抵抗地震，而更新地區的租金也容易上漲，把那些住了多年的老鄰居擠了出去。

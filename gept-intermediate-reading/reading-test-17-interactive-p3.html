Your Booking at Riverbend Inn

Dear Ms. Hart,

Thank you for choosing Riverbend Inn for your stay in July. Your accommodation is confirmed: one twin room for three nights, from 12 to 15 July, breakfast included.

Check-in begins at 3 p.m. If you arrive earlier, we are happy to keep your luggage at the front desk at no cost.

Our free walking tour of the old harbor leaves every morning at nine. Please let us know by 10 July if you would like to reserve two places.

The car park behind the building holds only four cars, so please tell us if you plan to drive.

Kind regards,
Peter Lang, Manager

入住時間自下午三點開始。若您提早抵達，我們很樂意免費為您把行李寄放在櫃檯。

我們免費的舊港區步行導覽每天早上九點出發。若您想預訂兩個名額，請於七月十日（含）前告知我們。

建築物後方的停車場只能停四輛車，所以若您打算開車前來，請務必告訴我們。

Green Hill Hostel House Rules

Green Hill Hostel — House Rules

Our hostel was founded in 1998 by two schoolteachers, and we still run it like a family home.

  • Reception is open from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. Guests arriving later must telephone in advance.
  • Large baggage may be left in the locked store room beside the stairs.
  • The shared kitchen closes at 10 p.m. Please wash your own dishes.
  • Smoking is forbidden everywhere inside the building, including the balcony.

Towels cost NT$50 each. A day trip to the tea farm leaves at 8 a.m. every Saturday; sign up at reception before Friday evening.

ARTICLE
Slow Travel Is Winning

A growing number of travelers now say that the journey matters more than the list of places they tick off. Instead of visiting eight cities in ten days, they choose one small town and stay there for a week.

Guides call this slow travel. Its followers spend less time on buses and more time in markets and parks. They argue that racing past famous sites is not real sightseeing at all; by the fourth day most travelers can no longer remember what they photographed.

Hotels have noticed the change. Some now offer lower prices for stays of five nights or more, and run cooking classes instead of bus tours.

全文中譯

愈來愈多旅客表示，旅程本身比走過幾個景點的清單更重要。他們不再十天走訪八座城市，而是選一座小鎮，在那裡待上一週。

導遊把這稱為慢旅行。奉行的人待在巴士上的時間變少，待在市場和公園裡的時間變多。他們主張，飛快掠過知名景點根本稱不上真正的觀光；到了第四天，大多數旅客已經想不起自己拍過什麼。

旅館也注意到這股改變。有些旅館現在為住滿五晚以上的房客提供較低價格，並開設烹飪課取代巴士行程。

ARTICLE
Why Small Shops Are Coming Back

For twenty years the story was simple: large chains opened, small shops closed. In several cities that trend has now slowed, and in a few it has turned around.

One reason is delivery. When almost anything can arrive at your door in a day, the advantage of a huge shop full of goods becomes less important. What a street shop can offer instead is advice, repair and a face you recognize.

Rent still decides much of it. Where landlords have kept rents moderate, small shops survive; where rents have doubled, the shutters stay down whatever the shopkeepers do.

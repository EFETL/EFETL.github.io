Reply to a Volunteer Application

Dear Ms. Tsai,

Thank you for applying to join the volunteer team at the Hengchun Sea Turtle Rescue Station. We are glad to tell you that your application has been accepted for the August program.

Before you arrive, please note two things. First, all new volunteers must attend a three-hour safety talk on August 2. Second, a swimming test is required only for those who choose the underwater group; if you prefer, you may work in the feeding kitchen instead.

Please bring your own hat and a reusable water bottle. We no longer hand out plastic bottles at the station.

Best regards,
Daniel Weng, Volunteer Coordinator

ARTICLE
The Noise Beneath the Waves

The ocean was once thought to be a silent world, but it is actually full of sound. Whales sing to find mates, and dolphins use clicks to locate food in dark water. Because sound travels far underwater, these calls can cross hundreds of kilometers.

Today, however, ships, drills, and underwater engines fill the sea with noise. Scientists say this makes it harder for whales to hear one another, so families may become separated. During the quiet months of 2020, when fewer ships sailed, researchers recorded calmer waters and healthier whale behavior. Slower ships, they argue, would cost little yet help a great deal.

全文中譯

海洋一度被認為是無聲的世界，其實卻充滿聲音。鯨魚以歌聲尋找伴侶，海豚則利用喀噠聲在黑暗的水中定位食物。由於聲音在水下能傳得很遠，這些叫聲可以跨越數百公里。

然而如今，船舶、鑽井與水下引擎讓海裡充滿噪音。科學家表示，這使鯨魚更難聽見彼此，家族因此可能失散。在 2020 年那段安靜的幾個月裡，出航的船變少，研究人員記錄到更平靜的海域與更健康的鯨魚行為。他們主張，讓船隻放慢速度花費不多，卻能帶來很大的幫助。

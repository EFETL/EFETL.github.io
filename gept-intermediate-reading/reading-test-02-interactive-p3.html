ARTICLE
Taking a Break from Social Media

Social media helps us stay in touch with friends, but spending too much time on it can be harmful. Studies show that people who check their phones every few minutes often feel more tired and less able to focus on their work.

More and more young people are now trying a 「digital break」. They turn off their apps for a day or a weekend to rest their minds. Many say they sleep better and enjoy real life more during this time. Experts do not ask us to give up social media completely. Instead, they suggest using it with a clear purpose, rather than out of simple habit.

全文中譯

社群媒體幫助我們與朋友保持聯繫，但花太多時間在上面可能有害。研究顯示，每隔幾分鐘就查看手機的人，往往感到更疲累，也更難專注於工作。

越來越多的年輕人如今嘗試「數位休息」。他們關掉應用程式一天或一個週末，讓大腦休息。許多人說，在這段期間他們睡得更好，也更享受真實生活。專家並非要我們完全放棄社群媒體。相反地，他們建議帶著明確的目的使用它，而不是出於單純的習慣。

ARTICLE
Why Small Shops Are Coming Back

For twenty years the story was simple: large chains opened, small shops closed. In several cities that trend has now slowed, and in a few it has turned around.

One reason is delivery. When almost anything can arrive at your door in a day, the advantage of a huge shop full of goods becomes less important. What a street shop can offer instead is advice, repair and a face you recognize. Some now order straight from a small manufacturer rather than a national chain.

Rent still decides much of it. Where landlords have kept rents moderate, small shops survive; where rents have doubled, the shutters stay down whatever the shopkeepers do.

全文中譯

二十年來故事很簡單：大型連鎖開店，小店關門。在數個城市，這個趨勢現已減緩，少數城市甚至逆轉。

原因之一是配送。當幾乎任何東西都能一天內送到你家門口，一間商品滿滿的大賣場的優勢就變小了。街邊小店能提供的，反而是建議、維修，以及一張你認得的臉。有些小店現在直接向小型製造商進貨，而不是透過全國連鎖。

租金仍然決定大半。房東把租金維持在適度水準的地方，小店存活下來；租金翻倍的地方，不論店主怎麼做，鐵門都拉不起來。

ARTICLE
The Cost of Being Poor

Economists have long noticed something unfair: it is expensive to have little money. A family that cannot pay a year's insurance all at once must pay monthly, and monthly costs more. A worker without a bank account pays a fee to cash a cheque. Without capital, a family cannot buy in bulk or mend a roof before it leaks.

Cheap shoes are another example. A pair that lasts four months may cost a third as much as a pair that lasts three years, but over a decade the cheap pair costs far more.

Some cities now lend small sums at no interest so that families can buy the yearly plan rather than the monthly one. Early results suggest that the loans are almost always repaid.

全文中譯

經濟學家很早就注意到一件不公平的事：錢少反而昂貴。一個無法一次付清整年保險的家庭必須按月繳，而按月比較貴。沒有銀行帳戶的工人兌現支票要付手續費。沒有資金，一個家庭無法整批採購，也無法在屋頂漏水前先修好。

便宜的鞋子是另一個例子。一雙只撐四個月的鞋，價格可能只有能穿三年那雙的三分之一，但十年下來，便宜那雙花的錢多得多。

有些城市現在提供無息小額借款，讓家庭可以買整年方案而非按月方案。初步結果顯示，這些借款幾乎都會如期還清。

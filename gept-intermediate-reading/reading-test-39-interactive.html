ARTICLE
Why Children Stop Drawing

Almost every four-year-old draws happily. By the age of twelve, most have stopped. Researchers who study art education blame one simple thing: comparison. Young children draw to tell a story, but older children begin to judge their work against a classmate's, or against a photograph.

One school in Tainan tried an experiment. For a whole term, teachers gave no marks for art and never displayed a 「best」 drawing. Instead, every student kept a private sketchbook. By June, twice as many students said they drew at home for fun.

The lesson may be that children do not lose their talent; they lose their courage.

全文中譯

幾乎每個四歲小孩都樂於畫畫。到了十二歲，大多數人卻不再畫了。研究藝術教育的學者把原因歸咎於一件很簡單的事：比較。年幼的孩子畫畫是為了說故事，但年紀較大的孩子會開始拿自己的作品和同學的、或和照片相比。

台南有一所學校做了個實驗。整整一個學期，老師都不為美術打分數，也從不張貼「最佳」畫作。取而代之的是，每位學生保有一本私人的速寫本。到了六月，表示自己在家會為樂趣而畫的學生人數增加為兩倍。

這件事的啟示或許是：孩子失去的不是天分，而是勇氣。

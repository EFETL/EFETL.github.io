1The witness gave a account of the accident, so the police knew exactly what had happened.
Adetailed
Bvague
Cbrief
Dfalse
✔ 正確答案：(A)　detailed
中譯目擊者對這起事故做了詳細的描述，所以警方確切知道發生了什麼事。

單字detailed 詳細的、witness 目擊者、account 描述、敘述

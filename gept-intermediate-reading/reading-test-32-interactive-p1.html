詞彙 Vocabulary

共 10 題／每題選出最適合題意的字詞
請由四個選項中，選出一個最適合句意的字或詞。
1Before publishing the report, the editor asked the reporter to every fact with a second source.
Averify
Bignore
Cinvent
Ddelay
✔ 正確答案：(A)　verify
中譯在刊出這篇報導之前，編輯要求記者用第二個來源查證每一項事實。
解析with a second source（用另一個消息來源）暗示要「查證 verify」。ignore 忽視、invent 捏造、delay 延遲皆不合新聞查核的語意。
單字verify 查證、證實、source 消息來源、editor 編輯
2The story spread so on social media that millions of people saw it within one day.
Ararely
Bhardly
Crapidly
Dslightly
✔ 正確答案：(C)　rapidly
中譯這則消息在社群媒體上散播得如此迅速，一天之內就有數百萬人看到。
解析millions of people saw it within one day（一天內數百萬人看到）表示速度快，選「迅速地 rapidly」。rarely 很少、hardly 幾乎不、slightly 稍微皆與結果矛盾。
單字rapidly 迅速地、spread 散播、social media 社群媒體
3A responsible reporter should never the facts just to make a story sound more exciting.
Aprotect
Btwist
Ccollect
Drecord
✔ 正確答案：(B)　twist
中譯負責任的記者絕不該只為了讓報導聽起來更刺激就扭曲事實。
解析never 加上 just to make it sound more exciting，指不該「扭曲 twist」事實。protect 保護、collect 蒐集、record 記錄都是中性或正面行為，與 never 的譴責語氣不合。
單字twist 扭曲、responsible 負責任的、fact 事實
4The magazine printed a short after admitting that the number in last week's article was wrong.
Ainvitation
Badventure
Cagreement
Dcorrection
✔ 正確答案：(D)　correction
中譯在承認上週文章中的數字有誤之後，這本雜誌刊登了一則簡短的更正啟事。
解析admitting that the number was wrong（承認數字錯誤）之後刊出的是「更正 correction」。invitation 邀請、adventure 冒險、agreement 協議皆與承認錯誤無關。
單字correction 更正、admit 承認、print 刊登、印刷
5Experienced readers are of headlines that promise shocking secrets no one else knows.
Asuspicious
Bgrateful
Cfamiliar
Dresponsible
✔ 正確答案：(A)　suspicious
中譯有經驗的讀者對那些宣稱有無人知曉、驚人祕密的標題抱持懷疑。
解析be suspicious of 表「對…起疑」，符合誇大標題應被質疑的邏輯。grateful 感激的、familiar 熟悉的、responsible 負責的皆與 shocking secrets 的可疑感不搭。
單字suspicious 懷疑的、headline 新聞標題、shocking 令人震驚的
6The spokesman the rumor at once, saying that no such plan had ever existed.
Asupported
Bdenied
Cannounced
Ddescribed
✔ 正確答案：(B)　denied
中譯發言人立刻否認了這則謠言，表示從未存在這樣的計畫。
解析no such plan had ever existed（從未有這種計畫）等於「否認 denied」。supported 支持、announced 宣布、described 描述都與後句的全面推翻矛盾。
單字deny 否認、rumor 謠言、spokesman 發言人
7It is getting harder to a real photo from one that has been changed by a computer.
Aremind
Bprevent
Cescape
Ddistinguish
✔ 正確答案：(D)　distinguish
中譯要分辨真實照片與經電腦修改過的照片，正變得越來越困難。
解析distinguish A from B 表「分辨 A 與 B」，是本句唯一能接 from 且合意的動詞。remind 提醒、prevent 防止、escape 逃離皆不成搭配。
單字distinguish 分辨、區別、real 真實的、change 更改
8The newspaper's dropped sharply after readers discovered several false stories in it.
Asalary
Bdistance
Creputation
Dtemperature
✔ 正確答案：(C)　reputation
中譯在讀者發現報上有數則不實報導之後，這家報紙的名聲急遽下滑。
解析readers discovered several false stories（讀者發現多則假新聞）會使「名聲 reputation」受損。salary 薪水、distance 距離、temperature 溫度皆與媒體信譽無關。
單字reputation 名聲、聲譽、sharply 急遽地、false 不實的
9Please the message carefully before you forward it to everyone in your family group.
Awaste
Bexamine
Cproduce
Dcelebrate
✔ 正確答案：(B)　examine
中譯在你把訊息轉傳給家族群組裡的每個人之前，請仔細檢視它。
解析carefully 與 before you forward it 提示先「檢視 examine」再轉傳。waste 浪費、produce 製造、celebrate 慶祝皆與檢查訊息的動作無關。
單字examine 檢視、檢查、forward 轉寄、轉傳、message 訊息
10The two websites reported the same event, but their of what happened were quite different.
Ainstruments
Bappetites
Cinvitations
Daccounts
✔ 正確答案：(D)　accounts
中譯這兩個網站報導同一事件，但它們對事發經過的說法卻相當不同。
解析account 在此為「敘述、說法」之意，of what happened 正是其常見搭配。instruments 樂器／儀器、appetites 食慾、invitations 邀請皆不合。
單字account 敘述、說法、event 事件、report 報導

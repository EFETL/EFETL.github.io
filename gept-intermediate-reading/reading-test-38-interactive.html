Getting Your Deposit Back

Most arguments at the end of a lease are about cleaning. Tenants think the flat looks fine; landlords point at the kitchen. The 11 problem is usually the cooker, where months of cooking leave a 12 layer that a quick wipe cannot remove. Bathrooms come second, because warm, damp corners let 13 grow. Experts suggest breaking the job into small tasks over a week, which makes it far more 14 than one exhausting day. Take photographs when you finish; if a landlord later disagrees, an image with a date is worth more than any 15 you could give.

Passage 2
An Old Flat in Winter

Cheap old flats hide costs that no advertisement mentions. In a 16 winter, water inside an outdoor pipe can 17 and split it open. Drains that are already narrow 18 in the first cold week, and the bill arrives before the landlord answers the phone. Ask two questions before you sign. Is the building 19 enough to have survived the last big earthquake without repair? And does the lease say who pays when a pipe fails? A tenant who asks looks difficult for five minutes; a tenant who does not may pay for months. That is a 20 many people learn too late.

ARTICLE
Why Moving House Is So Tiring

Anyone who has moved knows the strange exhaustion of the first night in a new place. Researchers say the cause is not only the load you carried up the stairs.

Every day at home runs on habits: your hand finds the light switch without looking. A new flat cancels all of them at once. For about two weeks the brain must think about actions it used to do automatically, and that costs energy.

The advice is simple. Unpack the kitchen and the bathroom first, not the books. Once you can make coffee and take a shower without searching, the rest of the flat stops feeling like someone else's.

全文中譯

搬過家的人都知道，在新地方的第一晚會有一種奇怪的疲憊。研究者說原因不只是你搬上樓的那些重物。

家中的每一天都靠習慣運作：你的手不用看就能找到電燈開關。新公寓一次取消了所有這些習慣。大約兩週的時間，大腦必須思考原本自動完成的動作，而這很耗費能量。

建議很簡單。先整理廚房和浴室，而不是書。一旦你不必翻找就能泡咖啡、洗澡，公寓的其他部分就不再感覺像是別人的了。

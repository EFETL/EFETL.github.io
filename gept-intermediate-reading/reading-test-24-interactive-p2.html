Passage 1
Tablets in the Classroom

Two years ago our school started a digital reading plan and gave a 11 to every student. The change was not easy. Teachers first had to learn the new 12 that runs the reading lessons, and the school 13 often slowed down when three hundred people went online at the same time. A few machines even 14 during the week of the first exams. Today, however, few teachers want to go back. Students hand in their homework through one simple 15, and anyone who misses a lesson can watch it again at home. Most teachers now say the technology finally saves time.

Questions 11–15
11
Afable
Btablet
Cclown
Dtrunk
✔ 正確答案：(B)　tablet
解析gave a ____ to every student 加上前面的 digital reading plan，可知發的是「平板電腦 tablet」。trunk 樹幹、clown 小丑、fable 寓言都不是可以發給學生的閱讀裝置。
12
Asoftware
Busage
Cauthority
Dfragrance
✔ 正確答案：(A)　software
解析the new ____ that runs the reading lessons 指跑課程的「軟體 software」。usage 用法、authority 職權、fragrance 香味都無法 run lessons。
13
Achain
Bsite
Ccycle
Dnetwork
✔ 正確答案：(D)　network
解析three hundred people went online at the same time 造成變慢的是學校的「網路 network」。chain 鍊條、site 位址、cycle 循環都不會因為上線人數而變慢。
14
Afled
Btrembled
Ccrashed
Dskimmed
✔ 正確答案：(C)　crashed
解析machines 在考試週發生的狀況是「當機 crash」。flee 逃離、tremble 發抖、skim 瀏覽都不是機器的動作。
15
Aobstacle
Binterpretation
Capp
Dfrustrate
✔ 正確答案：(C)　app
解析Students hand in their homework through one simple ____ 指用一個「應用程式 app」收作業。obstacle 阻礙、interpretation 詮釋都無法用來收作業；frustrate 使灰心是動詞，不能放在 one simple 之後當名詞。
全文中譯兩年前，我們學校開始推動數位閱讀計畫，發給每位學生一台平板電腦。這個改變並不容易。老師得先學會操作閱讀課程的新軟體，而當三百人同時上線時，學校的網路也常常變慢。第一次段考那週甚至有幾台機器當機。不過如今很少有老師想回到從前。學生透過一個簡單的應用程式繳交作業，缺課的人也能在家重看那堂課。現在多數老師都說，這項科技終於替他們省下了時間。
Passage 2
Everything in the Cloud

Ten years ago most people kept their files on one computer at home. If that 16 was stolen or damaged, the photos were gone forever. Today many of us store everything in the 17 instead. Companies keep our files on huge machines far away, and we open them with a password or a short 18 sent to our phone. The change is useful, but it does bring risks. A single 19 at one company can expose millions of private records. Experts therefore advise users to check their settings in a 20 way at least twice a year.

Questions 16–20
16
Alily
Bchick
Cdevice
Dcreature
✔ 正確答案：(C)　device
解析承接前句 one computer at home，被偷或損壞的是那台「裝置 device」。creature 生物、chick 小雞、lily 百合花都不是存檔案的東西。
17
Aperfume
Bcloud
Csausage
Dbump
✔ 正確答案：(B)　cloud
解析store everything in the ____ 加上後句「檔案放在遠方的大型主機」，指的是「雲端 cloud」。perfume 香水、sausage 香腸、bump 碰撞聲都不能存檔案。
18
Acode
Bscratch
Cmurderer
Dspark
✔ 正確答案：(A)　code
解析a password or a short ____ sent to our phone 指手機收到的驗證「代碼 code」。spark 火花、scratch 刮痕、murderer 兇手都不會傳到手機當作密碼。
19
Aequivalent
Brescue
Cprofit
Dleak
✔ 正確答案：(D)　leak
解析can expose millions of private records（讓私人紀錄曝光）的是資料「外洩 leak」。profit 利潤、rescue 營救、equivalent 相等物都不會造成外洩。
20
Abrand-new
Bacademic
Ctropical
Dsystematic
✔ 正確答案：(D)　systematic
解析check their settings in a ____ way at least twice a year 指「有系統的 systematic」方式。tropical 熱帶的、academic 學術的、brand-new 全新的都不能形容檢查方式。
全文中譯十年前，多數人把檔案存在家裡的一台電腦上。如果那台裝置被偷或損壞，照片就永遠不見了。今天我們許多人改把所有東西存在雲端。業者把我們的檔案放在遠方的大型主機裡，我們則用密碼或手機收到的短代碼來開啟。這樣的改變很方便，但確實帶來風險。一家公司的一次外洩，就可能讓數百萬筆私人紀錄曝光。因此專家建議使用者每年至少兩次有系統地檢查自己的設定。

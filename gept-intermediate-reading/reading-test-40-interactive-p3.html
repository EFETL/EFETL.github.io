ARTICLE
The Trees That Share

Under every healthy forest lies a hidden network of thin fungal threads. These threads join the roots of different trees, sometimes across hundreds of meters. Through them a large old tree can send sugar to a small one struggling in deep shade.

Scientists in Canada showed this by feeding one tree with marked carbon and later finding the very same carbon inside its neighbors. The discovery changed the way many people think about woodland. A forest is not a crowd of separate trees fighting for light; it behaves much more like a single community.

That is one reason why removing the oldest trees can weaken a whole hillside.

全文中譯

每一片健康的森林底下，都藏著一張由細細真菌絲線構成的網路。這些絲線把不同樹木的根連接起來，有時橫跨數百公尺。透過它們，一棵高大的老樹可以把糖分輸送給在濃蔭下艱難生長的小樹。

加拿大的科學家餵給某一棵樹帶有標記的碳，之後在鄰近樹木體內找到了同樣的碳，藉此證明了這件事。這項發現改變了許多人看待林地的方式。森林並不是一群各自爭光的樹，它的運作反而更像一個共同體。

這也是為什麼砍除最老的樹木，可能會使整片山坡變得虛弱。

Questions 21–24
21What connects the roots of different trees?
AThreads of fungus
BUnderground rivers
CBuried stones
DMetal wires
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Threads of fungus
中譯是什麼把不同樹木的根連結起來？
解析首句 a hidden network of thin fungal threads（細細的真菌絲線網路），下一句說明這些絲線連接樹根。地下水、石頭與金屬線文中皆未提及。
22How did the scientists prove that trees share food?
ABy counting the leaves
BBy cutting down old trees
CBy tracking marked carbon
DBy listening to the roots
✔ 正確答案：(C)　By tracking marked carbon
中譯科學家如何證明樹木會分享養分？
解析文中 feeding one tree with marked carbon and later finding the very same carbon inside its neighbors，即以標記碳追蹤養分流向。砍樹是文末提到的危害，不是實驗方法。
23According to the article, a forest is best described as ____.
Aa group of rivals
Ba single community
Ca source of wood

✔ 正確答案：(B)　a single community

解析文中 it behaves much more like a single community（更像一個共同體），並明確否定 a crowd of separate trees fighting for light，故 A 正是被反駁的說法。
24Why can removing the oldest trees be harmful?
AThey block strong winds
BThey are worth little money
CThey grow back very slowly

中譯為什麼移除最老的樹木可能造成傷害？
解析由第一段 a large old tree can send sugar to a small one 可知老樹在供養周圍幼樹，因此移除它們會削弱整片山坡。擋風與生長速度文中並未提及。
NOTICE
Nanshan Forest Park Visitor Rules

Nanshan Forest Park protects one of the island's last lowland forests. Please help us keep it that way.

  • Stay on the marked paths; the soil beside them takes years to recover.
  • Carry all rubbish out with you. There are no bins inside the park.
  • Do not feed any animal. Fed monkeys grow bold and are often killed later.
  • Camping is allowed only in Area C, and all fires must be out by 9 p.m.

The east trail is closed from April to June while the forest owls are raising their young. Rangers may check bags at the gate.

全文中譯

南山森林公園保護著本島最後幾片低海拔森林之一。請協助我們維持它的原貌。

  • 請走在標示的步道上；步道旁的土壤需要數年才能恢復。
  • 請把所有垃圾帶走。園區內沒有垃圾桶。
  • 請勿餵食任何動物。被餵食的猴子會變得大膽，日後常因此遭到撲殺。
  • 僅 C 區可以露營，且所有火源必須在晚上 9 點前熄滅。

四月至六月間東線步道封閉，因為森林裡的貓頭鷹正在育雛。巡護員可能會在入口處檢查背包。

EMAIL
An Email to a School Principal

Dear Principal Yeh,

My name is Sung Pei-yi and I work at the Hushan Wildlife Rescue Center. Every autumn hundreds of migrating birds die after flying into the tall glass walls of school buildings in our town.

We would like to offer your school a free set of thin white stickers. Placed on the outside of the glass, they make the surface visible to birds but hardly change the view for your students. Fitting them takes about two hours.

Could I visit next Tuesday to show you a sample?

Best regards,
Sung Pei-yi

全文中譯

敬愛的葉校長：

我叫宋佩宜，服務於虎山野生動物救傷中心。每年秋天，本鎮都有數百隻候鳥因撞上學校建築的高大玻璃帷幕而死亡。

我們想免費提供貴校一套細長的白色貼紙。貼在玻璃外側後，鳥類就能看見這片表面，而學生的視野幾乎不受影響。安裝大約需要兩個小時。

我可以在下週二前往拜訪，向您展示樣品嗎？

宋佩宜 敬上

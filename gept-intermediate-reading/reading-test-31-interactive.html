ARTICLE
The Library on the Mountain

For more than a thousand years, monks in a small house on the mountain have copied and repaired old books. The collection began with a single Bible and now holds nine thousand volumes, some of them written in languages that nobody speaks today.

The work is slow and quiet. One damaged page may take a week to mend. Younger monks learn the skill by sitting beside older ones for years, never from a manual.

Scholars from many countries now visit, and a few pages are shown to the public every spring. The monks refuse to sell anything. Books, they say, belong to the future, not to the market.

全文中譯

一千多年來，一棟小小山中屋舍裡的僧侶們持續抄寫並修補古書。這批藏書從一本聖經開始，如今已有九千冊，其中有些是用已經沒有人在說的語言寫成的。

這份工作既緩慢又安靜。一張破損的書頁可能要花一週才能修好。年輕僧侶靠著在年長者身旁一坐好幾年來學會這門技術，而不是靠手冊。

如今許多國家的學者都來造訪，每年春天也會展出少數幾頁。僧侶們拒絕出售任何一件藏品。他們說，書屬於未來，而不屬於市場。

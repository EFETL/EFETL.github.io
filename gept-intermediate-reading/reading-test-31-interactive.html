Of course, the life of a professional gamer is shorter than most people 13. Doctors do not tell young people to stop gaming; they ask them to game 16. Sitting still for five hours harms the back and the eyes far more than the game itself does. A simple rule is to stand up and look at something distant every hour. Sleep matters even 17. Screens at midnight push the brain into a state of alarm, and tired players lose more matches, not fewer. Teams in Korea now 18 their members to a fixed bedtime. Finally, a game should be one part of life, not the 19 one. Friends, school, and exercise keep a player 20 in the long run.

ARTICLE
The Coach Behind the Screen

When Wei Chun-hsiang joined a professional team at eighteen, he practiced twelve hours a day and won almost nothing. Five years later he became a coach, and his players now train only seven hours a day — yet they win far more often.

His method surprises people. Half of every session is spent watching recordings of matches they lost, with the sound turned off. Wei believes players learn faster when they see their own habits instead of hearing advice. He also forbids practice after eleven at night. As he puts it, a tired brain repeats mistakes rather than fixing them.

全文中譯

魏俊翔十八歲加入職業隊時，一天練十二小時，卻幾乎什麼也沒贏過。五年後他轉任教練，如今他的選手一天只練七小時，勝率卻高出許多。

他的方法讓人意外。每次訓練有一半的時間，是關掉聲音、觀看自己輸掉的比賽錄影。魏俊翔認為，比起聽別人給建議，選手看見自己的習慣時學得更快。他也禁止晚上十一點後練習。用他的話說，疲倦的大腦只會重複錯誤，而不是修正錯誤。

Passage 1
Under the City

In many large cities, the quickest way to get around is underground. The 11 carries millions of people every day, and a single 12 costs less than a cup of coffee. The trains are clean, and each 13 has space for bicycles at one end. Above ground, buses and private cars fight for the same road; an 14 may sit on the same street for twenty minutes without moving. City planners therefore spend more money on public 15 than on new roads, hoping that fewer drivers will mean cleaner air.

全文中譯在許多大城市裡，移動最快的方式是走地下。地鐵每天載運數百萬人，而一趟車資比一杯咖啡還便宜。列車很乾淨，每節車廂的一端都有放腳踏車的空間。地面上，公車和私家車搶著同一條路；一輛汽車可能在同一條街上二十分鐘動彈不得。因此都市規劃者把更多經費投入大眾運輸，而非新道路，期望駕駛人變少能換來更乾淨的空氣。
Passage 2
More Than Pressing a Pedal

Learning to drive takes far more than knowing where the 16 is. In the first lesson most students press it much too hard, and everyone in the car jumps forward. A good instructor teaches learners to change 17 smoothly, to 18 gently around corners, and to check the mirrors every few seconds. Drivers should also check each 19 before a long trip, because a soft one may burst at high speed. Above all, drivers must remember that arriving at a 20 on time is never worth the risk of a serious accident.

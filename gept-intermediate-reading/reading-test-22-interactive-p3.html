NOTICE
New Collection Times for Household Refuse

Notice from the Riverside District Office

From 1 March, household refuse will be collected on Tuesday and Friday evenings only. The truck stops at each street corner for ten minutes.

  • Green bins: kitchen waste. Blue bins: paper, glass and metal.
  • Collection of large junk such as sofas and broken beds must be booked by phone three days in advance.
  • Bags left on the street outside collection hours will not be taken away.

Residents who sort their waste correctly for six months will receive a free set of reusable bags. Questions: 02-2345-6789.

全文中譯

河濱區公所公告

自三月一日起，家庭垃圾僅於週二與週五傍晚收運。垃圾車在每個街角停留十分鐘。

  • 綠色垃圾桶：廚餘。藍色垃圾桶：紙類、玻璃與金屬。
  • 沙發、壞掉的床等大型廢棄物，其清運須於三天前以電話預約。
  • 非收運時間放在路邊的垃圾袋將不予清運。

連續六個月正確分類的住戶，可獲得一組免費環保袋。洽詢電話：02-2345-6789。

ARTICLE
The Birds That Cleaned the Fields

Thirty years ago vultures were everywhere in northern India. Then, within a single decade, more than ninety percent of them disappeared.

The cause was a cheap medicine given to sick cattle. It was harmless to the cows themselves, but any vulture that fed on a treated animal died within days. Three species came close to extinction.

The drug was banned in 2006, and special centers now raise young birds before setting them free. Numbers are rising, though slowly. Villagers know the difference well: without vultures, dead animals lie in the open for weeks, and packs of wild dogs move in.

全文中譯

三十年前，禿鷲在印度北部隨處可見。後來，短短十年之間，超過九成的禿鷲消失了。

原因是一種給生病牛隻使用的廉價藥物。這種藥對牛本身無害，但任何吃到用過這種藥的牛屍的禿鷲，幾天內就會死亡。有三個物種一度瀕臨絕種。

這種藥在二○○六年被禁用，如今也有專門的收容中心先養大幼鳥再野放。數量正在回升，只是相當緩慢。村民很清楚其中的差別：沒有禿鷲，動物屍體會在野外曝曬好幾週，成群的野狗也跟著出現。

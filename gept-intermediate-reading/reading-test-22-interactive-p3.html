A Question About Bank Fees

Dear Mr. Halstead,

I opened a Starter Savings account at your Wenshan branch in January. This month I noticed a NT$120 charge on my statement marked account service. Nothing like this appeared in the first four months.

When I signed up, the staff told me the account was free as long as I kept NT$5,000 in it. My balance did drop to NT$4,300 for six days in May, because I had to pay for a car repair, but it is now back above the line.

Could you explain the rule and tell me whether the fee can be returned? I would rather stay with your bank than move my savings.

Sincerely,
Pearl Ashgrove

全文中譯

Halstead 先生您好：

我於一月在貴行文山分行開立了「入門儲蓄」帳戶。這個月我在對帳單上看到一筆新台幣 120 元、註記為「帳戶服務費」的扣款。前四個月都沒有出現這樣的收費。

我開戶時，行員告訴我只要帳戶內維持新台幣 5,000 元就免收費用。我的餘額在五月確實曾有六天降到 4,300 元，因為我必須支付一筆汽車修理費，但目前已回到標準之上。

可否請您說明這項規定，並告知這筆費用是否能退還？比起把存款轉走，我更希望繼續與貴行往來。

Pearl Ashgrove 敬上

ARTICLE
The Cost of Paying Later

Shops now offer a tempting button: buy today, pay in three parts, no interest. For a washing machine that breaks in winter, such a plan can be sensible. The danger appears when the button is used for small things.

A consumer group in Kaohsiung followed 300 shoppers for a year. Those who used pay-later services spent about 30 percent more than they had planned, mostly on clothes and shoes. Nearly a quarter forgot at least one payment date and had to pay a late fee.

The group does not ask for a ban. It suggests one rule: before pressing the button, ask whether you would still buy the item if the whole price had to leave your account today.

全文中譯

商店如今提供一個誘人的按鈕：今天買，分三期付，零利息。對於冬天壞掉的洗衣機來說，這樣的方案可能是明智的。危險出現在這個按鈕被用來買小東西的時候。

高雄一個消費者團體追蹤了 300 位購物者一整年。使用先買後付服務的人，花費比原先計畫多出約三成，主要花在衣服和鞋子上。將近四分之一的人至少忘記一次付款日，必須繳交遲繳費。

該團體並未要求禁止這項服務。他們建議一個原則：按下按鈕之前，先問問自己——如果今天就得從帳戶扣走全額，你還會買這件東西嗎？

ItemAmountShare
Rent and utilitiesNT$4,50050%

SavingsNT$90010%

She has promised herself not to touch the savings unless her laptop breaks. Any money left over from food will be added to savings on the last day of the month.

全文中譯

大一新生文萱每個月從父母那裡拿到新台幣 9,000 元。以下是她為十月所寫的計畫。

項目金額比例
房租與水電NT$4,50050%
伙食NT$2,70030%

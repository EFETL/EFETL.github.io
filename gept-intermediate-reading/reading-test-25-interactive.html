ARTICLE
How Fast Bad News Travels

A false story can now travel around the world before a newspaper has time to check a single fact. Researchers at one university followed thousands of messages and found that news that makes people angry circulates about six times faster than calm reporting.

Part of the reason is simple. Sharing a message costs nothing and takes one second, while checking it takes a phone call and half an hour.

Some editors now print a short line at the top of every report telling readers where the information came from. Early tests suggest that readers who see that line are slower to pass a story on, and more likely to ask questions first.

全文中譯

如今一則假消息可以在報社來不及查證任何一項事實之前，就傳遍全世界。某大學的研究人員追蹤了數千則訊息，發現令人生氣的新聞傳播速度，約是平和報導的六倍。

原因之一很簡單。分享一則訊息不花錢，只要一秒鐘；查證一則訊息卻要打一通電話、花上半小時。

現在有些編輯會在每篇報導最上方加一行短短的文字，告訴讀者這則消息的來源。初步測試顯示，看到那行字的讀者比較慢才把消息轉出去，也比較會先提出疑問。

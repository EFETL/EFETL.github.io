詞彙 Vocabulary

共 10 題／每題選出最適合題意的字詞
請由四個選項中，選出一個最適合句意的字或詞。
1The teacher asked the students to their essays carefully before handing them in, checking for spelling mistakes.
Aignore
Bwaste
Creview
Dborrow
✔ 正確答案：(C)　review
中譯老師要學生在交作文前仔細檢查，找出拼字錯誤。
解析checking for spelling mistakes（檢查拼字錯誤）暗示要「檢閱 review」文章。ignore 忽視、waste 浪費、borrow 借皆不合。
單字review 檢閱、複習、essay 文章、論說文、hand in 繳交
2Even though the exam was difficult, Nancy remained and finished every question on time.
Anervous
Bcalm
Cangry
Dsleepy
✔ 正確答案：(B)　calm
中譯儘管考試很難，Nancy 仍保持冷靜，準時做完每一題。
解析Even though（儘管）表對比：考試難卻能準時做完，須「冷靜 calm」。nervous 緊張、angry 生氣、sleepy 想睡皆與從容作答矛盾。
單字calm 冷靜的、remain 保持、仍然是、on time 準時
3The professor's lecture was so that many students took notes eagerly and asked questions afterward.
Aboring
Bconfusing
Cuseless
Dinspiring
✔ 正確答案：(D)　inspiring
中譯這位教授的講課如此激勵人心，許多學生熱切地做筆記，課後還提問。
解析took notes eagerly and asked questions（熱切做筆記、提問）顯示講課「激勵人心 inspiring」。boring 無聊、confusing 令人困惑、useless 無用皆相反。
單字inspiring 激勵人心的、lecture 講課、eagerly 熱切地
4Students who wish to for the scholarship must submit their applications before the end of March.
Aapply
Bsupply
Cimply
Dreply
✔ 正確答案：(A)　apply
中譯想申請這份獎學金的學生，必須在三月底前繳交申請表。
解析apply for a scholarship 為固定搭配「申請」獎學金。reply 回覆、supply 供應、imply 暗示皆不符。
單字apply 申請、scholarship 獎學金、submit 繳交、提交
5Learning a foreign language requires ; you cannot become fluent in just a few weeks.
Aluck
Bsilence
Cmoney
Dpatience
✔ 正確答案：(D)　patience
中譯學一門外語需要耐心；你無法在短短幾週內就變流利。
解析cannot become fluent in a few weeks（無法幾週就流利）暗示需要「耐心 patience」。luck 運氣、money 金錢、silence 沉默皆非關鍵。
單字patience 耐心、fluent 流利的、require 需要
6The library has a large of books, ranging from science to history and art.
Adirection
Bcollection
Creflection
Dselection
✔ 正確答案：(B)　collection
中譯這座圖書館有大量的藏書，範圍從科學到歷史和藝術都有。
解析a large collection of books 指大量「收藏」。direction 方向、reflection 反射、selection 選擇語意皆不如 collection 貼切。
單字collection 收藏、蒐集、range 範圍；涵蓋、library 圖書館
7Because he studied abroad for two years, Tom is now in both English and French.
Acurious
Bfluent
Cabsent
Dcareful
✔ 正確答案：(B)　fluent
中譯因為在國外留學兩年，Tom 現在英語和法語都很流利。
解析studied abroad for two years（留學兩年）造成兩種語言「流利 fluent」。curious 好奇、absent 缺席、careful 小心皆不合因果。
單字fluent 流利的、abroad 在國外、curious 好奇的
8The school will a special ceremony to welcome the new students next Monday.
Ahide
Bhurt
Chang
Dhold
✔ 正確答案：(D)　hold
中譯學校下週一將舉辦一場特別的典禮來歡迎新生。
解析hold a ceremony 為固定用法「舉辦典禮」。hide 躲藏、hurt 傷害、hang 懸掛皆不搭配。
單字hold 舉辦、握住、ceremony 典禮、welcome 歡迎
9The instructions were written so that no student had any trouble following the steps.
Aclearly
Brarely
Cloudly
Dquickly
✔ 正確答案：(A)　clearly
中譯這些說明寫得如此清楚，沒有一個學生在照著步驟做時遇到困難。
解析no student had any trouble following（沒人照做有困難）對應「清楚地 clearly」。rarely 很少、loudly 大聲、quickly 快速皆不合語意。
單字clearly 清楚地、instruction 說明、指示、follow 遵循、跟隨
10Regular exercise can greatly your ability to concentrate while studying.
Areduce
Bprevent
Cimprove
Dwaste
✔ 正確答案：(C)　improve
中譯規律運動能大幅提升你讀書時的專注能力。
解析句意正面，運動「提升 improve」專注力。reduce 減少、prevent 阻止、waste 浪費皆與正面效果不符。
單字improve 提升、改善、concentrate 專注、ability 能力

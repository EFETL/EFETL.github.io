Asking About a Day Center

Dear Ms. Weng,

My name is Hsu Pei-chun. My father, who is seventy-nine, has lived with me since his stroke last winter. He can still walk with a stick, but he cannot be left alone all day while I am at work.

A neighbor told me that Muzhen Day Center takes seniors from Monday to Friday. Could you tell me whether a nurse is on duty at all times, and whether your small bus can pick him up on Anhe Road? I would also like to visit next Tuesday afternoon before I make a decision.

Thank you for your help.

Hsu Pei-chun

全文中譯

翁小姐您好：

我叫徐佩君。我父親七十九歲，自從去年冬天中風後就和我同住。他還能拄著拐杖走路，但我上班時無法讓他整天獨自一人。

鄰居告訴我，木鎮日間照顧中心週一到週五收托長者。能否請您告知中心是否隨時都有護理師值班？貴中心的小巴士是否能到安和路接他？在決定之前，我也想在下週二下午前往參觀。

感謝您的協助。

徐佩君

ARTICLE
Older Workers Are Not a Problem

When people talk about an aging society, they usually picture crowded hospitals and empty classrooms. Yet many companies in Japan and Germany have discovered something different: workers over sixty are among their most reliable staff.

Older employees make fewer careless mistakes, stay in one job much longer, and pass their skills on to younger colleagues. What they need is not pity but flexible hours and lighter physical duties. Some factories have added better lighting and adjustable tables, and accidents there have dropped for workers of every age.

The lesson is simple. An aging workforce becomes a problem only when we insist that useful work must stop at a fixed age.

全文中譯

人們談到高齡社會時，通常想像的是擁擠的醫院和空蕩的教室。然而，日本和德國許多公司卻發現了不一樣的事：六十歲以上的員工是他們最可靠的人力之一。

年長員工比較少犯粗心的錯誤，在同一份工作待得更久，也把技術傳給年輕同事。他們需要的不是同情，而是彈性工時和較輕的體力工作。有些工廠加裝了更好的照明與可調式工作桌，結果各年齡層員工的職災都減少了。

道理很簡單。只有當我們堅持有用的工作必須在某個固定年齡結束時，高齡勞動力才會變成問題。

CMuch longer working hours

BAccidents dropped for workers of every age
CProduction stopped for a month
DWages fell sharply
✔ 正確答案：(B)　Accidents dropped for workers of every age

NOTICE
Pinecrest Campground Rules

Welcome to Pinecrest Campground

Please read these rules before you set up your tent.

  • Check-in starts at 2 p.m. Sites must be cleared by 11 a.m. on your final day.
  • Quiet hours are 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. No music or generators.
  • Open fires are allowed only in the metal rings beside the river. Gas stoves may be used at any site.
  • Dogs are welcome, but they must stay on a leash at all times.

Firewood is sold at the office for NT$120 a bag. Please do not bring wood from home, as it may carry insects that harm our trees. Showers are free for all guests.

全文中譯

歡迎來到松崖營地

搭帳篷前請先閱讀以下規定。

  • 下午兩點開始辦理入住。最後一天須於上午十一點前撤離營位。
  • 安靜時段為晚上十點至早上七點。請勿播放音樂或使用發電機。
  • 明火僅限於河邊的金屬火圈內使用。瓦斯爐則可在任何營位使用。
  • 歡迎攜帶狗，但必須全程繫上牽繩。

木柴於辦公室販售，每袋新台幣 120 元。請勿自家中攜帶木柴，因為其中可能帶有危害本地樹木的昆蟲。所有住客皆可免費使用淋浴設施。

ARTICLE
The Quiet Return of the Camp Stove

For many campers a fire is the heart of the night. Yet in dry seasons, when a single spark can start a wildfire, more and more parks forbid open flames, and visitors are turning to small gas stoves instead.

The change is not only about safety. A stove boils water in three minutes, leaves no ash, and works in wind or light rain. Wood, on the other hand, must be bought or gathered, and its smoke can bother neighbors at a crowded site.

Still, few campers give up fire completely. When the risk is low, they light one simply to sit around it and talk.

全文中譯

對許多露營者而言，火是夜晚的核心。然而在乾季，一顆火星就可能引發野火，因此越來越多公園禁止明火，遊客轉而改用小型瓦斯爐。

這項改變不只關乎安全。爐子三分鐘就能把水煮開，不留灰燼，而且在風中或小雨中都能使用。相對地，木柴必須購買或撿拾，而它的煙在擁擠的營地會打擾鄰居。

儘管如此，很少有露營者完全放棄生火。當風險不高時，他們仍會生一堆火，單純為了圍著它坐下來聊天。

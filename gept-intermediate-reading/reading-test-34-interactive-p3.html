ARTICLE
The Price of a Cheap T-shirt

A T-shirt that sells for a hundred dollars looks like a bargain, but the low price hides other costs. Cotton needs huge amounts of water, and the dye used to color it often ends up in rivers near the factory.

Speed is the second problem. Some brands now design a new collection every two weeks, so shoppers buy more and wear each piece fewer times. Millions of shirts are thrown away while they are still in good condition.

A few brands have begun to print clear care labels and to take back worn shirts. Shoppers can help by asking one question before paying: will I still wear this next year?

全文中譯

一件賣一百元的 T 恤看起來很划算，但低價背後藏著其他代價。棉花需要大量的水，而用來染色的染料常常流進工廠附近的河川。

第二個問題是速度。有些品牌現在每兩週就設計一個新系列，於是消費者買得更多，每件衣服穿的次數卻更少。數以百萬計的衣服在還很完好的狀態下就被丟棄。

少數品牌已開始印上清楚的保養標籤，並回收穿舊的衣服。消費者能幫上忙的方法很簡單：付錢前先問自己一個問題——明年我還會穿它嗎？

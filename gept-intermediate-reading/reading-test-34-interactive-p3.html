NOTICE
North Tower: New Entry Rules

From 1 March, everyone entering the North Tower must show a photo pass at the front desk.

  • Staff passes are collected from the security office on the ground floor.
  • Visitors receive a day pass and must be met at the desk by the person they are visiting.
  • A lost pass must be reported the same day. A new one costs NT$200.

Please do not hold the door open for anyone you do not know. Last month an intruder walked in this way and took two laptops. Report anything odd to the guard on duty, extension 1188.

全文中譯

自三月一日起，所有進入北棟大樓的人都必須在櫃檯出示附照片的通行證。

  • 員工證請至一樓的保全辦公室領取。
  • 訪客領取一日證，並須由受訪者到櫃檯迎接。
  • 通行證遺失須當日通報。補發一張費用為新台幣 200 元。

請勿為不認識的人擋門。上個月就有一名侵入者以這種方式進入，帶走了兩台筆記型電腦。發現任何異狀，請通報值班警衛，分機 1188。

EMAIL
A Letter from the Bakery

Dear Mrs. Chen,

I am writing to thank you. When the robbery happened last Friday, you were the only witness who stayed until the police arrived, and your description was so precise that two men were stopped the same evening.

Because of you, nobody in my shop became a victim of anything worse than a fright. The insurance company has already agreed to pay for the broken window.

Please come in on Saturday morning. Coffee and bread will be free for you for a whole year, and I will not take no for an answer.

Gratefully,
Sam Lo

全文中譯

陳太太您好：

我寫這封信是要向您道謝。上週五發生搶案時，您是唯一留下來等警察到場的目擊者，而且您的描述非常精確，兩名男子當晚就被攔下了。

因為有您，我店裡沒有人受到比受驚更嚴重的傷害。保險公司也已同意賠付破掉的窗戶。

請您週六早上過來一趟。往後整整一年，咖啡和麵包都免費招待您，這件事我可不接受推辭。

謹此致謝
羅山姆

ARTICLE
Why Phone Theft Is Falling

Ten years ago, stolen phones were a daily phenomenon in most large cities. Today the number has dropped sharply, and the reason is not more police on the streets.

Makers were finally forced to add a switch that turns a stolen phone into a useless piece of glass. A thief who cannot sell the thing will not take the risk of stealing it.

Some experts warn that criminals have simply moved online, where one bank password is worth more than any handset. Still, the lesson stands: good design can prevent crime far more cheaply than punishment.

全文中譯

十年前，手機失竊在多數大城市是每天都會發生的現象。如今數字大幅下降，原因並不是街上警力變多。

製造商終於被迫加入一個開關，可以把被偷的手機變成一塊沒用的玻璃。賣不掉東西的小偷，就不會冒險去偷。

有些專家警告，罪犯只是轉往網路，在那裡一組銀行密碼比任何一支手機都值錢。不過這個道理仍然成立：好的設計防止犯罪，遠比懲罰便宜得多。

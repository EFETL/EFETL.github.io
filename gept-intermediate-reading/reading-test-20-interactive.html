ARTICLE
Bringing a Dry Land Back to Life

Dry lands cover almost a third of the globe. In one corner of the African continent, farmers once watched their fields blow away: years of drought had left the soil bare and hard, and every heavy rain washed more of it into the river.

Then a simple idea spread from village to village. Dig a shallow half-moon pit around each young tree. The pit holds rainwater long enough for the roots to drink, and the damp ground slowly comes back to life.

The farmers built no large dam and bought no machines. Twenty years later, five million trees stand where there was only dust, and young men who had left for the city are coming home.

全文中譯

乾旱土地覆蓋了將近三分之一的地球。在非洲大陸的某個角落，農民曾眼睜睜看著自己的田地被風吹走：多年的乾旱讓土壤變得光禿又硬實，每一場大雨又把更多土沖進河裡。

後來，一個簡單的做法從一個村子傳到另一個村子。在每棵小樹周圍挖一個淺淺的半月形坑。這個坑能把雨水留住夠久，讓樹根喝到水，潮濕的土地也慢慢恢復生機。

農民沒有蓋大型水壩，也沒有買機器。二十年後，原本只有沙塵的地方長出了五百萬棵樹，當年離鄉進城的年輕人也陸續回來了。

33Which station is the wettest in every year shown?
ACoast
BRiver Basin
CDry Plain
DMountain Peak
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Mountain Peak
中譯表中每一年雨量最多的是哪一個測站？
解析Mountain Peak 三年分別是 3400、3100、3520，都遠高於其他三站。River Basin 最高也只有 2050。
34In which year did all four stations get their least rain?
A2022
B2024
C2023
DThe years are the same
✔ 正確答案：(C)　2023
中譯四個測站都在哪一年雨量最少？
解析四列的 2023 數字（1150、1400、3100、410）都是該列最小值，也與文末所說的 the drought of 2023 相符。
35What have the farmers on the plain done since the drought?
AThey built a new dam
BThey moved to the coast
CThey share one deep well
DThey stopped growing rice
✔ 正確答案：(C)　They share one deep well
中譯乾旱之後，平原上的農民做了什麼？
解析最後一句 Farmers there now share one deep well。搬家、築壩、停止種稻都不是文中提到的做法。

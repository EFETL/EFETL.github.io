NOTICE
Green Valley Farm — Open Weekend

Saturday and Sunday, 12–13 October, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

  • Guided walk through the wheat fields, starting every hour
  • Meet the sheep and watch a shepherd work with his dogs (11:00 only)
  • Children may help feed the poultry behind the old barn

Entry is free for village residents; other visitors pay NT$100. Please park in the field by the main gate, not on the narrow lane.

The farm shop sells vegetables picked that morning, as well as bread and honey. Cash only.

Dogs must stay on a lead at all times, as the cattle are easily frightened.

  • 認識綿羊，並觀看牧羊人與牧羊犬工作（僅 11:00 一場）
  • 孩童可在老穀倉後方協助餵家禽

本村居民免費入場；其他訪客每人新台幣 100 元。請把車停在大門旁的空地，不要停在狹窄的小路上。

農場商店販售當天早上採收的蔬菜，以及麵包與蜂蜜。僅收現金。

EMAIL
Volunteers for the Apple Harvest

Dear Ms. Rowe,

Thank you for offering to send students to help with our apple harvest next month.

We can take up to twelve people on any weekday between 5 and 16 November. Work starts at eight and finishes at three, and lunch is served in the barn. Volunteers should bring boots and a warm coat; we supply gloves and baskets.

One thing I must mention: the ladders are heavy, so students under sixteen may only pick from the lower branches.

Could you confirm the number of students and the dates by 25 October? After that we will have to hire extra workers instead.

Best wishes,
Daniel Hsu

全文中譯

羅小姐您好：

謝謝您願意派學生來幫忙我們下個月的蘋果採收。

11 月 5 日至 16 日之間的任一個平日，我們最多可接受十二人。工作八點開始、三點結束，午餐在穀倉供應。志工請自備雨鞋和保暖外套；手套與提籃由我們提供。

有一點必須說明：梯子很重，因此未滿十六歲的學生只能採較低的枝條。

可否請您在 10 月 25 日前確認人數與日期？逾期我們就必須改僱臨時工人。

祝好
徐丹尼

ARTICLE
Young Farmers Come Home

For thirty years the young people of Ho-ping left for the cities, and the fields slowly turned to grass. Recently something has changed.

A small number of people in their thirties have come back, but they do not farm the way their grandparents did. They grow fewer kinds of crops and sell them directly to restaurants, which pay far more than a wholesale market. Several of them share one expensive machine instead of each buying one.

Those who have returned admit that the work is hard and the first two years brought almost no income. Still, the village school, which was due to close, now has enough children to stay open for another decade.

全文中譯

三十年來，和平村的年輕人紛紛前往城市，田地慢慢長滿了草。最近情況有了改變。

少數三十多歲的人回來了，但他們務農的方式和祖父母那一輩不同。他們種的作物種類較少，並直接賣給餐廳，價格遠高於批發市場。其中好幾個人共用一台昂貴的機器，而不是各買一台。

這些返鄉者坦承工作辛苦，前兩年幾乎沒有收入。儘管如此，原本即將關閉的村中小學，如今學童人數已足以再開下去十年。

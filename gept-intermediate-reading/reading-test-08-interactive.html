ARTICLE
What a Good Cry Really Does

Why do people feel lighter after a good cry? For years scientists believed that tears only washed dust out of the eye. Newer studies point to something more useful: tears of sadness carry away chemicals that the body no longer needs.

There is a second reason. A person in tears is easy to notice, so crying quietly asks for help without using a single word. A friend who arrives with a cup of tea often does more good than any clever advice.

Tears alone, however, cure nothing. Doctors warn that a low mood lasting for months can depress every part of daily life and should be treated rather than hidden.

全文中譯

人為什麼在好好哭一場之後會覺得輕鬆？多年來科學家認為眼淚只是把灰塵沖出眼睛。較新的研究指出更有用的作用：悲傷的眼淚會帶走身體不再需要的化學物質。

還有第二個原因。淚眼婆娑的人很容易被注意到，所以安靜地哭泣等於不用說一個字就在求助。帶著一杯茶出現的朋友，往往比任何聰明的建議更有幫助。

然而，光是流淚並不能治好什麼。醫師提醒，持續數月的低落心情會使日常生活的每個層面都變得沮喪，應該接受治療，而不是隱藏起來。

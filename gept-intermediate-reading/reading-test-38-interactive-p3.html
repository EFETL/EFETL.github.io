An Email to a Tea Supplier

Dear Mr. Kuo,

I run Lantern Alley Tea House on Anhe Road, and we have bought leaves from your farm for two years. This month's box arrived three days late, and half of it was the wrong type — jasmine instead of oolong.

Because of the delay, I had to close early last Saturday, which is normally our busiest day. I would like the correct leaves sent by Friday, and I hope we can agree on a small discount for this order.

I also plan to open a second shop in October, so I would like to discuss a larger monthly order once this matter is settled.

Best regards,
Chen Yi-hua

全文中譯

敬愛的郭先生：

我在安和路經營「燈籠巷茶館」，向貴農場採購茶葉已有兩年。這個月的貨箱遲了三天才到，而且其中一半還送錯品項——是茉莉花茶而不是烏龍茶。

因為這次延誤，我上週六必須提早打烊，而週六通常是我們最忙的一天。我希望正確的茶葉能在週五前送達，也希望我們能就這批訂單談一點折扣。

我另外打算在十月開第二家店，因此等這件事解決後，我想和您討論加大每月的訂購量。

陳依樺 敬上

Questions 21–24
21Why does Chen Yi-hua write this email?
ATo order new furniture
BTo apply for a job at the farm
CTo praise the delivery service
DTo report a problem with an order
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To report a problem with an order
中譯陳依樺為什麼寫這封信？
解析第二段指出貨箱遲到三天且品項送錯，整封信的目的是反映訂單出問題並要求處理。信中並未提到家具、應徵工作，也沒有稱讚送貨服務。
22What was wrong with this month's box?
AIt was too small
BHalf of it was the wrong tea
CIt was never delivered
DIt cost twice as much
✔ 正確答案：(B)　Half of it was the wrong tea
中譯這個月的貨箱出了什麼問題？
解析信中寫 half of it was the wrong type — jasmine instead of oolong（一半送成茉莉花茶而非烏龍茶）。貨有送到，只是遲到，故 C 錯；價格問題與尺寸文中都未提及。
23What happened because of the late delivery?
AThe shop closed early one day
BA worker quit the job
CThe price of tea went up
DThe farm lost its licence
✔ 正確答案：(A)　The shop closed early one day
中譯延遲送達造成了什麼後果？
解析文中說 I had to close early last Saturday（上週六必須提早打烊），是延誤造成的直接結果。其餘選項信中皆未提及。
24What does Chen Yi-hua plan to do in October?
AChange to another supplier
BVisit the tea farm
COpen another shop
DRaise her drink prices
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Open another shop
中譯陳依樺十月打算做什麼？
解析第三段明寫 I also plan to open a second shop in October（十月要開第二家店）。她想加大訂單而非換供應商，因此 A 與文意相反。

Peartree Handmade Soap

Peartree Soap — Now Open on Xinsheng Road!

After three years of making soap in her own kitchen, Ah-Ping has finally opened a real shop. Every bar is cut and wrapped by hand.

  • Opening week (June 1–7): buy two bars, get one free
  • Soap-making class: Saturdays, 2–4 p.m., NT$500 per person
  • Free gift wrapping for orders over NT$800
  • Bring your own box and take NT$20 off

We are closed on Mondays. Small orders for weddings and offices are welcome — please ask at least two weeks in advance.

全文中譯

梨樹手工皂——新生路新店開幕！

在自家廚房做了三年肥皂之後，阿蘋終於開了一間實體店面。每一塊皂都是手工裁切與包裝。

  • 開幕週（6/1–6/7）：買二送一
  • 手工皂課程：每週六下午 2–4 點，每人 NT$500
  • 免費禮品包裝，消費滿 NT$800 適用
  • 自備容器可折抵 NT$20

每週一公休。歡迎婚禮與公司行號的小量訂購——請至少提前兩週詢問。

ARTICLE
Lessons from a Night Market Stall

At nineteen, Wu Chia-hao borrowed NT$30,000 from his aunt and opened a fried-mushroom stall in a night market. He sold almost nothing for the first two evenings. Instead of giving up, he stood beside a busy stall and counted how long people were willing to wait in line.

He learned that hungry visitors will not wait more than five minutes, so he changed his fryer and simplified his packing. Sales doubled within a month. Today Wu owns three stalls and trains new sellers.

His advice is simple: your first idea is rarely your best one, and the market itself will teach you—if you are willing to watch and change.

全文中譯

十九歲那年，吳家豪向阿姨借了三萬元，在夜市開了一個炸香菇攤。頭兩個晚上他幾乎什麼都沒賣出去。他沒有放棄，而是站到一個生意很好的攤子旁邊，計算人們願意排隊等多久。

他發現肚子餓的遊客不會等超過五分鐘，於是換了油炸鍋、簡化包裝方式。一個月內業績就翻了一倍。如今吳家豪擁有三個攤位，還負責訓練新的攤商。

他的建議很簡單：你的第一個點子很少會是最好的，而市場本身就會教你——只要你願意觀察並改變。

Questions 29–32
29Where did Wu get the money to start his stall?
AFrom a bank
BFrom his aunt
CFrom a school prize
DFrom the government
✔ 正確答案：(B)　From his aunt
中譯吳家豪創業的錢從哪裡來？
解析首句 borrowed NT$30,000 from his aunt（向阿姨借了三萬元）。文中未提及銀行、獎金或政府補助。
30What did Wu do after two evenings without sales?
AHe closed the stall
BHe lowered his prices
CHe hired a helper
DHe watched a busy stall
✔ 正確答案：(D)　He watched a busy stall
中譯兩晚沒生意之後，吳家豪做了什麼？
解析文中 Instead of giving up, he stood beside a busy stall and counted…（他站到熱門攤子旁觀察並計算等待時間）。Instead of giving up 直接否定了 A；降價與請人手文中皆未出現。
31What did Wu learn about night market visitors?
AThey prefer cheap food
BThey come mostly on weekends
CThey dislike waiting long
DThey enjoy trying new flavors
✔ 正確答案：(C)　They dislike waiting long
中譯關於夜市遊客，吳家豪學到了什麼？
解析第二段寫 hungry visitors will not wait more than five minutes（不會等超過五分鐘），因此他改善出餐速度。價格、週末人潮與新口味都不是文中的觀察重點。
32What is the main message of the article?
ABe ready to change your first idea
BBorrow as much money as possible
CNight markets are slowly dying
DYoung people should not start a business
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Be ready to change your first idea
中譯這篇文章的主旨為何？
解析末段 your first idea is rarely your best one…if you are willing to watch and change，強調願意觀察並修正。文章對創業持正面態度，故 D 相反；B、C 文中皆無依據。

Startup Costs of a Small Cafe

Ms. Tsai kept a record of what she spent before opening her twelve-seat cafe last year.

ItemCost (NT$)Note
Rent (3 months)120,000Paid before opening
Coffee machine85,000Bought secondhand
Tables and chairs40,000Made by a local carpenter
Signboard and menus15,000Designed by a friend

Ms. Tsai suggests keeping another three months of rent in the bank after opening, since few cafes earn a profit in their first season.

全文中譯

蔡小姐記錄了去年開設十二個座位的咖啡館之前所有的花費。

項目費用（新台幣）備註
租金（三個月）120,000開幕前先付
咖啡機85,000購買二手品
桌椅40,000由在地木工製作
招牌與菜單15,000由朋友設計

蔡小姐建議開幕後在銀行再留三個月的租金，因為很少有咖啡館在第一季就開始獲利。

Questions 33–35

AThe coffee machine
BThe tables and chairs
CThe rent
DThe signboard and menus

解析表格中租金 120,000 元最高，高於咖啡機的 85,000 元。桌椅 40,000 與招牌菜單 15,000 更低。
34How did Ms. Tsai save money on the coffee machine?
AShe bought a used one
BShe rented it monthly
CShe borrowed it from a friend
DShe won it in a contest
✔ 正確答案：(A)　She bought a used one
中譯蔡小姐在咖啡機上如何省錢？
解析備註欄寫 Bought secondhand（購買二手品）。朋友幫忙的是設計招牌與菜單，不是提供咖啡機。
35What does Ms. Tsai advise new owners to do?
AChoose a bigger space
BHire a famous designer
COpen the shop in summer
DKeep three months of rent in the bank
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Keep three months of rent in the bank
中譯蔡小姐建議新老闆做什麼？
解析文末 keeping another three months of rent in the bank after opening（開幕後再留三個月租金）。她自己反而是找朋友設計、買二手機器，故 B 與文意相反。

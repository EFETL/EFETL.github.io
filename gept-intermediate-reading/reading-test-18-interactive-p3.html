Central Bus Terminal: Notice to Passengers

Central Bus Terminal — Notice to Passengers

  • From 1 May all long-distance buses will leave from Platform C, not Platform A.
  • Please buy your ticket before going aboard. Drivers no longer take cash.
  • Each passenger may carry two bags free of charge. A third bag costs NT$50.
  • The waiting room opens at 5 a.m. and closes thirty minutes after the last bus leaves.

Lost property is kept for fourteen days at the small office beside Gate 1. Please bring your ticket when you come to collect it.

ARTICLE
Quiet Cars on Busy Roads

Ten years ago an electric car was a rare sight on the roads of Taiwan. Today about one in six new vehicles sold in the country runs on a battery, and the number is still rising.

Drivers give two reasons. Charging at home costs less than filling a tank, and the cars are far quieter, which pleases neighbors in crowded districts.

Problems remain. There are still few charging points outside the cities, so a long trip needs careful planning. Repair shops also complain that they must retrain their staff, because an electric motor has far fewer moving parts than an old engine.

全文中譯

十年前，電動車在台灣的道路上還是罕見的景象。如今，全國每賣出六輛新車，大約就有一輛靠電池行駛，而且數字仍在上升。

駕駛人提出兩個理由。在家充電比加滿一缸油便宜，而且車子安靜許多，這讓擁擠社區的鄰居很滿意。

問題依然存在。城市以外的充電站仍然很少，因此長途行程需要仔細規劃。修車廠也抱怨必須重新訓練員工，因為電動馬達的可動零件比舊式引擎少得多。

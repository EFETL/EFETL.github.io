NOTICE
Wenchang Farmers' Market Rules

Welcome to the Wenchang Farmers' Market, open every Saturday from 7 a.m. to noon.

  • All sellers must grow or make what they sell. Reselling goods from wholesalers is not allowed.
  • Stalls must be ready before 6:40 a.m., and all vehicles must leave the square by 6:50 a.m.
  • Please bring your own bags. We no longer hand out plastic ones.
  • Free tasting is welcome, but samples must be kept covered.

Lost items are held at the information tent near the north gate. If it rains heavily, the market moves to the covered walkway beside the station.

全文中譯

歡迎光臨文昌農夫市集，每週六上午 7 點至中午 12 點營業。

  • 所有攤商必須販售自己種植或製作的產品，不得轉售批發商的貨品。
  • 攤位須於上午 6 點 40 分前備妥，所有車輛須於 6 點 50 分前駛離廣場。
  • 請自備購物袋，我們不再發放塑膠袋。
  • 歡迎免費試吃，但試吃樣品必須加蓋。

失物統一保管於北門旁的服務帳篷。若遇大雨，市集將移至車站旁的有頂走廊。

EMAIL
This Week's Vegetable Box

Dear Box Members,

Here is what is going into your box this Thursday: sweet potato leaves, long beans, two kinds of eggplant, and the first pineapples of the year.

Sadly, there will be no tomatoes. Two nights of heavy rain split most of the fruit, and split tomatoes go bad within a day. To make up for this, we have added extra ginger and a small jar of our own chili sauce.

Please remember to leave your empty basket outside your door on Wednesday evening. Our driver picks them up before sunrise.

Warm regards,
Ho Chia-wei, Two Hills Farm

全文中譯

親愛的菜箱會員：

本週四您的箱子裡會有：地瓜葉、長豆、兩種茄子，以及今年第一批鳳梨。

可惜這週沒有番茄。連續兩晚的大雨讓大部分果實裂開，而裂開的番茄一天內就會壞掉。為了彌補，我們多加了一些薑，以及一小罐自製辣椒醬。

請記得在週三傍晚把空籃子放在門外。我們的司機會在日出前來收。

兩山農場　何佳薇　敬上

ARTICLE
Who Will Farm Tomorrow?

In many countries the average farmer is now over sixty, and fewer young people are willing to take over the land. The work is hard, the income is uncertain, and a single typhoon can wipe out a whole year of effort.

Yet the picture is not entirely dark. Some young returnees are finding new ways to survive: selling directly through social media, opening their fields to city visitors for farm stays, or growing crops that machines cannot easily replace.

Governments can help by making farmland easier to rent and by teaching business skills, not only farming skills. Without such support, tomorrow's fields may be quiet indeed.

全文中譯

在許多國家，農民的平均年齡如今已超過六十歲，而願意接手土地的年輕人愈來愈少。工作辛苦、收入不穩定，而一個颱風就可能毀掉整整一年的努力。

然而情況並非全然黯淡。一些返鄉的年輕人正找到新的生存方式：透過社群媒體直接銷售、開放田地讓城市旅客體驗農村住宿，或種植機器不易取代的作物。

政府可以提供協助，讓農地更容易租用，並教導經營管理的技能，而不只是耕作技術。若缺少這樣的支持，明日的田野恐怕真的會一片寂靜。

What Is in Season at Two Hills Farm?

Two Hills Farm sells different crops through the year. The table shows the main months and the usual price.

CropMain SeasonPrice per kg
Bamboo shootsApril–JuneNT$120
PineapplesMay–JulyNT$60
Sweet potatoesOctober–JanuaryNT$45
StrawberriesDecember–MarchNT$320

Prices drop by about a fifth in the middle of each season. Box members pay ten percent less all year round.

全文中譯

兩山農場全年販售不同作物。下表列出主要產季與一般價格。

作物主要產季每公斤價格
竹筍四月至六月NT$120
鳳梨五月至七月NT$60
地瓜十月至隔年一月NT$45
草莓十二月至隔年三月NT$320

每個產季的中期，價格約下降五分之一。菜箱會員全年皆可少付一成。

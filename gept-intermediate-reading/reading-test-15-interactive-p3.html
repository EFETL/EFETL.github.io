ARTICLE
The Pianist Who Plays for Empty Halls

At seven every morning, Chen Yi-ting unlocks a small practice room and plays to nobody for three hours. She has done this for nineteen years.

Audiences see only the evening: the lights, the flowers, the bow at the end. They rarely imagine the slow, dusty mornings, or the two years she once spent on a single sonata.

Chen is not a famous pianist. She teaches sixteen students a week and plays perhaps four concerts a year. Yet she says she has never once thought of stopping. Music, she explains, is the only work she knows that never lets her feel finished.

全文中譯

每天早上七點，陳依婷會打開一間小練習室，對著空無一人的房間彈三個小時。這件事她已經做了十九年。

觀眾看見的只有晚上：燈光、鮮花、以及結束時的一鞠躬。他們很少想像那些緩慢而佈滿灰塵的早晨，或她曾經花在一首奏鳴曲上的兩年。

陳依婷不是有名的鋼琴家。她一週教十六個學生，一年大約演出四場音樂會。但她說自己從沒想過要停下來。她解釋，音樂是她所知唯一一份永遠不會讓她覺得已經做完的工作。

Questions 21–24
21How long has Chen followed this morning routine?
AFor four years
BFor seven years
CSince she was sixteen
DFor nineteen years
✔ 正確答案：(D)　For nineteen years
中譯陳依婷這樣的晨間作息維持了多久？
解析第一段最後 She has done this for nineteen years。seven 是時間點、four 是每年場次，都不是年數。
22What do audiences rarely think about?
AThe flowers she receives
BThe lights on the stage
CHer long morning practice
DThe price of the tickets
✔ 正確答案：(C)　Her long morning practice
中譯觀眾很少想到什麼？
解析They rarely imagine the slow, dusty mornings，燈光與鮮花正是觀眾看得到的部分。
23How much teaching does Chen do?
AShe teaches four students a year
BShe teaches sixteen students a week
CShe does not teach at all
DShe teaches only during the summer
✔ 正確答案：(B)　She teaches sixteen students a week
中譯陳依婷教多少學生？
解析She teaches sixteen students a week；four 指的是一年的音樂會場次。
24Why does Chen keep playing?
AMusic never lets her feel finished
BShe needs the money from concerts
CShe hopes to become famous
DHer students ask her to perform
✔ 正確答案：(A)　Music never lets her feel finished
中譯陳依婷為什麼持續彈下去？
解析最後一句 Music ... never lets her feel finished。文中強調她並不有名，也沒提到經濟因素。

Spring Arts Week - City Center

Five days, one ticket.

  • Mon to Wed: an exhibition of student paintings, Old Fire Station, free entry
  • Thursday 7:30 p.m.: opera night at the Grand Theater, NT$600

線上報名於三月二十日中午截止。大劇院座位不劃位，請提早入場。

EMAIL
Letter to Parents: Class Exhibition

Dear parents,

Our students will exhibit a year of work in the school library from 10 to 14 June.

Each child has chosen one creation to show: a painting, a small sculpture, or a set of photographs. Nothing is for sale, but visitors are welcome to leave a written comment beside any piece.

We also need four parents to help carry tables on the afternoon of 9 June. Please reply to this message if you are free after two o'clock.

The library is on the third floor, and the lift is out of order that week, so please allow extra time.

Best wishes,
Ms. Ho, Art Department

全文中譯

各位家長您好：

本校學生將於六月十日至十四日在學校圖書館展出這一年的作品。

每個孩子都挑了一件創作展出：一幅畫、一件小型雕塑，或一組照片。所有作品皆不販售，但歡迎參觀者在任何一件作品旁留下文字評語。

另外，我們需要四位家長在六月九日下午幫忙搬桌子。若您兩點以後有空，請回覆這封信。

圖書館位於三樓，該週電梯故障，請預留多一點時間。

敬祝安好
美術科　何老師

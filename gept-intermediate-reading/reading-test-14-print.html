ARTICLE
Why Cats Sleep So Much

Cat owners often joke that their pets do nothing but sleep. In fact, an adult cat rests for about sixteen hours a day. This habit comes from its wild ancestors, which hunted small animals at dawn and at dusk. Hunting uses a huge amount of energy in a short time, so long rests were necessary to stay ready for the next chase.

Indoor cats no longer need to hunt, yet the habit remains. Owners who worry about a lazy pet can help by playing with it for ten minutes twice a day. A sudden change in sleeping hours, however, may be a sign of illness and is worth a visit to the vet.

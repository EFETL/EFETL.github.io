Learning a Craft from a Screen

Ten years ago, anyone who wanted to learn woodworking had to find a teacher. Today a beginner can watch a free video and cut a first board the same afternoon. Craft teachers admit that this has brought many new people into the workshop.

Still, they warn that a screen cannot correct your hands. A video never notices that you are holding the chisel at a dangerous angle, and it never smells the glue burning. One studio owner in Hsinchu now asks her students to watch the videos at home and come to class only to practice. Her students, she says, learn twice as fast — but only because someone is standing beside them.

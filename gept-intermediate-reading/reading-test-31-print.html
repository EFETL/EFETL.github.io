ARTICLE
The Coach Behind the Screen

When Wei Chun-hsiang joined a professional team at eighteen, he practiced twelve hours a day and won almost nothing. Five years later he became a coach, and his players now train only seven hours a day — yet they win far more often.

His method surprises people. Half of every session is spent watching recordings of matches they lost, with the sound turned off. Wei believes players learn faster when they see their own habits instead of hearing advice. He also forbids practice after eleven at night. As he puts it, a tired brain repeats mistakes rather than fixing them.

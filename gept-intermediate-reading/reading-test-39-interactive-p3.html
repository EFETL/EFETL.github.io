ARTICLE
The Two-Hour Volunteer

Most charities want people who can promise a whole day every week. Fewer and fewer people can. A group in Tainan decided to try the opposite and asked for two hours, once a month, with no long-term promise at all.

In the first year 340 people signed up, four times the number the old system had attracted. Only a third stayed beyond twelve months, but the manager is not worried. She says a short, pleasant experience makes people return later, often once their own children have grown up.

Other groups are now copying the idea, though a few complain that training so many newcomers is expensive.

全文中譯

大多數慈善機構想要的，是能承諾每週投入一整天的人。而能做到的人越來越少。台南有個團體決定反其道而行，只要求每月兩小時，完全不必做長期承諾。

第一年就有 340 人報名，是舊制度所吸引人數的四倍。留下超過十二個月的只有三分之一，但負責人並不擔心。她說，一次短暫而愉快的經驗會讓人日後再回來，往往是在自己的孩子長大之後。

其他團體現在也開始仿效這個做法，不過也有少數人抱怨，訓練這麼多新手的成本很高。

Questions 21–24
21What did the group in Tainan change?
AThey accepted only students
BThey began paying their volunteers
CThey stopped training people
DThey asked for shorter, occasional help
✔ 正確答案：(D)　They asked for shorter, occasional help
中譯台南那個團體改變了什麼？
解析asked for two hours, once a month, with no long-term promise，把長時間承諾改為短時間、偶爾參與。
22How many people signed up in the first year?
AA third of the usual number
BFour
C340
DTwelve
✔ 正確答案：(C)　340
中譯第一年有多少人報名？
解析In the first year 340 people signed up。四倍指的是與舊制度相比的倍數，不是人數。
23Why is the manager not worried that many people leave?

BThey may come back years later

DThe work will soon be finished
✔ 正確答案：(B)　They may come back years later
中譯為何負責人不擔心許多人離開？

NOTICE
Beach Clean-up — Volunteers Welcome

Sunday 14 April, 8:00 a.m. — meet at the car park by the lighthouse

  • Bags, gloves and grabbers are provided; please wear closed shoes
  • Children under twelve must stay with an adult at all times
  • Do not touch glass, needles or anything you cannot name — tell a leader

If heavy rain is forecast, the event moves to the following Sunday. A message will be sent by nine o'clock on Saturday evening.

  • 垃圾袋、手套與夾子由主辦提供；請穿包鞋
  • 未滿十二歲的孩童必須全程與大人同行
  • 請勿觸碰玻璃、針頭或任何你叫不出名字的東西——請告訴帶隊人員

我們十一點結束，之後在海灘小屋供應熱湯。請自備杯子。

若預報有大雨，活動順延至下個週日。我們會在週六晚上九點前發出通知。

EMAIL
Thank You from the Reading Club

Dear Mr. Yeh,

On behalf of everyone at the Riverside Reading Club, thank you for the forty books you sent last week.

We have kept twenty-eight of them for our own shelves. The rest are picture books for very young children, so we have passed them to the hospital play room; we thought you would rather see them used than stored.

May I ask one more favour? Several of our helpers would love to hear how you built up such a collection. Would you consider giving a short talk one Thursday evening in June?

With thanks,
Amy Ko

全文中譯

葉先生您好：

謹代表河濱閱讀社全體成員，感謝您上週寄來的四十本書。

其中二十八本我們留在自己的書架上。其餘是給幼兒看的圖畫書，我們已經轉送給醫院的遊戲室；我們想您應該寧願看到它們被使用，而不是被收起來。

可以再請您幫個忙嗎？我們有幾位志工很想聽聽您是如何累積出這樣一批藏書的。您願意在六月的某個週四晚上做一場短講嗎？

謹此致謝
柯艾咪

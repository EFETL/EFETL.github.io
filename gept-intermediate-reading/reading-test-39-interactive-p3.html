NOTICE
Open Studio Weekend

On the last weekend of March, twenty-six artists at Cangku Art Village will open their working rooms to the public.

  • Dates: March 29–30, 11 a.m.–6 p.m.
  • Entry: free; no booking needed
  • Paint-your-own-tile: NT$150, sign up at the gate
  • Guided tour: 2 p.m. daily, meets by the water tower

Please do not touch unfinished work, and ask the artist before taking photos inside a studio. Most artists sell small pieces directly, and cash is preferred. Parking is very limited, so visitors are encouraged to take Bus 66 from Beimen Station.

  • 日期：3 月 29–30 日，上午 11 點至下午 6 點
  • 入場：免費；無須預約
  • 彩繪自己的磁磚：NT$150，於大門處報名
  • 導覽：每日下午 2 點，於水塔旁集合

請勿觸摸未完成的作品，在工作室內拍照前請先徵求藝術家同意。多數藝術家會直接販售小件作品，並以現金為佳。停車位非常有限，建議訪客從北門站搭乘 66 路公車前來。

ARTICLE
Why Children Stop Drawing

Almost every four-year-old draws happily. By the age of twelve, most have stopped. Researchers who study art education blame one simple thing: comparison. Young children draw to tell a story, but older children begin to judge their work against a classmate's, or against a photograph.

One school in Tainan tried an experiment. For a whole term, teachers gave no marks for art and never displayed a 「best」 drawing. Instead, every student kept a private sketchbook. By June, twice as many students said they drew at home for fun.

The lesson may be that children do not lose their talent; they lose their courage.

全文中譯

幾乎每個四歲小孩都樂於畫畫。到了十二歲，大多數人卻不再畫了。研究藝術教育的學者把原因歸咎於一件很簡單的事：比較。年幼的孩子畫畫是為了說故事，但年紀較大的孩子會開始拿自己的作品和同學的、或和照片相比。

台南有一所學校做了個實驗。整整一個學期，老師都不為美術打分數，也從不張貼「最佳」畫作。取而代之的是，每位學生保有一本私人的速寫本。到了六月，表示自己在家會為樂趣而畫的學生人數增加為兩倍。

這件事的啟示或許是：孩子失去的不是天分，而是勇氣。

Questions 25–28
25According to researchers, why do older children stop drawing?
AThey have less free time
BTheir hands grow slowly
CThey compare their work with others'
DSchools remove art classes
✔ 正確答案：(C)　They compare their work with others'
中譯根據研究者的說法，年紀較大的孩子為何不再畫畫？
解析文中直接點名 blame one simple thing: comparison，並說明孩子會拿自己的作品和同學或照片相比。時間、手部發育、學校刪課皆非文中理由。
26What did the school in Tainan do during the experiment?
AIt stopped giving marks for art
BIt invited famous artists
CIt bought expensive paints
DIt held a drawing contest
✔ 正確答案：(A)　It stopped giving marks for art
中譯台南那所學校在實驗期間做了什麼？
解析第二段寫 teachers gave no marks for art and never displayed a 「best」 drawing。舉辦比賽正好與「不評比」的做法相反。
27What was the result of the experiment?
AStudents drew much faster
BMore students drew at home
CArt marks became higher
DParents complained to the school
✔ 正確答案：(B)　More students drew at home
中譯這項實驗的結果是什麼？
解析文中 twice as many students said they drew at home for fun（在家為樂趣而畫的學生變成兩倍）。既然不打分數，就不會有分數變高的結果。
28What does the last sentence suggest?
ATalent disappears with age
BSketchbooks are a waste of paper
CTeachers should mark more strictly
DConfidence matters more than talent
✔ 正確答案：(D)　Confidence matters more than talent
中譯最後一句話暗示了什麼？
解析末句 children do not lose their talent; they lose their courage 明確否定 A，並強調勇氣（自信）才是關鍵。全文主張少評比，故 C 與文意相反。
EMAIL
An Email About a Watercolor Class

Dear Ms. Hsu,

Thank you for last Saturday's watercolor class. I joined as a complete beginner and was afraid that I would slow the whole group down, but your step-by-step demonstration made everything clear.

I have two small requests. First, could you email me the list of brushes you used? I only managed to write down three of them. Second, my sister would love to join in April, but she works every Saturday. Are you planning to open a weekday evening class?

If you are, please put both our names on the list.

Warm wishes,
Ariel Kao

全文中譯

敬愛的許老師：

謝謝您上週六的水彩課。我是以全然的初學者身分參加，原本很怕會拖累整組進度，但您一步一步的示範讓一切都變得清楚易懂。

我有兩個小小的請求。第一，可以請您把您用的畫筆清單寄給我嗎？我只來得及抄下其中三支。第二，我妹妹很想在四月加入，但她每週六都要上班。請問您有打算開平日晚間的班嗎？

如果有的話，請把我們兩人的名字都列上去。

高艾莉 敬上

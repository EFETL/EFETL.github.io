ARTICLE
The Trees That Cool a City

A street lined with trees can be four degrees cooler than a bare street nearby. The reason is not only shade. Water rises through the stem of each tree and escapes through the leaves, taking heat with it.

Choosing the right species matters. Trees that grow fast often have soft timber and break in typhoons. Slower-growing trees cost more to plant but last for decades.

City planners now also count the birds. A single mature tree may hold dozens of nests, and the insects it feeds keep gardens healthy. Cutting one down removes far more than wood.

Student Laboratory Rules

Room B-204: Student Laboratory Rules

  • Put on the safety glasses by the door before you touch any bottle.
  • No food or drink inside the room, not even water.
  • Write every observation in your own notebook, never on loose paper.
  • Leave the benches clean; the last group of the day washes the glassware.

Any experimental work after 5 p.m. must be booked with Dr. Hsu two days in advance, and no student may stay in the room alone. Broken glass goes into the red box, never into the paper bin.

ARTICLE
The Clock That Never Hurries

An atomic clock in a locked room in Colorado loses less than one second in a hundred million years. Few people need that kind of accuracy, yet almost everyone uses it, because every phone map depends on satellites that carry such clocks.

The scientific idea behind them is simple. Atoms of one metal always take exactly the same tiny amount of time to change their state, so counting those changes is more reliable than counting the swings of a pendulum.

Engineers are now building a clock that is ten times more accurate. Their aim is not to be on time for dinner, but to measure how gravity slightly slows time itself.

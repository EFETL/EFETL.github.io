ARTICLE
Why Buses Come in Groups

Have you ever waited twenty minutes for a bus, only to see three arrive at once? Traffic experts call this bus bunching, and it happens far more often than riders think.

The reason is simple. If one bus falls a little behind, more passengers gather at the next stop, so loading them takes longer. That bus grows later and later, while the bus behind it finds fewer riders and slowly catches up. Before long, the two are travelling side by side.

Some cities fight the problem by telling drivers to skip certain stops, and others give buses a lane of their own. Even so, patience remains a rider's best friend.

全文中譯

你是否曾等了二十分鐘的公車，結果卻一次來了三班？交通專家把這種現象稱為「公車串車」，而它發生的頻率遠比乘客想像的高。

原因很簡單。若某一班車稍微誤點，下一站聚集的乘客就會變多，上車所花的時間也更久。那班車於是愈來愈遲，而後面那班車遇到的乘客較少，便慢慢追了上來。不久之後，兩班車就並排行駛了。

有些城市要求司機跳過特定站牌來對付這個問題，也有些城市給公車專用車道。即便如此，耐心仍然是乘客最好的朋友。

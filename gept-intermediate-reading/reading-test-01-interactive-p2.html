Passage 1
A New Way to Hire

Many companies have changed the way they hire. In the past, a single manager read all the applications alone and made the decision in a single afternoon. Today a whole 11 of people takes part in the process: a supervisor, a senior worker, and someone from the human resources 12. Each of them meets the candidate in a short 13 of about twenty minutes. Afterwards they sit down together, compare notes, and try to 14 what kind of worker the person will become. Supporters say the method is fairer, because one bad first impression can no longer 15 the outcome on its own.

Questions 11–15
11
Acolony
Bcrew
Cbait
Ddepth
✔ 正確答案：(B)　crew
解析a whole ____ of people 指「一整組工作人員 crew」。colony 殖民地、bait 誘餌、depth 深度皆不合。
12
Awilderness
Badministration
Cinnocence
Ddecoration
✔ 正確答案：(B)　administration
解析human resources ____ 指人資「部門 administration」。其餘三者與公司組織無關。
13
Aclay
Bsession
Ccue
Ddock
✔ 正確答案：(B)　session
解析about twenty minutes 表示一「節、場 session」的會談。dock 碼頭、cue 暗示、clay 黏土皆不合。
14
Aresist
Bpeep
Cinfer
Dassassinate
✔ 正確答案：(C)　infer
解析compare notes 後「推論 infer」對方會是什麼樣的員工。peep 偷瞥、assassinate 暗殺明顯不合。
15
Aengage
Bdecide
Cvanish
Dfile
✔ 正確答案：(B)　decide
解析cannot ____ the whole decision 指單一印象無法「決定 decide」全局。file 歸檔、engage 吸引、vanish 消失皆不通。
全文中譯許多公司改變了招募的方式。過去，單一位主管獨自看完所有申請，一個下午就做出決定。如今是一整組人共同參與這個過程：一位主管、一位資深員工，以及人資部門的人。他們每個人都在約二十分鐘的一節會談中與應徵者見面。之後他們坐下來一起比對筆記，試著推論這個人會成為什麼樣的工作者。支持者說這個方法比較公平，因為單一個不好的第一印象已無法獨自決定最後結果。
Passage 2
Why People Change Jobs Later in Life

It is no longer unusual to change one's 16 at the age of fifty. Some workers leave a quiet office to become a 17 who repairs machines all day; others use their savings to open a small shop and become a 18 in their own neighborhood. Money is not always the reason for the move. Many of them say they simply want work that feels 19 to their own values. Career advisers add that older workers bring years of experience that younger staff cannot match, so the change is often 20 to employers as well.

Questions 16–20
16
Aprofession
Bprosperity
Cfoundation
Dinference
✔ 正確答案：(A)　profession
解析change one's ____ at fifty 指換「職業 profession」。prosperity 繁榮、foundation 基礎皆不合；inference 是推論，是思考的結果，不能拿來指一個人的工作。
17
Aburglar
Bslave
Ctechnician
Dresearcher
✔ 正確答案：(C)　technician
解析repairs machines（修理機器）對應「技術人員 technician」。researcher 是研究人員，不修機器。
18
Aadviser
Bsupervisor
Cexecutive
Dshopkeeper
✔ 正確答案：(D)　shopkeeper
解析open a small shop 之後成為「店主 shopkeeper」。其他三者都不是開店的人。
19
Aagreeable
Bfrequent
Caccidental
Dtight
✔ 正確答案：(A)　agreeable
解析feels ____ to their own values 指與價值觀「相符、令人愉悅的 agreeable」。tight 緊的、frequent 頻繁的、accidental 意外的皆不通。
20
Aacceptable
Btremendous
Cliberal
Dconcrete
✔ 正確答案：(A)　acceptable
解析帶來經驗，因此對雇主也是「可接受的 acceptable」。tremendous 巨大的語氣過強，liberal、concrete 語意不合。
全文中譯五十歲換職業已經不再稀奇。有些工作者離開安靜的辦公室，去當整天修理機器的技術人員；有些人用積蓄開一間小店，在自己住的社區裡當起商店老闆。金錢並不總是轉職的原因。他們許多人說，自己只是想要一份與自身價值觀相符的工作。生涯顧問補充，年長工作者帶來年輕員工難以匹敵的多年經驗，所以這種轉變對雇主來說通常也是可以接受的。

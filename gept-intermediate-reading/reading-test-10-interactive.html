Your great-grandmother came south alone at seventeen and worked as a maid for a family in Tainan. She married at twenty-four and had six children; two of them died as infants. The old photograph on your grandmother's wall was taken the week the youngest was born.

ARTICLE
Waiting at the School Gate

In many towns the person waiting at the school gate is not a parent but a grandparent. As more couples take jobs in distant cities, children are left in the care of the older generation. Officials in one county say that almost one child in five now lives this way.

The arrangement has clear benefits. Grandparents pass on dialect, cooking and family stories that a busy household often loses.

全文中譯

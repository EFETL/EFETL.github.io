詞彙 Vocabulary

請由四個選項中，選出一個最適合句意的字或詞。
1The charity concert managed to enough money to build a new shelter for homeless people.
Aspend
Bborrow
Craise
Dwaste
✔ 正確答案：(C)　raise
中譯這場慈善音樂會成功募得足夠的錢，為無家可歸的人蓋一座新收容所。
解析raise money 為固定用法，指「募款、籌錢」。spend 花費、borrow 借入、waste 浪費皆不合善款募集的語意。
單字raise 募集、籌集、charity 慈善、shelter 收容所、庇護所
2Everyone in the neighborhood was to help clean up the park after the big storm.
Areluctant
Bwilling
Ccurious
Dabsent
✔ 正確答案：(B)　willing
中譯大風暴過後，社區裡的每個人都樂意幫忙清理公園。
解析be willing to 表「願意、樂意」做某事，符合鄰里互助情境。reluctant 不情願的、curious 好奇的、absent 缺席的皆不合。
單字willing 願意的、樂意的、neighborhood 社區、鄰里、reluctant 不情願的
3The city government decided to the old bus routes so that more people could reach the new hospital.
Acancel
Bhide
Cignore
Dexpand
✔ 正確答案：(D)　expand
中譯市政府決定擴充舊的公車路線，讓更多人能抵達新醫院。
解析so that more people could reach（讓更多人能抵達）暗示路線要「擴充 expand」。cancel 取消、hide 隱藏、ignore 忽略皆與目的相反。
單字expand 擴充、擴展、route 路線、government 政府
4As a member of the community center, Mrs. Wang teaches free painting classes every weekend.
Atemporary
Bselfish
Cdevoted
Dcareless
✔ 正確答案：(C)　devoted
中譯身為社區中心一位盡心奉獻的成員，王太太每個週末都義務教繪畫課。
解析teaches free classes（義務授課）顯示其「盡心奉獻的 devoted」。temporary 暫時的、selfish 自私的、careless 粗心的皆不合。
單字devoted 盡心奉獻的、community 社區、selfish 自私的
5The two villages have long enjoyed a friendly and often share their harvests with each other.
Aconflict
Brelationship
Cargument
Ddistance
✔ 正確答案：(B)　relationship
中譯這兩個村莊長久以來維持友好的關係，經常彼此分享收成。
解析friendly 與 share their harvests（分享收成）指良好「關係 relationship」。conflict 衝突、argument 爭吵、distance 距離皆與友好不符。
單字relationship 關係、harvest 收成、收穫、conflict 衝突
6Public transportation helps the number of cars on the road, which reduces air pollution.
Acut
Binvite
Cincrease
Dcollect
✔ 正確答案：(A)　cut
中譯大眾運輸有助於減少路上的車輛數量，進而降低空氣污染。
解析which reduces air pollution（進而降低空污）顯示要「減少 cut」車輛。increase 增加、invite 邀請、collect 收集皆不合因果。
單字cut 減少、削減、transportation 運輸、交通、pollution 污染
7The older residents and the young students got along well despite the between their generations.
Agap
Blink
Chabit
Dsupport
✔ 正確答案：(A)　gap
中譯儘管世代之間存在差距，年長的居民和年輕學生仍相處融洽。
解析despite（儘管）與 between their generations 指世代「差距 gap」。link 連結、habit 習慣、support 支持皆不符轉折語意。
單字gap 差距、隔閡、generation 世代、resident 居民
8Volunteers are needed to meals to elderly people who live alone in our town.
Aborrow
Bdeliver
Cwaste
Drefuse
✔ 正確答案：(B)　deliver
中譯我們鎮上需要志工把餐點送給獨居的長者。
解析meals to elderly people（把餐點送給長者）搭配「遞送 deliver」。borrow 借、refuse 拒絕、waste 浪費皆不合送餐服務。
單字deliver 遞送、傳送、elderly 年長的、volunteer 志工
9The mayor gave a speech to citizens to take part in the recycling program.
Aprevent
Bforbid
Cencourage
Dwarn
✔ 正確答案：(C)　encourage
中譯市長發表演說，鼓勵市民參與資源回收計畫。
解析to take part in（參與）計畫是正面號召，故用「鼓勵 encourage」。prevent 阻止、forbid 禁止、warn 警告皆語意相反。
單字encourage 鼓勵、citizen 市民、公民、recycle 回收
10Living in a big city can be exciting, but the high cost of living is a serious for many families.
Ahobby
Breward
Cburden
Dtalent
✔ 正確答案：(C)　burden
中譯住在大城市可能很刺激，但高昂的生活開銷對許多家庭是沉重的負擔。
解析but 與 high cost（高開銷）顯示是「負擔 burden」。reward 獎賞、hobby 嗜好、talent 才能皆與負面對比不合。
單字burden 負擔、cost of living 生活開銷、serious 嚴重的

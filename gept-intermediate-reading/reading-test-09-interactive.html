ARTICLE
The Loop Between Worry and Sleep

Sleep is the first thing a busy mind gives up and the last thing it can afford to lose. In one study, healthy adults who slept five hours a night for a week became slower at simple tasks and much quicker to anger.

The link works in both directions. Worry keeps people awake, and a short night then makes every problem look bigger the next morning. Researchers call this a loop rather than a chain.

Breaking the loop rarely requires medicine. Fixed wake-up times, darker rooms and no screens after ten helped most volunteers within two weeks. A short message of sympathy from a friend, the study noted, helped almost as much.

全文中譯

睡眠是忙碌的頭腦最先犧牲的東西，卻也是最不能失去的東西。在一項研究中，連續一週每晚只睡五小時的健康成人，做簡單工作變慢，也更容易發脾氣。

這個關聯是雙向的。煩惱使人睡不著，而睡得太短又讓隔天早上每個問題看起來更大。研究人員稱之為一個迴圈，而不是一條鏈子。

要打破這個迴圈，很少需要藥物。固定的起床時間、更暗的房間，以及十點後不看螢幕，讓多數受試者在兩週內獲得改善。研究也指出，朋友一則簡短的關懷訊息，效果幾乎不相上下。

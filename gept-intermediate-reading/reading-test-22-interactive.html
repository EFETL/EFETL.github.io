ARTICLE
The Cost of Paying Later

Shops now offer a tempting button: buy today, pay in three parts, no interest. For a washing machine that breaks in winter, such a plan can be sensible. The danger appears when the button is used for small things.

A consumer group in Kaohsiung followed 300 shoppers for a year. Those who used pay-later services spent about 30 percent more than they had planned, mostly on clothes and shoes. Nearly a quarter forgot at least one payment date and had to pay a late fee.

The group does not ask for a ban. It suggests one rule: before pressing the button, ask whether you would still buy the item if the whole price had to leave your account today.

全文中譯

商店如今提供一個誘人的按鈕：今天買，分三期付，零利息。對於冬天壞掉的洗衣機來說，這樣的方案可能是明智的。危險出現在這個按鈕被用來買小東西的時候。

高雄一個消費者團體追蹤了 300 位購物者一整年。使用先買後付服務的人，花費比原先計畫多出約三成，主要花在衣服和鞋子上。將近四分之一的人至少忘記一次付款日，必須繳交遲繳費。

該團體並未要求禁止這項服務。他們建議一個原則：按下按鈕之前，先問問自己——如果今天就得從帳戶扣走全額，你還會買這件東西嗎？

ARTICLE
Sixty Years of Paint

The great red bridge across the bay has been painted for sixty years without a break. As soon as the workers reach one end, they go back and start again at the other.

The reason is rust. Salt air eats into bare steel, and a small brown spot can grow into a hole in a single wet winter. The paint they use is waterproof, and each layer must dry for two full days before the next one goes on.

Modern coatings last longer, so the team today is smaller than it was in 1970. Even so, the painters like to say that their work will never truly be finished.

全文中譯

橫跨海灣的那座紅色大橋，六十年來油漆工作從未中斷。工人一走到這一端，就折回另一端重新開始。

原因是鏽。含鹽的空氣會侵蝕裸露的鋼材，一個褐色小點在一個潮濕的冬天就能擴大成一個洞。他們使用的油漆具防水功能，每一層都必須乾燥整整兩天，才能塗上下一層。

現代的塗料撐得比較久，所以現在的團隊比一九七○年時小。即使如此，油漆工人還是喜歡說，他們的工作永遠不會真正完成。

Questions 21–24
21Why is the bridge painted all year round?
ATo make it look brighter for visitors
BTo test a new kind of paint
CTo hide the marks left by traffic
DTo protect the steel from rust
✔ 正確答案：(D)　To protect the steel from rust
中譯這座橋為何整年都在油漆？
解析第二段開頭 The reason is rust，接著說明鹽分空氣會侵蝕鋼材，可知目的是防鏽。文中並未提到美觀或測試新漆。
22What can a small brown spot become in one wet winter?
AA crack in the road
BA layer of new paint
CA hole in the metal
DA nest for birds
✔ 正確答案：(C)　A hole in the metal
中譯一個褐色小點在一個潮濕的冬天會變成什麼？
解析a small brown spot can grow into a hole in a single wet winter，會擴大成一個洞。
23What is true about the paint used on the bridge?
AIt is mixed on the bridge itself
BEach layer needs two days to dry
CIt must be put on in summer only
DIt is cheaper than older paint
✔ 正確答案：(B)　Each layer needs two days to dry
中譯關於橋上使用的油漆，何者正確？

24Why is the painting team smaller today?

BThe bridge is now shorter

✔ 正確答案：(A)　Modern coatings last longer

解析第三段 Modern coatings last longer, so the team today is smaller。文中未提機器取代人力或應徵人數不足。

EMAIL
Before We Place the Order

Dear Mr. Chang,

Thank you for the price list you sent on Monday. Before we order, there are three things we need to check.

First, are the copper tubes sold in three-meter lengths only? Our workshop door is just 2.4 meters wide.

Second, the list gives one price for steel wire and another for aluminium wire, but the sizes are missing. Could you let us know what they are?

Third, you offer a ten percent discount on orders over NT$50,000. Does that include delivery, or is it charged separately?

We hope to place the order on Friday, so an early reply would help us a great deal.

Best regards,
Lin Wei-chen

全文中譯

張先生您好：

謝謝您週一寄來的價目表。在我們下單之前，有三點需要釐清。

第一，銅管是否只有三公尺一支的規格？我們工作室的門只有 2.4 公尺寬。

第二，表上分別列出鋼線與鋁線的價格，卻沒有標示尺寸。可以請您告訴我們嗎？

第三，貴公司對五萬元以上的訂單提供九折優惠。這個優惠含運費，還是運費另計？

我們希望在星期五下單，若能盡早回覆，將對我們幫助很大。

謹此
林蔚辰

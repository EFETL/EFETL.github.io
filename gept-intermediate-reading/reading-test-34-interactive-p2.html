Passage 1
Why Old Styles Come Back

Look at a photo of your parents as teenagers, and you may notice clothes that seem strangely 11 today. Fashion moves in circles. A style that once looked old-fashioned returns about twenty years later, usually with a small change in shape or color. Designers do this 12 purpose, because familiar shapes feel safe while new details feel fresh. Shoppers play a part too. Many of us keep a favorite jacket for years, so old and new styles are always 13 together on the same street. If you are patient 14 , the coat you refuse to throw away may become 15 once again.

✔ 正確答案：(C)　fashionable
解析全文說舊款式會回歸，故留著的大衣可能再次變得「時髦的 fashionable」。
全文中譯看看你父母青少年時期的照片，你可能會注意到有些衣服在今天顯得莫名地眼熟。時尚是繞圈子前進的。曾經看起來過時的風格，大約二十年後就會回歸，通常在版型或顏色上有些微改變。設計師是刻意這麼做的，因為熟悉的版型讓人安心，而新的細節則帶來新鮮感。消費者也扮演了一角。我們許多人會把心愛的外套留上好幾年，於是新舊風格總是在同一條街上交錯出現。只要你夠有耐心，那件你捨不得丟的大衣，可能會再次變得時髦。
Passage 2
Making Clothes Last

A shirt that falls apart after ten washes is not really 16 , even if it costs very little. Simple habits can double the life of everything in your closet. Wash in cold water, since heat slowly 17 the shape of cotton. Turn dark jeans inside out to protect the color, and dry them in the shade 18 of under strong sunlight. When a button falls off, sew it back the same evening, because a small hole left alone soon 19 into a large one. Learning to repair your own clothes takes an hour, yet it saves money and 20 for years.

20
Aresources
Bsilence
Cweight
Ddistance
✔ 正確答案：(A)　resources
解析與 money 並列的應是同樣被省下的「資源 resources」，因為少買衣服就少消耗原料。silence 寂靜、weight 重量、distance 距離皆不合。
全文中譯一件洗十次就壞掉的襯衫，即使售價很低，其實也稱不上便宜。簡單的習慣能讓衣櫃裡每一件衣服的壽命加倍。用冷水洗，因為熱水會慢慢破壞棉布的版型。深色牛仔褲要翻面洗以保護顏色，並在陰涼處晾乾，而不是曝曬在強烈的陽光下。鈕扣掉了就當天晚上縫回去，因為放著不管的小破洞很快會變成大洞。學會自己修補衣服只需要一小時，卻能為往後數年省下金錢與資源。

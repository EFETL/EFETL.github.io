ARTICLE
Learning a Craft from a Screen

Ten years ago, anyone who wanted to learn woodworking had to find a teacher. Today a beginner can watch a free video and cut a first board the same afternoon. Craft teachers admit that this has brought many new people into the workshop.

Still, they warn that a screen cannot correct your hands. A video never notices that you are holding the chisel at a dangerous angle, and it never smells the glue burning. One studio owner in Hsinchu now asks her students to watch the videos at home and come to class only to practice. Her students, she says, learn twice as fast — but only because someone is standing beside them.

全文中譯

十年前，想學木工的人都得找個老師。今天，初學者看一支免費影片，當天下午就能鋸下第一塊木板。工藝老師們承認，這確實把許多新人帶進了工作坊。

不過他們也提醒，螢幕沒辦法糾正你的雙手。影片不會注意到你握鑿刀的角度很危險，也聞不到膠燒焦的味道。新竹一位工作室主人現在要求學生在家先看完影片，來上課只做實作練習。她說，她的學生學得快上一倍——但那只是因為有人就站在他們旁邊。

A Special Order

Dear Mr. Kuo,

I saw your walnut cutting boards at the Dadaocheng market last Sunday, and I would like to order one as a wedding gift for my brother. Could you carve the date and the couple's two names on the back?

The wedding is on October 25, so I would need the board by the 18th at the latest. Please tell me whether that is possible and how much extra the carving costs. If walnut is sold out, cherry wood would be fine too.

Best wishes,
Lin Chia-hao

全文中譯

郭先生您好：

上週日我在大稻埕市集看到您的胡桃木砧板，想訂購一塊當作送給我哥哥的結婚禮物。可以請您在背面刻上日期和新人兩人的名字嗎？

婚禮在十月二十五日，所以我最晚需要在十八日拿到砧板。請告訴我這樣是否可行，以及刻字要額外收多少費用。如果胡桃木賣完了，櫻桃木也可以。

祝好，
林家豪

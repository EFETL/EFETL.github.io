ARTICLE
Learning to Move Again

After a stroke, many patients are told to rest completely. Modern doctors disagree. Gentle movement in the first weeks helps the brain build new paths to the muscles it can no longer reach.

At one hospital in Taichung, patients play simple ball games instead of lying in bed. Nurses report that those who join the games walk without help about three weeks earlier than those who do not.

The programme is not about sport. Nevertheless, its leader is a former athlete, and she says the rules are the same: start small, repeat often, and never compare yourself with the person beside you.

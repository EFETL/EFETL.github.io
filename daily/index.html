烹調動詞 Cooking verbs

會了這幾個動詞，就能把上面任何一道菜說出口。

中文EnglishKK 音標例句 Example
炒stir-fry/ˈstɝˌfraɪ/My mom stir-fries water spinach with garlic.
汆燙blanch/blæntʃ/Blanch the broccoli for two minutes.
滷／燉braise/brez/We braise the napa cabbage with dried shrimp.
蒸steam/stim/Steam the pumpkin until it turns soft.
煮boil/bɔɪl/Boil the winter melon with pork ribs.
煎pan-fry/ˈpænˌfraɪ/Pan-fry the eggs with basil.
炸deep-fry/ˈdipˌfraɪ/Never deep-fry leafy greens.
涼拌toss（cold）/tɔs/Toss the cucumber with sesame oil.
醃marinate/ˈmærəˌnet/Marinate the radish in salt first.
切絲shred/ʃrɛd/Shred the carrot into thin strips.
切片slice/slaɪs/Slice the bamboo shoots before serving.
削皮peel/pil/Peel the taro with gloves on.
調味season/ˈsizn̩/Season it with a little soy sauce.

教學提醒：台灣的青菜幾乎都用 stir-fry，不要用 cook 帶過。整盤菜的量詞是 a plate of，湯是 a bowl of，例如 a plate of stir-fried cabbage、a bowl of bitter melon soup。

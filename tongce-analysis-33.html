▶B-7Given that transport accounts for a large share of a city's carbon emissions, switching fuels can make a real difference.

🔍 句構分析①Given that transport accounts for a large share of a city's carbon emissions副詞子句given that＝「由於」；account for「占（比例）」
②switching fuelsS 主詞動名詞片語作主詞，視為單數
③can make / a real differenceV/Omake a difference「帶來改變」
文法重點　account for「占（比例）」；make a difference 為固定用法（不可寫 make a different）。

▶B-8The government hopes that clean, cheap, and safe public transport will encourage more people to leave their cars at home.

▶B-14As a result, fewer traffic jams and lower fuel bills could benefit the whole community.

🔍 句構分析①As a result修飾語轉折語
②fewer traffic jams and lower fuel billsS 主詞兩個比較級修飾的名詞並列作主詞

文法重點　fewer + 可數複數（traffic jams）；benefit 當及物動詞意為「使…受益」，後面直接接受詞。

一、單字填空（15 題，每題 2 分，共 30 分）

基隆高中 113上期中1#1
It is hard to stay ________ when discussing personal matters, but try not to let your own wants and needs cloud your judgement.
答案：D objective 客觀的　L2 核心字。討論私事時要保持客觀，別讓私欲蒙蔽判斷。❌ notable 顯著的／subsequent 隨後的／political 政治的。
基隆高中 113上期中1#2
Rescue workers ________ in their efforts to locate the missing children and finally found them trapped under a fallen tree.
答案：B persevered 堅忍不拔　L1〈If—〉核心概念。救難人員不放棄，最後找到孩子。❌ indulge 沉溺／distort 扭曲／advise 建議。
小港高中 113上期中1#3
The fog was so ________ that we could hardly see a few feet ahead.
答案：A dense 濃密的　L3 densely forested 的形容詞原形。霧濃到看不見前方幾呎。
基隆高中 113上期中1#4
The figures are ________ and don’t give an accurate picture of the company’s success this year.
答案：D misleading 誤導的　L2 核心字（mislead 的形容詞）。數字會誤導，無法反映真實情況。❌ valid 有效的／credible 可信的，語意相反。
基隆高中 112上期中2#5
Although they are sisters, Jamie ________s from Tina in personality and appearance.
答案：D differ from 與…不同　L3 核心字。雖是姊妹，個性外貌卻不同。
基隆高中 113上期中1#6
Rather than just ignore the problem, Ben decided to ________ his fear of public speaking and do something about it.
答案：C confront 正視、面對　L1 面對困境的主題字。❌ encounter 偶然遇到（非主動面對）／retain 保留／possess 擁有。
基隆高中 113上期中1#7
Although Neil and Ashe didn’t succeed in their previous ________ to reach the top of the mountain, they made it in the end.
答案：D attempt 嘗試、企圖　L2 核心字（attempts to influence voters）。previous attempts 先前的嘗試。
基隆高中 112上期中2#8
The child demonstrated an ________ talent for music and will be a world-famous pianist one day.
答案：C exceptional 絕佳的、異常的　L3 核心字（exceptional strategic location）。
小港高中 113上期中1#9
Her design style blends ________ elements with traditional architecture.
答案：D contemporary 當代的　L1 核心字，「一字兩義」：同時代的人／當代的，學測常考。
基隆高中 113上期中1#10
Newt’s dreams of becoming an engineer were ________ when a car accident left him blind.
答案：C shatter 粉碎、使破滅　L2 核心字（confidence was shattered）。車禍讓他失明，工程師夢破滅。
基隆高中 112上期中2#11
The ________ Edinburgh Castle sits atop a hill at one end of the city’s main street.
答案：A majestic 雄偉的、壯麗的　L3 核心字（majestic mountains）。
金門高中 113上期中2#12
The government can’t find a good reason to ________ its high expenses on weapons, especially when the number of people living in poverty is so high.
答案：C justify 為…辯解、證明正當　L1 核心字（justifying his decision）。
基隆高中 113上期中2#13
When the pirates attacked the merchant ship, they quickly ordered the captain, passengers, and the ________ to gather in one place and hand over all their valuable possessions.
答案：A crew 全體船員　L3 核心字（a crew of Portuguese sailors）。集合名詞，與 captain、passengers 並列。
新北高中 113上期中1#14
People attribute John’s success to ________ and perseverance. He always works hard and never gives up.
答案：C diligence 勤勉　L2 課文結語 with due diligence（謹慎盡責）。與 perseverance 並列。
明倫高中 上學期期中2#15
Linguistic features between two languages differ ________ more than those between two dialects.
答案：B considerably 相當地、大幅地　L3 核心副詞（differ considerably）。

The poem begins with advice regarding how to behave righteously in the face of unrighteousness. When people are  26  toward you, remain calm and steady. Have faith  27  yourself when others blame or doubt you, but be receptive to constructive criticism. No less important  28 , for without it, the fruits of your labor may be lost. (B) L3 Formosa　題源：新店高中 113 上期中1 Cloze (B) 26–30

“Formosa” is commonly known as an unofficial name for Taiwan. However, many people don’t know that there is another Formosa  31  on the banks of the Paraguay River in Argentina. Let’s take a detailed look at these two namesakes and investigate the aspects  32  which they are similar and those in which they differ.

Interestingly, it appears that both Formosas were unintentionally discovered by European explorers in the sixteenth century. It is believed that a crew of Portuguese sailors first came across Taiwan and named it “Ilha Formosa,” which means “beautiful island.”  33 , Formosa was the European name given to an area of land discovered in South America by Spanish explorers around the same time. These explorers considered the area they had found to possess  34  exceptional beauty that they decided to live there.

 35  their shared name and historical similarities, there is another interesting geographical phenomenon that connects these two locations. If someone in northern Taiwan dug a hole straight down into the Earth, penetrated its core, and then continued digging to the surface on the other side, he or she would end up in Formosa, Argentina! It is amazing that these two bits of land are exactly antipodal!

新店高中 113上期中1#31
第 31 格
答案：C located　＝(which is) located，過去分詞片語後位修飾 another Formosa。本句已有動詞 is，不能再用 is located。
新店高中 113上期中1#32
第 32 格
答案：A in　the aspects in which they are similar ＝ they are similar in the aspects。介系詞＋關代，由 in the aspects 還原。
新店高中 113上期中1#33
第 33 格
答案：B Likewise 同樣地　前句講葡萄牙人命名台灣，本句講西班牙人同樣以 Formosa 命名，屬同質並列。❌ However 表轉折，不合。
新店高中 113上期中1#34
第 34 格
答案：C such　such + (a/an) + adj. + N + that…；此處 beauty 為不可數名詞 → such exceptional beauty that…。若用 so 須接形容詞（so beautiful that）。
新店高中 113上期中1#35
第 35 格
答案：A Aside from 除了…之外（還有）　後句 there is another interesting fact，為累加語氣。❌ Despite 讓步／Due to 原因，皆不合。

四、閱讀測驗（8 題，每題 2 分，共 16 分）

(A) L2 Battling Fake News　短文取自小港高中 113 上期中1 綜合測驗原文

Fake news comes in various forms, with two being the most common. One type arises from sloppy journalism, where information is published without proper fact-checking. The second type involves deliberately false information intended to achieve social or political outcomes, such as influencing votes or public opinion.

Two examples highlight the harm fake news can cause. In 1998, a flawed study linked the MMR vaccine to autism, causing a drop in vaccination rates. However, larger studies revealed that the original findings were inaccurate. Despite this, some parents continue to refuse vaccinations for their children, fearing that they might do more harm than good. More than two decades have now passed since the original study was published, but its effects can still be observed worldwide. The second example, from 2018 in Taiwan, involved a retailer falsely claiming an upcoming increase in the price of toilet paper. Two mainstream media outlets reported this without fact-checking, and others soon followed suit, amplifying the misinformation. This led to widespread panic buying among Taiwanese people, many of whom feared running out of this daily necessity.

With such significant events in mind, we are reminded that we must resist fake news. Whenever we come across new information, it is important to assess its validity lest we be tricked into accepting fake news as the truth. One notable feature of authentic news is the inclusion of the writer’s name, enabling readers to verify credibility. Another characteristic of real news is the citing of multiple primary sources, demonstrating a high level of authenticity. Where the article is published, such as by respected platforms like the BBC or CNN, indicates reliability. Lastly, factual reports use objective writing that does not appeal to readers’ emotions or use deliberately provocative language. These features help us avoid falling for fake news and see the world accurately.

答案：B　全文＝假新聞的種類（第1段）→ 危害實例（第2段）→ 辨識方法（第3段），主旨為「什麼是假新聞、如何辨識」。

小港高中 113上期中1#38

答案：C　a retailer falsely claiming an upcoming increase… reported this without fact-checking → panic buying。

(B) L1 延伸：Rudyard Kipling《叢林奇譚》　題源：基隆高中 113 上期中1 閱讀測驗 44–47（原題原答）

The Jungle Book, written by Rudyard Kipling in 1894, is one of his most famous works. It is a collection of stories set in the jungles of India. The most well-known stories in the book are about a boy named Mowgli, who is raised by wolves. The Jungle Book is not just one story but a group of stories, each with its own lessons and themes. However, the stories about Mowgli are the most popular.

Mowgli is a young boy who is found in the jungle by a wolf pack when he is just a baby. The wolves decide to raise him as one of their own. Mowgli grows up learning the ways of the jungle with the help of his animal friends. One of his best friends is Baloo, a big, gentle bear who teaches him important lessons about life. Another important friend is Bagheera, a wise black panther who is always looking out for Mowgli’s safety.

The main conflict in the stories comes from Shere Khan, a fierce tiger who wants to kill Mowgli. Shere Khan believes that Mowgli does not belong in the jungle because he is human. Throughout the book, Mowgli has to deal with the threat of Shere Khan while trying to find his place in the jungle.

One of the twists in the story happens when Mowgli is forced to leave the jungle and live in a human village. This is a big change for Mowgli, as he has grown up thinking of himself as part of the jungle. However, he struggles to fit in with the humans and eventually returns to the jungle to face Shere Khan.

In the end, Mowgli defeats Shere Khan and proves that he belongs in the jungle. The Jungle Book is a story about friendship, bravery, and finding where you truly belong. It has been loved by readers for many years and has been made into movies and shows.

五、英翻中（3 題，每題 4 分，共 12 分）

基隆高中 113上期中1#44
And if people despise you or spread lies about you, be truthful and refrain from returning the hatred.
參考答案：如果別人輕視你，或散播關於你的謊言，要保持誠實，並且克制自己不以仇恨回報。
關鍵：despise 輕視；spread lies 散播謊言；refrain from + V-ing 克制不做；return the hatred 以仇恨回報。
小港高中 113上期中1#45
Whenever we come across new information, it is important to assess its validity lest we be tricked into accepting fake news as the truth.

關鍵：come across 偶然遇到；assess its validity 評估可信度；lest + S + (should) V 以免（後接原形動詞）；be tricked into V-ing 被誘騙去做。
新店高中 113上期中1#46
If someone in northern Taiwan dug a hole straight down into the Earth, penetrated its core, and then continued digging to the surface on the other side, he or she would end up in Formosa, Argentina!

六、中翻英（4 題，每題 3 分，共 12 分）

小港高中 113上期中1#47
我們應該經常批判性地思考，以免可能會把謠言誤認為是可靠訊息。
（………… for fear that …………）
參考答案：We should often think critically for fear that we might mistake rumors for reliable information.
關鍵：think critically 批判性思考；for fear that + S + might V 以免；mistake A for B 把 A 誤認為 B。
台南二中 113上期中1#48
在相信被報導的新聞之前，我們應該理性地、客觀地評估新聞，以免被誤導。
（Prior ………… for fear that …………）
參考答案：Prior to believing the news reported, we should evaluate it rationally and objectively for fear that we should be misled.
關鍵：prior to + N/V-ing 在…之前（L2 片語）；evaluate 評估；rationally / objectively 理性地／客觀地。
明倫高中 113上期中1#49
為了個人好處而扭曲事實並犧牲了大眾利益的人，不管是誰，都應該被嚴厲譴責。
（限以 Whoever 開頭）
參考答案：Whoever distorts the facts for personal gain at the expense of the public interest should be severely condemned.
關鍵：Whoever 複合關代引導名詞子句當主詞（視為單數 → distorts）；distort the facts 扭曲事實；at the expense of 犧牲…。
明倫高中 113上期中1#50
每當我們在手機裡收到可疑簡訊時，我們最好評估其可信度，以免錯把假訊息誤認為是可靠消息。
（限用 lest）
參考答案：Whenever we receive suspicious text messages on our cellphones, we had better evaluate their credibility lest we mistake fake messages for reliable information.
關鍵：Whenever 每當；had better + 原形；lest + S + (should) + 原形動詞；credibility 可信度（L2 補充字）。
